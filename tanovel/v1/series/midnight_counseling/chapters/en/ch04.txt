That night.
The woman who had been a guest cried for a long time before falling asleep on the table, exhausted.
Eun-jo brought an old blanket and carefully covered the sleeping woman's shoulders.

"Name is... Yoo Min-seo."

Ji-hyuk said, picking up the ID card the woman had dropped.
Manager of the Marketing Team at a large corporation.
It was a flashy business card that some would envy, but the reality hidden behind it was miserable.
Just looking at the worn corners of the card showed how fiercely she had been holding on.

"Detective."

Eun-jo called out in a low voice.
Her gaze lingered on the sleeping Min-seo's wet eyelashes.

"When this person goes to work tomorrow, she has to fight a war again. You can't fight for her, Detective... but can't you at least be a shield so the hand holding the knife doesn't shake?"

"Police intervention in civil matters is principally not allowed."

Ji-hyuk grumbled.
But contrary to his words, his hand was already taking a notebook and pen out of his pocket.

"Prohibition of workplace harassment, and the Unfair Competition Prevention Act regarding idea theft. Evidence collection is key. Things like recordings, emails, and messenger records. It's hard with just the victim's statement."

The sound of the pen scratching filled the quiet counseling center.
He wrote down specific tips for collecting evidence and the contact information of a free legal counseling center he knew in tiny letters on a memo pad.
And he slipped it into the inner pocket of Min-seo's open bag.

"This is just... on the level of civic service. Let's say it's a tip from a neighborhood older brother, not a detective."

Eun-jo smirked.

"Mister, you're more 'tsundere' than I thought."

"Is that a compliment?"

"Well. It means it's not bad."

Ji-hyuk cleared his throat and looked away.
Eun-jo's smile kept reminding him of someone from 10 years ago.

***

The next morning.
Yoo Min-seo opened her eyes on the old leather sofa of the counseling shop.
Morning sunlight was seeping through the window cracks.
Her head hurt like it was going to split, but she felt strangely refreshed.
It felt like a clogged drain had been unclogged, like the lump pressing on her throat had disappeared.

"Are you awake?"

Eun-jo handed her a cup of warm tea.
This time it was clear rosemary tea.
A subtle herb scent lingered at the tip of her nose.

"Thank you... I, yesterday I made such a scene..."

Min-seo's voice flowed out, albeit quietly.
It wasn't completely cleared, but the unpleasant wheezing sound was gone.

"A scene? You vomited out rotten blood. It's a natural process to stop hurting. Ah, and take this."

Eun-jo handed her the note Ji-hyuk left and her own Tarot card <Queen of Swords> together.

"This note is a gift left by that fierce-looking detective yesterday, and this card is a charm I'm giving you."

Min-seo read the note.
Densely written legal clauses and advice.
Her nose stung at the sincerity felt in those rough letters.

"The Queen of Swords is not swayed by emotions. she only wins with facts. When you go to work today, the team leader will surely yell and intimidate you again. He'll try gaslighting."

Eun-jo looked straight into Min-seo's eyes and said.

"Don't be scared then. Because now there is a sword in your hand. Believe in that sword."

Min-seo gripped the note and the card tightly.
Strength entered her fingertips.
She didn't want to run away anymore.

***

2 PM, Marketing Team Conference Room.
The team leader's shouting echoed outside the conference room to the office.

"Hey Yoo Min-seo! Aren't you going to snap out of it? What is this trash of a proposal? You left out the key points I told you to say! You don't want to work these days, do you? You must differ full of yourself?"

Normally, Min-seo would have bowed her head deep and repeated that she was sorry like a parrot.
Her heart would have been beating like it would burst, and her voice would have crawled away.

But today was different.
Inside her pocket lay the cold <Queen of Swords> card.
As she touched the sharp edge of the card with her fingertips, she seemed to hear Eun-jo's voice.
*Don't swallow it. Spit it out.*

Min-seo slowly raised her head.
The team leader's eyes went wide. *Dare to make eye contact?*

"Team Leader."

Min-seo's voice was small but clear. There was no trembling.

"I recorded what you just said."

"......What? What did you just say?"

The team leader doubted his ears. It was a word that couldn't come out of Yoo Min-seo's mouth.

"I also secured the history of you changing the 'Eco-friendly Package' proposal I submitted last month to your name and uploading it. I've written an email cc'ing the Director and the Audit Team."

Silence flowed in the conference room.
The team leader's face turned pale in an instant.

"You... Are you crazy? Do you know who I am..."

"And the verbal abuse you are doing now clearly falls under workplace harassment. I will file a report with the Labor Office."

Min-seo took out the resignation letter she had prepared from her bag and placed it on the table in front of the team leader with a *thud*.

"Because this company is not everything in my life. I decided to protect myself."

Min-seo got up from her seat without lingering attachment.
She opened the conference room door and left without looking back.
Behind her back, she heard the team leader screaming, but that sound was now nothing more than barking that couldn't deal any blow to Min-seo.
Rather, it felt refreshing.

As she pushed the revolving door and came out of the building, dazzling afternoon sunlight was pouring down.
Min-seo took a deep breath.
It was city air mixed with exhaust fumes, but today it felt exceptionally sweet and cool.
For the first time in a year, breath came into the depths of her chest.

[Thank you, teacher. And to the detective too. I can breathe now. I can hear my voice.]

That night.
Eun-jo smiled as she looked at the long text message Min-seo sent.
In the corner of the counseling shop, Ji-hyuk was sitting like it was his reserved seat, as always.

"Did it get resolved?"

"Yes. She said she gave him a refreshing blow? Thanks to the legal clauses the detective wrote down, the team leader couldn't say anything."

"Hmph. It wasn't much. That much is basic common sense."

Ji-hyuk lifted his teacup pretending to be indifferent, but a faint smile he couldn't hide hung on his lips.
He thought as he looked at his face reflected in the teacup.
*I can help someone too. Not with a gun, but with a small piece of memo paper.*

Midnight Counseling.
Today, it restored one person's lost voice.
That alone was enough reason for this suspicious shop to exist.

(End of Episode 4)
