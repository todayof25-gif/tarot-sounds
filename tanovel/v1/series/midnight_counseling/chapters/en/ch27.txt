Seongbuk-dong Redevelopment District.
A place where old houses facing demolition were left neglected like a ghost village. In the darkness where even streetlights didn't work, only a two-story western-style house half-collapsed by fire 10 years ago stood like a monstrosity.
The place that was formerly 'Hope Counseling Center', and Eun-jo's home.

"It's here..."

Getting out of the car, Eun-jo said with a trembling voice looking at the old gate.
Blackened bricks, yard overgrown with weeds. It was exactly the scenery stopped on that day 10 years ago.
A fishy smell of oil was floating faintly in the air.

"Be careful. The bastard is inside."

Ji-hyuk took out his pistol and took the lead. He entered slowly, hiding Eun-jo behind his back.
Broken glass shards were stepped on the floor, crunching.
Then, a faint candlelight flickered in the second-floor window. The bastard is calling.

*Creeeak...*
The living room entered opening the front door was bizarre.
On the floor, something like a complex magic circle was drawn with red paint, and dozens of candles were lit in the center.
And in the middle of the living room, a mannequin shaped like sitting in a wheelchair was placed.
On the headless mannequin, a name tag written 'Father' was attached.

"Crazy bastard..."

Ji-hyuk swallowed a curse.
At that moment, clapping sounds were heard from the second-floor stairs.

*Clap, clap, clap.*

"Welcome. To my holy ritual."

It was Lee Jin-oh.
He was wearing a white patient uniform like that day 10 years ago. Half of his face was distorted with burn scars, but the other half was smiling brightly like a child.
In his hands were an oil can and a lighter.

"Jin-oh Oppa..."

Eun-jo stepped forward.

"Don't come! Stand there!"

Jin-oh lit the lighter and waved it.
Ji-hyuk stopped instinctively. Not only around the bastard, but thinner was sprinkled abundantly on the entire living room floor. A situation where the whole building would blow up with a single spark.

"Why did you do it? Why did you destroy Father... and our family?"

Eun-jo asked. Her voice didn't tremble. Rather, it was calm to the point of coldness.

"Destroy? I saved them! Your father was a drunkard. He bullied you and gave Mother a hard time. That's why I purified him. As Mother said!"

Jin-oh's eyes glistened with madness.

"And you, Eun-jo. You were having a hard time too. crying every night. I tried to erase all those memories... why did you run away? Why do you refuse my gift?"

"Gift...?"

"Yes! Because fire makes everything clean. Pain, sadness, all become ashes and disappear. Look. This stage I prepared for you."

Jin-oh spread his arms.
Behind him, a huge Tarot card picture was hanging.
A picture of a horned devil binding a man and a woman with chains.
**<The Devil>.**

"We are sinners. Prisoners trapped in dirty flesh and memory. Only fire can break this chain. Let's go together, Eun-jo. Don't run away this time."

Jin-oh poured the oil can over his head.
A nauseating smell vibrated. He intended to become a sacrifice himself and burn with Eun-jo.

"He's crazy. Completely lost it."

Ji-hyuk aimed the muzzle, but couldn't pull the trigger. If he shoots, there was a risk that the muzzle flash would ignite the oil vapor.
Jin-oh knew that too.

"Shoot! The moment you shoot, we all go to heaven together! Hahaha!"

Jin-oh laughed madly.
Then, Eun-jo blocked Ji-hyuk's front and stepped out.

"Move, Eun-jo! It's dangerous!"

"No. What that person wants isn't a bullet."

Eun-jo walked out slowly, looking straight at Jin-oh.
Oil sloshing under her feet soaked her shoes, but she didn't stop.

"Oppa. You are not a devil."

At Eun-jo's soft voice, Jin-oh's laughter stopped abruptly.

"What...?"

"You were just... a lonely child who wanted to be loved. By Mother, and by me."

"Shut up! I'm a savior! I was chosen!"

"No. You were just afraid. Afraid of being abandoned. Afraid of being locked in that cold room again. So you wanted to get attention even by setting a fire."

Eun-jo took out **<The Devil>** card from her pocket.
And placed **<The High Priestess>** card next to it.

"Look at this. The devil's chains are loose. Those bound can untie it and come out whenever they want. What binds you isn't fire. It's your own obsession."

"Stop it! I don't want to hear it! Aaargh!"

Jin-oh stepped back in pain. His most painful shame was caught. As justification collapsed, madness began to shake.
Taking that opportunity, Ji-hyuk sent a signal with his eyes.
*Now.*

Ji-hyuk threw away the gun and threw his body.
Kicking the slippery oil floor, he sprang up the stairs.

"No! Don't come!"

Jin-oh tried to throw the lighter to the floor.
But Ji-hyuk was faster.
Ji-hyuk's body tackle hit Jin-oh hard. The two tangled and broke the second-floor railing, falling to the first-floor floor.
*Crash—!*

"Ugh..."

Even in the moment of falling, Ji-hyuk embraced Jin-oh to reduce the impact. Of course, not for Jin-oh, but to take the lighter he was holding.
The lighter rolled on the floor and slid toward the oil puddle.
Just before it caught fire, Eun-jo threw her body and snatched the lighter.

"Haa... Haa..."

Ji-hyuk twisted Jin-oh's arm to subdue him. Jin-oh struggled, but couldn't beat the 'Emperor's' strength.
Everything is over.
No, now the real judgment remained.

Ji-hyuk took out handcuffs and cuffed Jin-oh's wrists.
*Clink.*
The cold metallic sound echoed in the ruined house.

"Lee Jin-oh. You are under arrest for arson causing death and attempted murder."

Ji-hyuk's voice was dry but heavy.
Jin-oh giggled with his face stuck to the floor.

"Think it's over? The embers... still remain..."

His eyes were still burning like unextinguished fire.

(End of Episode 27)
