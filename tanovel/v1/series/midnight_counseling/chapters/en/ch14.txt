"Welcome to the masquerade, huh..."

Ji-hyuk muttered low. The eyes of the hunting dog chasing the shadow sank coldly.
The man standing under the streetlamp light didn't even seem to think of running away.
Black hoodie, hat pressed deep. But Ji-hyuk knew intuitively.
*That guy isn't the 'Shadow'. He's just another puppet mimicking the Shadow.*

"Get him."

As soon as Ji-hyuk gave the short order, undercover detectives hiding at the corner of the alley stormed in.
The man raised both hands obediently.
It was too bland an arrest. But Ji-hyuk's heart was rather heavy.
Because the man grinned while being handcuffed.

"Give my regards... to Yu-na. Tell her my love will never change."

A familiar voice.
Ji-hyuk took off the man's hat.
The moment he saw the revealed face, Ji-hyuk had no choice but to let out a sigh.
He was Yu-na's manager, Chief Kim.

***

"Oppa... really did that?"

Inside the counseling shop, Yu-na shook her head with a face that said she couldn't believe it.
Thick tears dripped from her eyes.
Chief Kim was like family who had been with her for over 10 years since her rookie days.

"The motive was 'possessiveness'. He said he was anxious that you would drift away from him as you became famous. So he stalked and sent threatening letters to isolate you."

Ji-hyuk explained with a bitter expression.
In the interrogation room, Chief Kim repeated laughing like a madman and then crying.
He said 'a certain voice' coached him over the phone. On how to make Yu-na his forever.
It was the Shadow. The bastard was digging into people's weakest hearts and manipulating them.

"How... how could he do that. I trusted him so much."

Yu-na sobbed and collapsed.
It was the moment the mask called 'trust' that was supporting her shattered.
Eun-jo quietly approached and placed a new teacup in front of Yu-na.
The tea in the transparent glass cup had a subtle purple hue.

"It's 'Dawn's Comfort', a blend of lavender and chamomile. It will help calm your mind."

Yu-na lifted the teacup with trembling hands. Warmth was transmitted through her fingertips.
As she took a sip, fragrant flower scent filled her mouth, and her frozen heart seemed to melt little by little.

"Right now, your heart is like a shattered mirror, Yu-na. The person I trusted, the world I trusted, and even myself... everything is broken."

Eun-jo's voice was calm and warm.
She spread Tarot cards on the table.

"But a broken mirror can shine again. Would you like to pick a card?"

Yu-na hesitated, then turned over a card.
A picture of a large star shining in the night sky, and a woman pouring water from two jars under it.
**<The Star>.**

"It's The Star card. It means hope, healing, and inspiration. Like a star that rises after a storm passes, peace always comes after pain."

"Hope... do I have anything like that left?"

"Of course. Look at the woman in the picture. She is naked. It's her purest natural self, hiding nothing."

Eun-jo looked straight into Yu-na's eyes and said.

"Yu-na. It's time to take off the mask. The mask of the 'perfect actress' the public wants, the mask of a 'doll' the manager made. Throw all of that away, and live as the real Kim Yu-na."

"As the real... Kim Yu-na?"

"Yes. The ordinary you who gets hurt, sometimes makes mistakes, and cries your eyes out. Stars shine brightest not under artificial lights, but in pitch-black darkness."

Yu-na stared at the card. The woman in the card looked peaceful.
She slowly took off the sunglasses hiding her face.
Puffy eyes, face with smudged makeup.
But Ji-hyuk thought that appearance was much more beautiful than the glamorous actress on screen.

"I'm scared. That people will be disappointed."

"Some people might be disappointed. But there will definitely be people who love the real you. Like that star."

Eun-jo pointed to the card.
A faint smile spread on Yu-na's lips.
It wasn't acting, but a real smile.

***

A few days later, Yu-na held a press conference.
She revealed the manager's stalking and declared she would suspend activities for the time being.
Dressed in a plain shirt instead of a flashy dress, she raised her 'real voice' in front of the public for the first time.

[I am not perfect. I get scared easily, and I get hurt easily. But from now on, I won't hide, and I will stand up again on my own.]

Ji-hyuk smiled warmly watching the TV news.
She was no longer anyone's doll. She was a star emitting light by herself.

"It's a happy ending."

Ji-hyuk said, putting down his teacup.
But Eun-jo's expression was dark. She was staring at the darkness outside the window.
Because the words Chief Kim left to Ji-hyuk just before being arrested weighed on her mind.

['He said. The real thief is coming soon.']

"A thief..."

Ji-hyuk muttered.
It was then.
*Click, click.*
The sound of someone walking was heard in the quiet alley.
It was past 1 AM.
The counseling shop door opened quietly. It was a secret intrusion where even the bell didn't ring.
Ji-hyuk instinctively moved his hand to the gun at his waist.

"Who is it. We're closed."

The man who walked in from the darkness was wearing a fedora and holding an old pocket watch in one hand.
He took off his hat gentlemanly and placed it on his chest, bowing politely.
But the corners of his mouth were twisted bizarrely.

"Excuse me. Is this the counseling shop that finds lost things?"

Eun-jo jumped up from her seat. Her complexion turned pale.
The man opened his mouth again.

"I am a thief. But I didn't come to steal things."

The man closed the pocket watch with a *click*.
That sound was like a switch stopping Ji-hyuk's heart.

"I... came to steal memories. The very 'memory of fire' you want to forget."

Ji-hyuk's mind went blank.
10 years ago, the fire scene.
That scorching heat and the screams... he couldn't remember.
Like someone erased his head with an eraser, white.

(End of Episode 14)
