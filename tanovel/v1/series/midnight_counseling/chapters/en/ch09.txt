The night streets after the rain stopped were filled with wet asphalt and the smell of acrid cigarette smoke.
Ji-hyuk sat at an outdoor table at a convenience store, staring at an old manila envelope.

[10 Years Ago Seobu Market Fire Case - Witness Statement Report]

It was data he found in a dusty box deep in the police station archives.
A case already closed. Records of a disaster everyone had forgotten.
But Ji-hyuk was digging through those ashes again.

"Sword of Truth, huh..."

Ji-hyuk took out the **<Ace of Swords>** card from his inner pocket.
An image of a hand reaching out from the clouds, firmly grasping a shining sword.

*'This sword cuts away lies and illusions and lets you see only the truth.'*

Eun-jo's voice lingered in his ears.
He couldn't tell if seeing the truth would be a blessing or a curse.
But one thing was certain: he couldn't stop.
Because a hunting dog never lets go once it bites.

"Let's go, to the end."

Ji-hyuk stood up. The night breeze was cold, but the fire inside his chest didn't cool down.

***

*Jingle—.*

With the bell sounding midnight, the counseling shop door opened.
It was a female student in a school uniform.
With drooping shoulders, a bloodless face, and dark circles under her hollow eyes, she looked like she had stayed up for days.

"Is this... the place that listens to worries?"

Her voice was cracked like scraping metal.
Eun-jo offered a welcome tea with a warm smile. It was a herbal tea blended with chamomile and orange peel.
The girl took the teacup, hesitated, and then opened her mouth.

"Can you... turn back time?"

Eun-jo's hand stopped.

"Do you want to turn back time?"

"Yes. No, actually... I'm trapped."

The girl, Jo Su-a, bit her lip.

"I'm a repeat student, no, taking the exam for the third time. I've been living the same day for three years. The day before the CSAT, November 13th. When I open my eyes, it's always that morning. I go to the study room, organize my wrong answer notes, fall asleep anxiously... and it's the morning of November 13th again."

Time loop. It sounded like a fantasy story, but Su-a's expression was desperate.

"I thought it was a dream. But the workbook pages, even Mom's side dishes are exactly the same without a single mistake. I think it's been a hundred times already. I tried running away, but it was useless. When midnight comes, sleep pours down, and when I open my eyes, I'm back in front of the desk."

Su-a's tears fell into the teacup.

"I'm so sick of it now. I'm scared. I wonder if I can never go to tomorrow forever... Am I cursed?"

Eun-jo listened silently.
A prison of the mind created by the pressure and fear of repeated exam life.

"Su-a. Have you checked what date it is today?"

"Of course. My phone, calendar, everything says November 13th..."

Su-a stopped talking. The calendar on the wall pointed to 'November 14th'.

"Uh...?"

Su-a hurriedly turned on her phone. November 14th, 0:10 AM.
It was definitely tomorrow.

"How... did this happen? Just until a moment ago..."

"Time was flowing. Except for your heart, Su-a."

Eun-jo lit a scented candle.
The scent of Clary Sage mixed with subtle lavender and earthy notes spread.
It was a scent that calmed confusion and gave a sense of happiness.

"You were so desperate that you wanted to hold onto time. 'What if I fail?', 'I have to be more perfect'. That fear created the prison called today."

Eun-jo spread the Tarot cards.
Su-a picked a card with trembling hands.

**<Wheel of Fortune>.**

A card depicting a giant wheel and four sacred animals.
The wheel symbolizes the providence of the world that constantly turns.

"This card means 'change' and 'cycle'."

Eun-jo began reading in a low voice.

"The wheel shouldn't stop. It has to roll. If there's a time to go up, there's a time to go down too. You stopped the wheel itself because you were afraid of going down, Su-a."

"Is going down... failing?"

"No. It's moving forward."

Eun-jo held Su-a's hand.

"You might make a mistake tomorrow. But life doesn't end. The wheel turns again, and another opportunity comes. What's important is not staying stopped."

Su-a stared blankly at the card.
During the hundred November 13ths, she hadn't moved a single step forward. She had just been trembling in place.

"I'm scared... of tomorrow coming."

"Everyone is. I'm scared every night too. But tomorrow has to come. So we can eat delicious cake and see pretty flowers, right?"

At Eun-jo's playful wink, a faint smile spread across Su-a's face.

"Here, if you drink this tea, the magic will break. Say goodbye to the tiresome yesterday and welcome the real today."

Su-a took a sip of tea. Fragrant orange scent lingered in her mouth.
It was a freshness like waking up from a long nightmare.

"Thank you..."

Her face looked comfortable.

"I should go sleep. If I sleep well and wake up, it'll be a real new day, right?"

Su-a left the counseling shop with light steps. *Jingle*, the bell cheered her on.

Eun-jo cleared the empty teacup and looked out the window.
The rain had stopped, but the night sky was still hidden by clouds.

*Is Ji-hyuk... wandering somewhere right now too?*

Suddenly, Ji-hyuk's face came to mind.
Will the sword she gave him protect him? Or will it drag him into deeper darkness?
Eun-jo felt a corner of her heart go cold.
It felt like invisible shadows were seeping in through the cracks of the door.

But she shook her head with effort.
The wheel must turn. Whatever future it brings.

(End of Episode 9)
