"So... you mean we both had the same dream?"

Ji-hyuk's voice rang low in the quiet air of the counseling shop.
Eun-jo nodded slowly, fiddling with her teacup.
The vivid sensation felt in last night's dream. It felt like the acrid smell of gunpowder that pierced his nose and the scorching heat that seemed to burn his skin were still soaking into his collar.

"It's not a simple dream. It's Resonance. The memories of two people formed at the threshold of death 10 years ago connected a passage of the unconscious. Like radio frequencies coinciding by chance."

Eun-jo's explanation was unscientific, but he couldn't refute it.
The gun he fired in the dream. And the expression Eun-jo showed when that gunshot rang out. All of it was too vivid to be a hallucination.

"Then is that 'Shadow' bastard also... an illusion created by my unconscious?"

"No. He is real. An entity with an obsession strong enough to ride our connected unconscious and enter. Perhaps he has been waiting for this door to open for 10 years, watching for a chance."

Ji-hyuk rubbed his face with dry hands. The gap between supernatural phenomena and reality investigation. But as a detective, he needed evidence.

"Let's talk about reality first. Please take a look at what I brought."

Ji-hyuk took out a few Polaroid photos from his worn briefcase and spread them on the table.
They were photos of strange crime scenes that recently occurred in his jurisdiction.

"Attempted serial arson... or should I say simple property damage, I don't know. The scale of damage is small, but the pattern is very nasty."

In the photos, burned objects were captured miserably.
A charred leather diary, a safe melted hideously by heat, a pure white wedding dress half-burnt with ashes flying.

"The culprit entered without any trace of intrusion and selectively burned only the objects the victims cherished the most. It seemed like... performing something like a ritual."

Eun-jo picked up the photos one by one and examined them closely.
Her white fingers slowly scanned over the ashes in the photo.

"Can you feel it?"

"This culprit... is not angry. He is worshipping. He is passionately admiring and imitating someone. Like a believer offering a sacrifice to a god."

The word 'Copycat' flashed through Ji-hyuk's mind.

"A guy mimicking... the Shadow."

The Shadow didn't move directly. A follower infected by his malice did it.

"What do the cards say?"

At Ji-hyuk's question, Eun-jo drew a card.
A bizarre devil with a goat's head ruling over a naked man and woman bound in chains.
**<The Devil>.**

"The Devil whispers. To release your darkest desires. This culprit is addicted. To the dangerous pleasure of playing with fire, and to the false idol called the Shadow. He won't be able to stop. Until he is destroyed."

"Addicted, huh... Can we know the next target?"

"His desire to show off is getting stronger. Next, he will want to burn something in front of many people. He wants to show it. That he can handle fire like 'Him'."

"In front of many people..."

Ji-hyuk took out a map of the jurisdiction from his bag.
When he connected the recent incident points with a red pen, the culprit's movement path was slowly narrowing down.

"Seobu Market Outdoor Stage."

This weekend, it was where the redevelopment commemorative local festival was being held.
Crowds of people, flashy lights, and wooden structures piled up for the festival. It was optimal for playing with fire.

***

Weekend night, Seobu Market Outdoor Stage.
It was late at night after the festival heat had subsided, but stage equipment and tents still remained disordered in the square.
Ji-hyuk was hiding in the darkness behind the stage.
4 hours into the stakeout. His clothes were getting wet from the cold night dew and his legs were going numb, but he didn't budge.

*He will definitely come.*

If Eun-jo's profiling was right, the bastard would try to make this place his own 'holy ground' tonight.
*Rustle.*
Then, a man with a black hoodie pressed low appeared from behind the tent, looking around.
The man was holding a heavy-looking oil can in his hand.

*Bingo.*

The man opened the lid and sprinkled oil on the stage pillars. The smell of gasoline wafted out strongly.
The moment the man took a lighter out of his bosom and lit it, Ji-hyuk sprang out from the darkness.

"Don't move! Police!"

"Ack!"

The man was startled, dropped the lighter, and tried to run away.
But running away from 'Hunting Dog' Ji-hyuk was impossible.
Ji-hyuk rushed in at once, snatched the man's nape, and slammed him into the ground.

"Ugh!"

"Playing with fire, you punk."

Ji-hyuk twisted the man's arm back to subdue him.
He was an ordinary-looking young man in his 20s. But his unfocused eyes were strangely loose.

"Why did you do it. Is this fun?"

Ji-hyuk growled.
Then the man started giggling. It was a chilling laughter.

"It is fun... Burning is beautiful... It's being purified..."

"Who ordered you. Is it the Shadow?"

"He is... great. I just follow in his footsteps."

Eyes full of madness.

"I'm just a matchstick. The real bonfire... will burn soon."

Ji-hyuk's spine went cold.

"What are you talking about. Speak straight!"

Then, the cell phone in Ji-hyuk's pocket rang loudly. It was Eun-jo.

"Detective, did you catch him?"

"Yeah, I caught him. But this guy's condition is weird. He says he's just a matchstick..."

"Detective, be careful! The card... the card changed!"

Eun-jo's urgent voice sounded like a scream.
At that moment, the sound of sirens was heard from afar. It was a fire truck, not the familiar police car.
Where Ji-hyuk's gaze landed was the opposite side of the market, right towards the alley where 'Midnight Counseling' was located.
Black smoke and red flames were swallowing the night sky.

"Don't tell me..."

The bastard was a decoy.
A trivial matchstick to lure Ji-hyuk here.
The real target the Shadow aimed for was fixed from the beginning.

"Eun-jo! Get out of there!"

Ji-hyuk shouted into the phone.
But over the receiver, with a roaring *boom-*, the connection was cut off. Only the empty beep- beep- mechanical sound echoed.

"Damn it!"

Ji-hyuk threw the culprit aside and ran like crazy.
The culprit's laughter stuck to his back like ringing in his ears.

The arsonist of the heart.
What they wanted to burn was Ji-hyuk's heart, Eun-jo herself.
