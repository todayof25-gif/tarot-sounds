"Damn it, open up! Open!"

Ji-hyuk kicked the iron door madly.
Pain as if his shoulder blade would break rushed in, but he couldn't stop.
In the screen, the Shadow was approaching Eun-jo.
The bastard was mocking Eun-jo turning the lighter on and off.

"Calm down... let's calm down Kang Ji-hyuk."

Ji-hyuk gasped for rough breath and stepped back.
Getting excited and running wild will only drain stamina.
If he is a hunting dog, he must find an escape route calmly.
He shone the lantern to examine the room.

Boiler room blocked on all sides. The only exit is the locked iron door.
But a small vent was visible on the ceiling.
It looked way too narrow for a person to pass through, but it was worth a try.
Ji-hyuk stepped on the desk and tried to tear off the vent cover.
Then, a pile of documents scattered on the desk fell to the floor.

*Thud.*

A dusty old file opened.
[Hope Nursing Home Counseling Log - 2013]
2013. 10 years ago.
Ji-hyuk picked up the file as if possessed.
The name of the counselor in charge was familiar.
[In Charge: Seo Ji-soo]
It was Eun-jo's mother. A shaman and a psychological counselor who volunteered here.

Ji-hyuk flipped through the journal with trembling hands.
Among the records of numerous patients, there was a particularly noticeable name.

[Patient Name: Lee Jin-oh (Then 19)]
[Symptoms: Severe arson impulse, delusional disorder. Believes he purifies people through fire.]

The counseling record continued.

- March 5: Jin-oh fears fire but admires it. He thinks fire removes all pain. He wants to erase the memory of being abused.
- April 12: Jin-oh started calling me 'Mother'. A dangerous attachment relationship is forming.
- May 20: He blindly believes what I said inadvertently, "Bad memories must be burned to live". He says he is preparing a purification ritual. Must stop him.

"Lee Jin-oh..."

A groan burst out of Ji-hyuk's mouth.
The identity of the Shadow wasn't Eun-jo's father, Lee Myung-chul.
Lee Myung-chul was just sacrificed trying to stop a patient running wild that day 10 years ago.
The real culprit is Lee Jin-oh. The heartbroken boy Eun-jo's mother took care of.
And the person Eun-jo followed like a real older brother.

"My god... Eun-jo doesn't know this..."

Ji-hyuk clenched his fist.
Eun-jo had been suffering from guilt suspecting her father for 10 years.
That all of it was false guilt.
At that moment, Eun-jo's voice was heard from the screen.

***

"Oppa... It's Jin-oh Oppa, right?"

Officetel living room.
Eun-jo didn't step back, but called the Shadow with a trembling voice.
The hand of the man wearing a black raincoat paused.
The lighter flame wavered.

"Do you... remember me?"

Not a mechanical voice, but a scratching real voice of a man flowed out.
Eun-jo's eyes turned red.
She remembered.
The kind older brother she played with at the nursing home in her childhood. The affectionate touch that folded paper flowers for her.
But after the fire 10 years ago, she thought he died.

"How can I forget. I still have the paper flower you folded for me."

"Flower... Yes, flowers wither but ashes are eternal."

The Shadow, Lee Jin-oh took off his gas mask.
A face distorted by burn scars was revealed.
Half remained the appearance of a young boy from 10 years ago, but the other half had melted down like a devil.

"Eun-jo. I came to keep my promise."

Jin-oh approached with staggering steps.

"Mother said. You live only if you burn everything. I failed 10 years ago, but today I will succeed. Those painful memories of yours, sadness, and even that detective bastard tormenting you. I'll burn them all. Then you will become clean."

"No. That wasn't Mother's intention."

Eun-jo shook her head firmly.

"What Mother talked about wasn't playing with fire. It meant to put down the burden of the heart. You were wrong, Oppa."

"Wrong? I'm wrong?"

Jin-oh's eyes overturned with madness.

"I'm trying to save you! From this dirty world, trying to liberate you from pain! Why don't you understand my heart!"

Jin-oh threw the lighter.
The sofa caught fire. In an instant, the flames moved to the living room curtains.
Acrid smoke rose.
Sprinklers activated and water poured down.
Sirens rang.

"No..."

Eun-jo collapsed.
It's fire again.

"Now, let's go. It's time for purification."

Jin-oh grabbed Eun-jo's arm roughly.
It was then.

*Bang!*

The front door opened as if breaking.
No, the door lock was unlocked and someone ran in.
It was Ji-hyuk who looked like a drowned rat.

"Let go of that hand!"

Ji-hyuk tore off the boiler room vent, crawled through the narrow passage, and ran through the rain.
His whole body was full of wounds and his clothes were covered in muddy water, but his eyes were alive.

"Ji-hyuk!"

"Having a fun party. I don't have an invitation but include me too."

Ji-hyuk gasped for breath and aimed his pistol.

"Lee Jin-oh. Escaped mental hospital patient from 10 years ago. Right?"

Jin-oh's expression hardened.

"How... my name..."

"I read your counseling log. It was quite a spectacle. Salvation? You're just a patient kicked out for playing with fire."

"Shut up! You know nothing!"

Jin-oh took a knife out of his bosom and rushed at Ji-hyuk.
Ji-hyuk didn't pull the trigger. Instead, he threw his body and tackled Jin-oh.
Two men tangled and rolled on the floor.
In the flames and water, the final hand-to-hand combat began.
