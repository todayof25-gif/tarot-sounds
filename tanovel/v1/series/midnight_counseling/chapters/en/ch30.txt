"Okay, one, two, three! Lights on!"

*Pop! Pop!*
Along with the sound of congratulatory firecrackers popping, the lights on the new signboard turned on.

[Sunshine Counseling & Cafe]

People gathered in front of the neat shop with floor-to-ceiling glass on the Seongsu-dong cafe street clapped with cheers.
Eun-jo looked up at her new workplace with a touched expression.
A bright space facing south where sunlight comes in full. Everything was different from 'Midnight Counseling' where she had to hold her breath relying on a single candle in a damp back alley basement just a year ago.
Instead of gloomy purple curtains, white chiffon curtains fluttered in the wind, and instead of acrid incense smell, the scent of freshly baked cookies and coffee filled the street.

"Congratulations, Boss."

Ji-hyuk thrust out a large bouquet. It was a bouquet where lavender's purple and freesia's yellow were harmoniously mixed.
It was a color combination signifying the coexistence of the past and the future.

"Thank you, Team Leader. You have good taste."

Eun-jo buried her nose in the bouquet and smiled brightly.
Now she didn't have to wait for midnight.
Because her time was now flowing from dark night past dawn, towards brilliant morning.

"Now, everyone come in! Have some opening rice cakes and drinks!"

At Eun-jo's shout, Min-seo, Old Man Kim, and acquaintances including Yu-na went into the shop noisily.
From the first day of opening, there was no room to step in the shop. Thanks to Yu-na promoting it on her SNS as 'the healing spot that changed my life', young customers lined up, and thanks to Old Man Kim spreading rumors all over the neighborhood, elderly people also visited as if going for a walk.

Ji-hyuk played the role of 'Security Team Leader' thoroughly guarding next to the counter as promised.
He politely(?) restrained people trying to cut in line with his unique gaze, and escorted customers carrying heavy loads.

"Excuse me, isn't that security guard oppa so cool?"
"But the atmosphere with the boss is unusual? They're wearing couple rings."

At the students' whispering, Ji-hyuk coughed and fixed his tie for no reason. Eun-jo stuck out her tongue secretly watching him from behind the coffee machine. It was a peaceful and happy afternoon.

Time passed quickly and the sun began to set.
After seeing off all the last customers, only Eun-jo and Ji-hyuk were left in the shop.
Golden sunset shone long inside the shop.

"Tired, right?"

Ji-hyuk pulled out a chair and asked.

"No. My legs hurt a bit but... I feel like flying. It feels like a dream to be able to meet people in a sunny place proudly like this."

Eun-jo answered with an overwhelmed voice.
Ji-hyuk approached and massaged Eun-jo's shoulders.

"It's thanks to you enduring without giving up. That long night of 10 years."

"I couldn't have done it without you. My Emperor."

Eun-jo overlapped her hand on the back of Ji-hyuk's hand. Silver rings on their ring fingers sparkled receiving the sunset light.
It was then.

*Jingle-.*

Just before hanging the sign announcing closing time, the wind chime rang.
Someone came in.
It was a fernale student wearing a school uniform. Shoulders drooping, and dark circles under the eyes were somewhat familiar.
The student hesitated and approached the counter.

"Excuse me... Is this the place that does counseling too?"

"Yes, that's right. But we are closed..."

Ji-hyuk was about to politely refuse, but Eun-jo grabbed his hem.
The student's eyes.
Eun-jo knew those desperate eyes shouting to save her. Ji-hyuk's eyes 1 year ago were like that, and her own eyes 10 years ago were like that.

"It's okay. Come in. The tea water hasn't cooled yet."

Eun-jo smiled softly and welcomed the student.
The student sat on the chair as if relieved only then.
Hesitating for a long time, the student opened her mouth with a crawling voice.

"I... can't sleep. I have nightmares every night. It's scary to go to school so I want to die."

School violence, and insomnia.
Eun-jo and Ji-hyuk's gazes met in the air.
Like fate, a wounded soul came to find them again. But this time it was different.
They were now ready to reach out their hands proudly without running away or hiding.

Eun-jo brewed warm herbal tea on the spot instead of taking out a hidden menu from behind the menu board. It was fragrant tea blending chamomile and lavender.
And spread Tarot cards on the table with familiar hand movements.

"What is your name?"

"Su-jin... Lee Su-jin."

Su-jin.
Ji-hyuk's eyes opened wide. The same name as the child he couldn't save 10 years ago in the fire.
It felt like his heart sank with a thud.
Is it the last test sent by God, or a gift?
Ji-hyuk quietly wrapped his hand around Eun-jo's shoulder. Eun-jo's warm body temperature was conveyed.
He took a deep breath. Now he didn't see the ghost of the past. He only saw the living child sitting in front of him who he could save and had to save.

"Su-jin. Would you like to drink this tea? It's magic tea that makes your mind comfortable."

Eun-jo handed the teacup.
And spread 78 Tarot cards in a fan shape.

"Now, do you want to pick a card? As your heart commands."

Su-jin picked one card carefully with trembling hands.
Eun-jo flipped the card carefully.

A gorgeous card where a naked woman is dancing inside a laurel wreath, and a lion, bull, eagle, and angel are drawn in the four corners.
The last number of Major Arcana, number 21.

**<The World>**.

'Completion, success, harmony, and happy ending.'

"Wow... A really good card came out."

Eun-jo's eyes twinkled.

"This means the hard journey ends, and a completely new world opens. It means the egg that trapped you breaks, and it's time to move forward to a wider world. Your long night will end soon too. The key to open that door is already in your hand."

"Really... will it be so? Can I... become happy too?"

Tears welled up in Su-jin's eyes.

"Of course. I will help you. And..."

Eun-jo pointed to Ji-hyuk standing next to her.

"There is a very reliable sheriff uncle here too. He will protect you so nightmares can't even come close."

Ji-hyuk nodded with a pretend solemn expression making eye contact with Su-jin.

"Yes. Uncle promises. From today, you are not alone."

A faint but clear smile spread on Su-jin's face. Like a sprout sprouting on cold winter ground, it was a small sprout of hope.

Red sunset completely set outside the window, and streetlights on the street began to turn on one by one.
Night was coming.
But it wasn't scary anymore.
Darkness cannot beat light, and cold winter surely becomes warm spring.

"Now, shall we start counseling? Tell me your story."

Eun-jo's voice rang out warmly.
The light of the shop signboard twinkled like stars in the night sky.
Their story ended, but a story for another someone was just beginning.

The wheel of fate was now turning vigorously towards happiness.

(The End)
