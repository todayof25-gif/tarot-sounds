Morning sunlight squeezed through the blackout curtains and tickled Ji-hyuk's eyelids.
Ji-hyuk opened his heavy eyes.
He paused while trying to check his smartphone next to him out of habit.
Because unfamiliar clattering sounds were heard from the kitchen.
The cheerful sound of a knife hitting the cutting board, the sound of stew boiling, and... the savory smell of soybean paste.
Sounds and smells that could never occur in his house, which was full of only instant coffee and cigarette smells.

"Is this a dream..."

For him who only had nightmares every day for 10 years, this peaceful morning was too unrealistic.
Ji-hyuk shook his messy hair and went out to the living room.
And he stood still blankly.

Eun-jo was standing in the kitchen wearing his loose apron.
Rolling up her t-shirt sleeves, and skillfully preparing breakfast.
Her profile receiving sunlight coming through the window was dazzlingly beautiful.
To the point where the miserable fire and tragedy of last night felt like a lie.

"Did you wake up? Did you sleep well?"

Eun-jo felt his presence, turned around, and smiled brightly.
It was a smile brighter and warmer than the morning sunlight.

"Ah... yes. But what are you doing right now? You're not feeling well."

"Breakfast. I opened the refrigerator and there was nothing but water bottles. People have to live on rice power, how can you survive filling your stomach with water."

"So..."

"I hurriedly ordered some ingredients via dawn delivery. Tofu, zucchini, eggs... Ah, I paid with your card, Detective. Your wallet was on the table. Is that okay?"

"Ah, yes. Well... as much as you want."

Ji-hyuk nodded bewilderedly. It seemed not wasteful even if he spent his entire fortune.
On the table, steaming tofu soybean paste stew, thick rolled omelet, and freshly cooked white rice were set.
For Ji-hyuk who lived on convenience store lunch boxes, it was a proper home-cooked meal he hadn't seen in months, no, years.

"Sit down. You need to cure your hangover. You inhaled a lot of smoke yesterday. I wanted to make bean sprout soup but there wasn't any, so I made soybean paste stew."

The two sat facing each other.
He thought awkward silence would flow, but it was unexpectedly natural.
Like a newlywed couple who had lived like this for a very long time.

"It's... delicious."

Ji-hyuk took a spoonful of stew and admired.
It wasn't the taste of MSG, but a deep and light taste. It felt like his stomach was unwinding.

"That's a relief. I was worried it wouldn't suit your taste. I'm quite good at cooking. Since I lived alone for a long time."

Eun-jo was relieved and picked up her spoon.
Ji-hyuk glanced at Eun-jo while eating rice.
She seemed like a mysterious and unapproachable 'Witch' at the counseling shop, but here she was just a lovely woman.
It's a relief that she is alive, eating rice in front of me.
Ji-hyuk swallowed relief chewing rice grains.

"Detective, there's rice on your face."

Eun-jo reached out and picked a grain of rice off the corner of Ji-hyuk's mouth.
Ji-hyuk's heart sank with a thud.
The spot her finger brushed against was hot as if burned.

"Ahem. Thank you."

Ji-hyuk buried his nose in the soup bowl and slurped to hide his reddened face.
How long has it been since this kind of ordinary happiness?
But this peace was only borrowed for a moment.
They were still being chased, and the Shadow would strike again anytime.

After finishing the meal, Ji-hyuk prepared for work.
He was tying his tie roughly as usual in front of the mirror, when Eun-jo approached.

"Wait. The tie is crooked. If you go out like that, the criminals will laugh at you."

Eun-jo stood on tiptoe and fixed Ji-hyuk's tie.
It was close.
The subtle scent of shampoo from her tickled the tip of his nose.
Ji-hyuk held his breath.
He could even see Eun-jo's long eyelashes trembling in front of his eyes.
*I want to hug her.*
The impulse rose strongly, but Ji-hyuk clenched his fist and held back.
Now was not the time. Until he catches the bastard and secures perfect safety.

"Done. Now you look a bit like 'The Emperor'."

"I'll be back. Lock the door well, and never open it whoever comes. No delivery food. I'll buy it and come."

"Yes, alright. Nagging Emperor. Be careful."

Leaving the front door receiving Eun-jo's send-off, Ji-hyuk's steps were light as a feather, but his eyes were sharper than ever.
He had something to protect. Something more precious than his life.
Now he will make it so no one can touch her.

Arriving at the police station, Ji-hyuk headed straight to the NFS Evidence Analysis Room.
He handed over the 'non-burning card' collected from the scene last night to his colleague, an analyst.

"Hey, this material is unique? It's not normal paper. Looks like flame-retardant treated special plastic synthetic resin. Plus, this pattern carved on the back..."

The analyst magnified the back of the card with a microscope and displayed it on the monitor.
Inside the eye-shaped symbol invisible to the naked eye, small letters were carved.

[L.O]

"L O? What does this mean? Is it a brand logo?"

Ji-hyuk stared at the monitor piercingly.
Is it initials? Or an abbreviation of some group?
Lucifer? Lost One? Tens of thousands of words flashed through his head.

"Hey, Kang Ji-hyuk. And the ink component analysis came out, it's a very rare pigment. 'Egyptian Blue', it reproduces ancient pigment, and there's only one printing shop in the country that uses this."

"Where is it? Speak quickly."

"It's a place called 'Arcana Art' in Seongsu-dong. It's a custom workshop specializing only in rare items like collector's Tarot cards or magic supplies. The unit price is so expensive that not anyone can use it."

*Bingo.*
Finally caught the bastard's tail.
Ji-hyuk stood up immediately.

"Thanks. I'll buy you a big meal later."

"Hey, where are you going! The team leader is looking for you! You should write the report before going!"

"Tell him I'll write it after catching the criminal!"

Ji-hyuk ran out without looking back.
Seongsu-dong. The bastard's hideout, or at least a string connected to the bastard is there.

Starting the car, Ji-hyuk sent a text to Eun-jo.
[Found a clue on the culprit. I'll solve it quickly and go. I'll buy something delicious for dinner so wait.]

The moment he pressed the send button, another card slipped out of Ji-hyuk's jacket pocket.
It was a card Eun-jo secretly put in this morning saying it's a charm.
A picture of a man and a woman exchanging cups and vowing.
**<Two of Cups>.**

'Partnership, Love, Union.'

Ji-hyuk smirked looking at the card.
Not being alone was a much more reassuring thing than he thought.
"Wait. I'll be there soon."
He stepped on the accelerator hard.
For the woman he loves, and for their perfect union.

(End of Episode 22)
