The siren sound of police cars sharply tore through the blue silence of dawn.
Red and blue warning lights rotated dizzyingly around the ruined two-story western-style house in the Seongbuk-dong redevelopment district. Police lines were set up, and forensics officers in white protective suits were busy moving back and forth.
It was the moment when the truth buried 10 years ago was revealed to the world again.

"Come out! Head down!"

With the shouts of detectives, handcuffed Lee Jin-oh was dragged out.
He didn't resist. His patient uniform was torn and covered in dirt from the intense struggle, but his expression was strangely peaceful. No, he looked blank.
Looking up at the sky, white breath puffed out from his lips as he mumbled something.

"Not yet... not over. Purification must continue... Need... quiet..."

Repeating what he said like a broken radio, the boy from 10 years ago was nowhere to be found. Only a shell encroached by madness remained.

Flashbulbs went off.
Reporters flocked like hyenas smelling prey and pressed their shutters.
Sensational headlines like 'Arrest of Real Culprit of 10-Year-Old Fire Case', 'Exclusive! Serial Arsonist's Identity is Pseudo-Religion Believer', and 'Police's Tenacious Pursuit' would be wired in real-time.

Ji-hyuk was sitting on the back of the ambulance receiving first aid.
His already injured leg swelled up from the fall impact, and abrasions all over his face stung. But his expression was relaxed with genuine relief without pretense for the first time in 10 years.
Next to him, Eun-jo was holding a warm canned coffee wrapped in a gray blanket.

"Are you okay?"

Ji-hyuk asked.
Eun-jo nodded slowly, feeling the warmth of the canned coffee.

"Yes. It's calmer... than I thought. It feels like pulling out a tooth that has been aching for a long time."

"That's a relief."

"It's over... right?"

At Eun-jo's question, Ji-hyuk looked at the convoy car where Lee Jin-oh was riding.

"No. There is still one thing left."

Ji-hyuk stood up with his limping leg. The paramedic dissuaded him, but he stopped him by raising his hand.
He had one thing he definitely had to do.
Ji-hyuk limped towards the convoy. Fellow detectives cleared the way.

"Wait a minute. I have something to say."

Just before the convoy door closed, Ji-hyuk looked inside the car.
Jin-oh was curling up with his head buried in his knees.

"Lee Jin-oh."

At Ji-hyuk's cold call, Jin-oh slowly raised his head. Turbid eyes without focus turned to Ji-hyuk.

"You are not a savior. You're just a coward who likes playing with fire. What you burned wasn't sin, but the happiness of innocent people."

"What do you know... I was chosen. Mother... Mother ordered it..."

Jin-oh argued with a hoarse voice as if unfair.

"Do you know what the last words left by that mother were?"

Ji-hyuk took out an old notebook from his jacket inner pocket. Mother Seo Ji-soo's diary that was inserted along with the **<The High Priestess>** card Eun-jo gave him.
Ji-hyuk opened the page he folded in advance and thrust it in front of Jin-oh's eyes.

"**<Judgment (Judgement)>**."

Ji-hyuk showed the Tarot card he held in his other hand.
A picture of an angel blowing a trumpet and people rising from coffins to resurrect.

" The real meaning of this card isn't 'death' but 'resurrection'. And 'right judgment'. You set fires and killed people for the last 10 years, but Seo Ji-soo wanted to save people. Including the pitiful soul that is you."

Ji-hyuk put a piece of paper torn from the diary into handcuffed Jin-oh's hand.
It was a page with a prayer written by his mother for him when Jin-oh cried every night at the nursing home 10 years ago.

[Jin-oh. Fire cannot burn you. Inside you, there is a light warmer than fire. You are not a cursed child, but a child born to be loved. Please forgive yourself.]

Jin-oh's gaze was stuck on the paper.
His pupils shook violently as if there was an earthquake.
He read and read the paper again with trembling hands. As if engraving each letter in his eyes.
The voice of his mother, which he distorted, twisted, and edited in his delusion, was stuck as printed text, becoming a real voice. In those sentences, instead of the curse 'Die', only warm comfort and love were full.

"Ah... Aaa..."

Jin-oh's lips trembled.
Thick tears dropped from his eyes and flowed down onto the handcuffs.

"Uwaaaaaa-!"

Soon, a beast-like cry burst out.
It wasn't a mad laugh. It was the sorrowful wailing of an abused child vomiting out for the first time in 10 years.
It was the moment when the sturdy castle wall called delusion he built up collapsed with a crash by a single sheet of thin paper containing love.

"Pay for your crimes. That's the only atonement you can do. And... don't appear in front of Eun-jo again."

Ji-hyuk spoke coldly and closed the car door.
*Bang.*
The heavy door closed, and the convoy started. Jin-oh's crying sound remained faintly behind the receding car and then disappeared.

Ji-hyuk sighed deeply. It felt like a hot stone lump that had been knotted in his chest slipped out.
Turning around, Eun-jo was standing there.
She was also watching all those scenes. Her appearance standing against the morning sunlight looked like the angel of the **<Judgment>** card.

"Thank you."

Eun-jo smiled wetly.

"For giving that person... a last chance. Although it's too late."

"Your mother would have wanted it. You said the real job of a counselor is to hug instead of hitting with a ruler."

Ji-hyuk rubbed his nose as if embarrassed.
Eun-jo approached and held Ji-hyuk's hand. Warmth was circulating in her cold fingertips.

Dawn was breaking.
The rain clouds that fell all night retreated, and the sky of Seoul was dyed in transparent indigo.
Above the ruined two-story house, warm morning sunlight shone down for the first time in 10 years. The light brilliantly illuminated even the blackened bricks and broken glass windows.
As if the gates of hell closed and the gate to a new world opened.

"What are you going to do now?"

Ji-hyuk asked.

"Well... First, I'm hungry. I want to eat a bowl of warm rice soup. And..."

Eun-jo looked up at Ji-hyuk and smiled brightly.

"I want to sleep soundly. Without dreaming."

"That sounds good. I agree."

Ji-hyuk held Eun-jo's hand tight.
The two moved their steps side by side. Crossing the police line, passing the dark alley, towards the main road where light poured.
The shadow disappeared.
Now in front of them, only the footprints of the two people and the warm morning sunlight continued long.

(End of Episode 28)
