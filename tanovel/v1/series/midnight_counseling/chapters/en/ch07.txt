A few days after the rain stopped, a strange stillness hung over the counseling shop.
The air was heavy. It wasn't just because of the humidity.
Ji-hyuk dropped by the shop after work as a habit, but the atmosphere was distinctly different from usual.
Instead of warm lighting, a chilly darkness lay settled, and Eun-jo was curled up in the corner of the counter, in the darkest shadow.
Her eyes were sharp like a beast stalking prey, yet terrified, watching the entrance door.

"Is something wrong?"

When Ji-hyuk asked, Eun-jo startled and raised her head.
Her pale face had turned even whiter.

"N... nothing. Welcome."

Her voice was an octave higher than usual and trembled slightly.
She's hiding something.
His detective instincts sounded a sharp warning alarm.

"Must have had a guest."

Ji-hyuk gestured with his eyes to the two teacups on the table.
One looked like Eun-jo's, the other the guest's.
But the chair opposite was stone cold.
Not a trace of warmth could be found.

"Yes. He just left."

"What kind of guest was it?"

"Just... an ordinary man. With severe insomnia."

Eun-jo avoided his gaze and hurriedly cleared the teacups.
But Ji-hyuk saw it.
There were absolutely no lip marks on the rim of the guest's teacup.
And something even stranger.

"That's weird."

Ji-hyuk looked down at the floor intently.
Because of the rain yesterday, it was muddy everywhere outside.
The soles of Ji-hyuk's shoes were also covered in sticky mud.
But...

"There are no footprints."

From the entrance to the table, the floor was clean without a spec of dust.
If someone just left, there should normally be at least water or dirt marks.
Unless they flew in, it was impossible.

"Maybe he took his shoes off."

Eun-jo's excuse was pathetic.

"Knowing full well this is a place where you wear shoes? And if he took them off, sock marks would have remained."

Ji-hyuk's eyes narrowed.
He jumped up, opened the front door, and went out.
He scoured the dark alleyway.
His own footprints were clearly stamped, but there were zero traces of anyone else having visited this alley.

*CCTV.*
The security CCTV on the telephone pole at the entrance of the alley caught his eye.

"I need to check."

Ji-hyuk immediately contacted the situation room at his precinct and requested the CCTV footage for that time slot.
30 minutes later, goosebumps rose on Ji-hyuk's spine as he checked the footage sent to his smartphone.

[11:50 PM ~ 12:30 AM]

In the video, no one entered the counseling shop.
Except for 12:10 when Ji-hyuk arrived, not even an ant passed through the alley.
Then who was the 'guest' Eun-jo spoke of?
Eun-jo is lying, or...

*Is she saying a ghost visited?*

Ji-hyuk went back inside the shop.
Eun-jo was still hiding behind the counter, trembling.
What she feared was not Ji-hyuk's interrogation, but something invisible.

"Eun-jo. Be honest. Who was that 'guest' just now? No one was on the CCTV."

Ji-hyuk asked in a low, firm voice.

"No one... was there."

"Liar. There were two teacups. You were talking to someone."

"It was like a ritual offering."

Eun-jo mumbled with her head bowed deep.

"What?"

"Someone who shouldn't come... no, something not human kept snooping around. So I treated it like a bribe, begging it to just go away."

*Something not human.*
Ji-hyuk felt his back go cold.
The usual Eun-jo would have laughed it off saying "Ghosts are guests too," but the fear she felt now was real.
It wasn't an act.

It was then.
From the dark shadow in the corner of the counseling shop, something fell with a thud.
Ji-hyuk reflexively reached for the gun at his waist and turned around.
An old hardcover book that had been well-placed on the bookshelf had fallen to the floor.

"Was it the wind..."

The windows were tightly closed.
Ji-hyuk approached carefully and picked up the book.
On the open page, scribbles lay messy in red pen, as if vomiting out rage.

[FOUND YOU]
[FOUND YOU]
[WITCH]
[MURDERER]
[MURDERER]

The horrifying letters filled the page with blood-red ink.
Ji-hyuk looked at Eun-jo, who had frozen on the spot.

"What is this? Who played such a nasty prank..."

"It's not a prank."

Eun-jo barely spat out the words with pale blue lips.

"The shadow... found me. I hid for 10 years, but it eventually smelled me out and came."

"Shadow?"

"A monster born from fire. A bastard that feeds on people's nightmares."

Eun-jo's pupils dilated with extreme terror.
She stumbled closer and grabbed Ji-hyuk's clothes tight like a lifeline.
Her fingertips turned white.

"Detective. I have a favor."

"Speak. Anything."

"Don't dig any further. Don't look for CCTV or footprints. That thing... is not an opponent you can catch. Laws and bullets don't work on it."

"Is it a blackmailer? A stalker?"

"No. It's not something trivial like that."

Eun-jo shook her head violently.

"That thing... uses your guilt as bait. It will touch the memories you hurt from and want to hide the most."

For a moment, Ji-hyuk felt like the illusion of the girl whose hand he lost 10 years ago was shimmering before his eyes.
This woman knows.
What he fears, and what the identity of this 'shadow' bastard is.

*Jingle—.*

At that moment, the bell on the door rang again.
But the door didn't open.
There was no wind either.
Only the bell rang loudly as if someone had playfully flicked it with their hand.

"Kyaaaak!"

Eun-jo screamed, covered her ears, and collapsed.
Ji-hyuk pulled out his gun and aimed at the empty air.
"Who's there, come out!"

An invisible enemy.
But the enemy's murderous intent was felt clearly enough to sting the skin.
Midnight Counseling.
Now this place was not a peaceful shelter, but a hunting ground for an invisible hunter.

(End of Episode 7)
