6 months later.

The season had already entered late autumn.
Fallen leaves rolling on the street made rustling sounds, and people's clothes became thicker. A season where the smell of winter was stained when cold wind brushed the tip of the nose.

A quiet cafe street in Seongsu-dong.
Not a damp and dark back alley like before, but a small shop in a sunny alley where sunlight came in long diagonally was undergoing remodeling construction.
Between laborers diligently moving materials, a man wearing gray work clothes climbed up a ladder and was hanging a signboard hard.
It was Ji-hyuk.

"Hey, Team Leader Kang! A bit more to the right! No, too far!"

The one crossing his arms below and giving instructions was the Violent Crimes Team Leader. He came to help despite it being his day off.

"Team Leader, don't just talk but hold it a bit, will you? This is quite heavy."

Ji-hyuk grumbled and drove the drill. With a whirring sound, the screw was firmly constantly.
His face looked much more comfortable than 6 months ago. The deep wrinkles always caught between his eyebrows were smoothed out, and the dark circles that came down to his chin disappeared cleanly.
He no longer feared sleepless nights.

"Wow, our 'Emperor'. Now that I see it, you're practically MacGyver? You could quit the police and open an interior design company."

From inside the shop, Eun-jo came out holding iced Americano and snacks on a tray and laughed.
Her appearance had strictly changed dazzlingly too.
The clothes she insisted only on black changed to a beige knit and long skirt, and a healthy flush circulated on her pale cheeks. Above all, vitality was twinkling in her eyes that resembled deep wells.

"Thank you for the compliment. But the salary is a bit salty. Will the boss give me some bonus?"

Ji-hyuk came down from the ladder, wiped his sweat, and winked at Eun-jo.
Eun-jo smirked and handed him cool coffee. The sound of ice clattering was cheerful like autumn sunlight.

"But are you really okay? Coming every day off and suffering like this... You'll get sick."

"Not at all. Rather, I'm in trouble because I have too much energy these days. Maybe because I sleep too well at night."

Ji-hyuk's words were not a joke.
After Lee Jin-oh was arrested and the truth 10 years old was revealed that day, Ji-hyuk's insomnia disappeared like a lie.
No more 'Starlight Tea' or sleeping pills were needed. Listening to Eun-jo's breathing sound and holding her hand, sleep poured in.
Nightmares no longer visited him.

"Have you decided on the shop name?"

The Team Leader asked looking at the signboard frame.

"Um... I'm thinking. 'Midnight Counseling' doesn't fit anymore. Since we'll open during the day too."

"Right. Opening only at night is suspicious. Name it something bright this time. How about 'Sunshine Counseling'?"

"That's so tacky."

The three people looked at each other and burst into laughter.
At that moment, a jingling- wind chime sound rang at the shop entrance.
They were welcome faces.

"Teacher! Hello!"

A sophisticated lady with short hair that suits her well. The client of Episode 3, Min-seo. In her hand was a flower pot with a ribbon written 'Hit the Jackpot'.
Behind her, Old Man Kim from Episode 5 came in leaning on a cane, and actress Yu-na from Episode 13 even appeared disguised with a hat and sunglasses.

"Oh my, an old man came empty-handed tactlessly. Instead, I brought some photos of our Nabi. She gained so much weight these days she became a pig-cat."

"Oh my, Unnie! It's so pretty here! Who did the interior? It's totally my style!"

Eun-jo ran out surprised and welcomed them.
Min-seo became a confident career woman now, and Old Man Kim looked much healthier. Yu-na was also active again overcoming the scandal.
They all walked out of their own hells through Eun-jo's counseling and were living new lives.
And now they came to support Eun-jo's new beginning in reverse.

Ji-hyuk watched that scene with satisfaction.
Saving people wasn't some grand superpower or magic.
Someone's warm gaze listening to one's story. And sincere comfort saying "It's not your fault". That was enough.
Eun-jo was a person who had that power.

"Detective, have a drink too. Eat some cake too."

Min-seo handed a plate to Ji-hyuk.

"Ah, Ms. Min-seo. I'm not a detective anymore."

"Yes? Really?"

Min-seo, Eun-jo, and even the Team Leader (this was acting) looked with surprised eyes.
Ji-hyuk scratched the back of his head as if awkward.

"I didn't submit a resignation letter but... applied for a position change. I'm leaving the Violent Crimes Unit and going to the Women and Juvenile Affairs Division."

"Women and Juvenile Division? Why there?"

Eun-jo asked as if puzzled.

"Catching criminals is important too but... I wanted to do work protecting and preventing victims. So that second Lee Jin-ohs and second Eun-jos don't appear."

Ji-hyuk's eyes were softer than 6 months ago, but much harder.
It was the eyes of a person who didn't get buried in past wounds but used those wounds as fertilizer to move forward.

"And... I'm going to work a second job too."

"Second job?"

Ji-hyuk took out a business card from his inner pocket and handed it to Eun-jo.
It was a piping hot business card with ink not even dried yet.

[Psychological Counseling Cafe Security Team Leader Kang Ji-hyuk]

"I will protect this shop. Determine bad customers and kick them out, change light bulbs if they go out, and sometimes brew coffee too."

Ji-hyuk looked deeply into Eun-jo's eyes and took a step closer.

"Above all... I will become an anchor that holds you whenever you shake. For the rest of my life."

Eun-jo's face turned red like a well-ripened apple.
Cheers and applause sounds like "Wow!" burst out from the surroundings. Yu-na made a fuss saying "Is this a drama? No way!"

Eun-jo smiled shyly and nodded.
And flipped one Tarot card placed on the table.
A picture of a dove, a symbol of peace, coming down biting a host above a huge golden cup. A card where five streams of water overflow into a lake where lotuses bloomed.

**<Ace of Cups>**.

'Overflowing love, start of new emotions, and joy.'

"Welcome, Team Leader Kang. You're hired."

At Eun-jo's answer, the inside of the shop filled with laughter.
Lavender scent, smell of freshly roasted beans, and people's warmth harmonized to fill the space warmly.
Outside the window, autumn wind still blew, but inside the shop, it was already spring.
This was exactly the scent of dawn.

(End of Episode 29)
