Everything starts again on ashes.
The Tarot card **<Death>** signifies inevitable destruction for a new beginning, not the end. But the process of destruction is always painful.

2 AM.
After the police dispersed the protesters, cleared the alley, and left, heavy silence settled in the counseling shop.
Like the sea after a storm, it was a precarious peace.
Ji-hyuk was blocking the broken window with plywood as a temporary measure. The sound of hammering echoed dully in the empty space.
Eun-jo lit scented candles in the corner.
But an ominous smell that couldn't be covered even by lavender scent was seeping in.
Acrid, oily, the very smell reminding of the nightmare 10 years ago.

"Smell of oil..."

Eun-jo muttered and jumped up.

"We have to get out of here."

Ji-hyuk threw the hammer and shouted. The hunting dog's instinct sensed danger that made his hair stand on end.
But it was late.

*BOOM—!*

With a huge roar, the front door flew off entirely.
The shockwave of the explosion swept through the counseling shop. The table flipped and glass cups shattered.
Red flames rushed into the shop like the tongue of a hungry beast.
In an instant, the indoor temperature soared. Heat that seemed to burn the skin attacked.

"Eun-jo!"

Ji-hyuk reflexively threw his body and covered Eun-jo.
The two rolled roughly onto the floor.
The ceiling chandelier fell and shattered in the spot where Eun-jo was standing just a second ago.
*Crash—!* Crystal shards flew like shrapnel.
Black smoke covered the vision in an instant. It was hard to even breathe.
Like that night 10 years ago, the world was dyed entirely in red and black.

"Cough... Cough..."

"Are you okay? Stay with me! Hold your breath!"

Ji-hyuk took off the shirt he was wearing and covered Eun-jo's nose and mouth.
Then, someone walked in leisurely from within the blazing flames.
A man wearing a thick fire suit and a black gas mask.
Holding a nozzle like a flamethrower in one hand, and fanning Tarot cards in the other.
That appearance looked like a reaper walking out of hellfire.

"Isn't it beautiful? The flame of purification."

A modulated mechanical voice heard through the gas mask.
It was a chillingly calm and joyful voice.
Ji-hyuk could tell. It's him.
The guy who killed Su-jin—or so he thought—10 years ago, and pushed Ji-hyuk into hell. 'The Shadow'.

"What the hell are you! Why are you doing this!"

Ji-hyuk shouted.
Instead of answering, the Shadow threw a card.
A special black card made of material that doesn't burn. **<Death>.**
A picture of a skeleton knight riding a white horse and walking leisurely over corpses.

"I am forgotten memory, and the returned judge. I came to burn the dirty sins you buried."

"Sins? Don't be funny. You're just a crazy arsonist! A murderer whose hobby is killing people!"

Ji-hyuk pulled out his pistol.
But the Shadow shot flames as if mocking him.
*Fwoosh—!*
The fire spread along the wall and completely blocked the front door, the only escape route.

"Hunting Dog, do you really think that woman you saved 10 years ago... is a pure victim?"

The Shadow pointed at Eun-jo.

"That woman knew. That there would be a fire that night."

Ji-hyuk's gaze wavered. He looked at Eun-jo.
Eun-jo shook her head with a pale face. But her pupils were shaking severely.

"What bullshit..."

"That woman's mother, that possessed shaman bitch prophesied. 'You live only if you burn everything'. So that woman watched without putting out the fire. Watching her own father burn to death! She ran away even though she could have saved him!"

"It's a lie... No!"

Eun-jo screamed.
That was the reality of the nightmare that had tormented her for 10 years.
Bystander. Silent sympathizer.
The terrible guilt that she couldn't save her father who could have lived, because of fear, no, because of her mother's prophecy.

"Yes. That is your sin. That's why you are called a witch."

The Shadow approached.

"Let's end it now. This time I'll burn everything without mistakes. Along with your sins."

The nozzle of the flamethrower aimed at the two.
A desperate moment where red flames were right in front of their noses.
Ji-hyuk hugged Eun-jo and threw himself toward the window.
The crack in the broken window, the only hole the fire hadn't reached yet.

"Hold on!"

*Thud!*

Glass shards scratched his back, but he didn't have time to feel it.
The two fell onto the garbage dump in the back alley.
Ji-hyuk rolled, absorbing the shock with his whole body. Terrible pain was felt in his back, but not even a scream came out.
The counseling shop building was already burning, becoming a huge torch.
The Shadow was standing in the flames looking down at them.
Like the King of Hell presiding over an execution by burning.

"Survived, huh. Tough lives."

The Shadow disappeared into the smoke.
He didn't hurry.
The **<Death>** card was already cast, and their sanctuary had become ashes.
Because his goal wasn't murder, but breaking their 'souls'.

"Detective... Detective! Open your eyes!"

Eun-jo shook bloodied Ji-hyuk.
Ji-hyuk gasped for breath and opened his eyes.
The counseling shop was burning in front of him.
The place declared as Ji-hyuk's 'territory', Eun-jo's only sanctuary, was collapsing.
Acrid smoke stung his lungs.

"Don't cry..."

Ji-hyuk wiped Eun-jo's soot-covered cheek with a trembling hand.
Instead of moaning in pain, he gritted his teeth and raised his body.

"It's not over."

His eyes were flashing with chilling murderous intent, not despair.
The hunting dog's house burned down, but the hunting dog survived.
And now he had bone-deeply imprinted the bastard's smell, the bastard's voice, and the bastard's malice.

"I will catch him. Even if I chase him to the ends of the earth. I will burn him to death with my own hands."

Leaving the burning counseling shop behind, Ji-hyuk supported Eun-jo and walked into the darkness.
On the ashes, the fates of the two were completely tied as one.
There is nowhere to hide anymore.
Now they had to become the chasers, not the ones running away.

The curtain fell.
And Act 2, the real war, was about to begin.
