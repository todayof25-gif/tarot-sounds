The next night, worries became reality.
The alley in front of the counseling shop was chaotic.
Dozens of people filled the narrow alley.
People wearing red headbands, YouTubers holding smartphones for live broadcasts, and even neighborhood residents snooping out of curiosity.

"Drive out the cult witch!"
"Arrest the hypnotist luring runaway teenagers immediately!"

The megaphone sound tore through the quiet dawn air.
The entrance of the counseling shop was covered in eggs and flour.
The windows were covered with newspapers, but the fear felt inside couldn't be covered.

Inside the counseling shop, Eun-jo was sitting on the sofa hugging her knees.
The memory of that night 10 years ago exploded like a flashback.
Neighbors surrounding the house and pouring out curses.
Voices shouting "Tell the murderer's daughter to come out!".
The helplessness of that time was swallowing her again.

"It's okay. I'm here."

Ji-hyuk held Eun-jo's shoulder firmly.
His touch tied her to the present.

"Don't go out... it's dangerous."

Eun-jo grabbed Ji-hyuk's clothes with trembling hands.
But Ji-hyuk gently loosened her hand.

"The Emperor doesn't hide his people. He makes them walk proudly even in hellfire."

Ji-hyuk opened the firmly closed door wide.

*Creeeak—.*

As the door opened, the protesters' shouts subsided.
When a burly man wearing an old leather jacket walked out with long strides, people flinched at his overwhelming momentum.

"What, who is that man?"
"Must be the witch's lover!"

Someone sneered.
Ji-hyuk stood on the stair landing without a word.
And he took out his police badge from his pocket and held it high.

"I'm Detective Kang Ji-hyuk from Gangbuk Police Station Violent Crimes Unit."

A low but resounding voice echoed in the alley.

"As of this moment, gathering here is illegal. Obstruction of traffic, obstruction of business, and special property damage. I've collected all the video evidence. I'll make sure every single one of you eats prison food, so only those confident stay, the rest get lost."

People murmured at the word detective.
But the mob mentality didn't subside easily.
A skinny man standing at the very front stepped forward huffing.

"So what if you're police? We saw everything, that woman luring kids and making them run away! Aren't you bewitched too?"

"Bring evidence."

Ji-hyuk approached right up to the man's nose.
Murderous eyes. It was the eyes of the 'Hunting Dog' dealing with real criminals.
The man gasped, *hup*.

"If you don't have evidence, shut up. If you know the human tongue is scarier than a knife, you shouldn't wag it carelessly."

At Ji-hyuk's momentum, the protesters started to step back one by one.
It was then.

"Don't be deceived! That man is her puppet!"

Someone shouted from the darkness.
A man wearing a gray hoodie. It was a somewhat familiar silhouette.
At his single gesture, the crowd began to get excited again.
A raw egg flew towards Ji-hyuk.

*Splat.*

The egg hit Ji-hyuk's forehead and burst. Yellow liquid flowed down his face.
But Ji-hyuk didn't wipe it. He glared at the crowd without blinking an eye.

"Throw more. Throw bricks instead of eggs, set a fire too. See if I step back even a single step from here."

He stood enduring like a mountain.
The will to take all the blame and filth with his whole body, and not let a single hair on Eun-jo behind him be touched.
That appearance looked like a martyr hanging on a cross, or a sage seeing the world hanging upside down.
Watching that scene from inside the counseling shop, Eun-jo covered her mouth.
Tears poured out.

*Because of me... why are you...*

Eun-jo thought of **<The Hanged Man>** card.
He wasn't suffering.
Voluntary sacrifice.
Ordeal willingly endured for the loved one.
That was the most noble form of love.

Eun-jo stood up.
She couldn't hide anymore.
She couldn't let her knight bleed alone.
She put strength into her trembling legs and walked out the door.

"Stop it!"

At Eun-jo's shout, gazes focused.
She took out a handkerchief and wiped Ji-hyuk's face.
Her hands trembled, but her eyes were alive like that day 10 years ago.

"I am not a witch. And this person... is not a puppet, but my person."

Eun-jo blocked the front of Ji-hyuk.
A small and frail back, but at that moment, it looked more solid than anything in the world.

"If I have a sin, it's only the sin of surviving the fire 10 years ago. If that's the reason to be stoned... I will take it. But don't touch my people."

Silence.
The crowd was also overwhelmed.
At the confident attitude of the woman who was being humiliated as a witch, the madness seemed to wash away.

*Wee-woo- wee-woo- wee-woo-.*

Police car sirens were heard from afar.
The backup Ji-hyuk reported finally arrived.
The protesters started to scatter in panic.
The alley became empty in an instant.
Only broken eggs and trash were rolling on the ground.

"Haa..."

Ji-hyuk staggered as his tension released.

"Detective!"

Eun-jo supported him.
Ji-hyuk smirked and looked at Eun-jo with an egg-covered face.

"See? I told you I protect my territory."

"Idiot..."

Eun-jo buried her face in Ji-hyuk's chest and burst into tears.
Ji-hyuk patted Eun-jo's back with messy hands.
The world was still cold to them, but only each other's body temperature was hot.

But the storm wasn't over.
In the scattering crowd, the man in the gray hoodie was glaring at them.
The man took out a black Tarot card from his bosom and kissed it.
**<Death>.**

"The witch hunt is over. Now it's time to hold a real funeral."

The man lit a lighter.
*Fwoosh.*
The deepest and darkest night was approaching.

(End of Episode 19)
