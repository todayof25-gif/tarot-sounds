After the 'Arsonist of the Heart' incident, a subtle tension hung over the counseling shop.
Ji-hyuk now stopped by the shop every night.
It was under the pretext of 'surveillance', but in reality, it was to guard Eun-jo's side.
Because they didn't know when the Shadow's threat would strike again.

"The guest is quite late today."

Midnight, the grandfather clock struck, but the door didn't open.
It was then, as Ji-hyuk yawned and checked the date on the calendar.

*Jingle—.*

With a cautious bell sound, the door opened.
The person who entered was a woman wearing a black trench coat, a wide-brimmed hat, and even sunglasses covering half her face.
She looked like a spy carrying out a 007 mission.

"Excuse me... is this the place rumors say..."

The voice was familiar.
Ji-hyuk frowned. A voice he had heard a lot somewhere, on the news or in a drama.

"Welcome. This is <Midnight Counseling>."

Eun-jo welcomed the guest calmly as always.
The woman looked around, then flinched and stepped back upon seeing Ji-hyuk.

"There's... a person here?"

"Ah, he is my partner. Confidentiality is guaranteed, so you can rest assured."

At Eun-jo's words, the woman hesitated for a moment before collapsing onto the sofa.
And with trembling hands, she took off her sunglasses and hat.

"......!"

Ji-hyuk almost let out an exclamation without realizing it.
Lee Yu-na.
A top actress so famous there was a saying that if you didn't know her in Korea, you were a spy.
Why did she, who receives flashy spotlights, come to a place like this at this late hour?

"I'll give you some tea first. Please calm down."

Sweet yet bitter orange scent rose from the teacup Eun-jo offered.
Yu-na cupped the teacup with both hands. Her hands were shaking severely.

"What brings you here?"

At Eun-jo's gentle question, Yu-na bit her lip.
She took out a crumpled letter from her handbag and placed it on the table.
On the letter, a horrifying phrase was written in red letters.

[Take off the mask, you liar. Before I expose your real face to the world.]

"Is it a stalker?"

Ji-hyuk asked. Yu-na nodded.

"It started a month ago. My house, filming set, even the dermatology clinic I go to... he knows all my movements. At first, I thought it was just a hater, but the level is getting..."

Yu-na couldn't continue and sobbed.
She, who looked confident and haughty on screen, struck Ji-hyuk as a terrified child right now.

"Did you report it to the police?"

"I can't. If rumors spread... my image is over. It's already hard enough these days with 'character controversy' and stuff."

Ji-hyuk clicked his tongue.
The sorrow of the celebrity profession. The reality of having to hide even when you become a victim.

"Looking at the content of the letter, it seems like someone who knows me very well. Who I ate with, even what pajamas I wore to sleep last night..."

Eun-jo picked up the letter.
Her fingertips read the texture of the paper.

"The person who sent this letter... loves you very much."

"What? Love? He's making such terrible threats?"

"There is a fine line between love and hate. This person loves you so much that he wants to monopolize you. He is under the illusion that only he knows your 'real self'."

Eun-jo spread the Tarot cards.
Yu-na picked one with trembling hands.
**<The Lovers>.**
But it was **Reversed**.

"It's the Lovers card. But it's flipped. This means discord, wrong choices, and a 'broken relationship'."

"Broken relationship...?"

"The culprit is not far away. Someone who knows your every move. Someone who can see behind your mask, even your most intimate self."

Yu-na's face turned pale.

"Don't tell me..."

Then, Yu-na's cell phone rang.
The caller's name appeared on the screen.
[Manager Oppa].

"D... Don't pick up."

Ji-hyuk warned, but Yu-na pressed the call button as if possessed.

"Hello? Oppa?"

[...]

Only silence flowed over the receiver.
No, listening closely, breathing sounds could be heard.
Rough, excited breathing.

[Yu-na, where are you right now? Did you hide somewhere wearing that mask again?]

It was a modulated voice, but Yu-na recognized it at once.
Her pupils dilated with fear.

"Oppa...?"

[I told you. You are nothing without me. Do you think the public will love you if they know your real self? No, they will despise you.]

*Click.*
The call ended.
Yu-na screamed and threw her phone.

"Chief Kim... No, it can't be. Someone I've been with for 10 years..."

The saying 'stabbed in the back by the axe you trusted' fit perfectly.
The closest person was the most dangerous enemy.

"We need to catch him."

Ji-hyuk stood up. The hunting dog's eyes flashed.
This time it might not be the Shadow.
But the method of digging into someone's weakness and tormenting them.
It resembled the Shadow's method too much.

"Eun-jo, keep Yu-na inside. The bastard might be nearby."

Ji-hyuk opened the door and went out.
The alley was thick with darkness.
Under the streetlamp light, someone's shadow stretched long.
The shadow didn't run away even after seeing Ji-hyuk.
Rather, as if waiting, he waved his hand slowly.
As if welcoming him to the masquerade.
The night air cooled down chillingly.

(End of Episode 13)
