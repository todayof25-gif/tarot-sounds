"Lee Myung-chul's daughter... I found her."

Detective Park's voice over the phone was urgent.
Ji-hyuk gripped the steering wheel tight. His vision of the rainy Gangbyeon Expressway was getting blurry.
No matter how much the wipers moved, the fog in his heart didn't clear.

"Where is she. Where is she right now?"

"Don't be surprised. That woman, she changed her name. Lee Eun-jo."

*Screeeech—!*

Ji-hyuk slammed on the brakes. The car behind him honked angrily as it passed.
Ji-hyuk stared blankly at the rain-streaked windshield. His heart pounded in his ears.

*Lee Eun-jo.*

The owner of the counseling shop. The witch who bewitched people with mysterious scents.
To think she was the daughter of the arsonist from the Seobu Market fire case 10 years ago.
Fate had a terribly bad taste.

"Are you sure?"

"Yes. She changed her name when she was adopted by her aunt's family after the fire. She was the sole survivor rescued from the scene that day 10 years ago."

*Survivor.*
A fragment of memory he had forgotten flashed in Ji-hyuk's mind.
10 years ago, Seobu Market swallowed by the flames.
Ji-hyuk, a rookie police officer, broke through the police line and jumped into the burning building.
Because he heard screams that someone was inside.

Acrid smoke, collapsing ceiling, and a small child curling up in the corner.

*Save me... please...*

The child wasn't crying. She was just looking at the burning flames with empty eyes.
As if those flames were everything in the world.
When Ji-hyuk carried the child out, she didn't let go of his clothes.
The smell of burning and blood he smelled then, and a strangely sweet... lavender scent.

"Senior? Hello?"

"......Got it."

Ji-hyuk hung up and turned the car around. There was only one destination. Midnight Counseling.

***

Sharp bell sounds rang as he opened the counseling shop door.
Eun-jo was wiping a teacup behind the counter. She didn't look surprised.
As if she knew he would come.

"You're here, Detective."

Eun-jo put down the teacup and spoke calmly.
Her eyes were like a deep lake. Ji-hyuk strode over and put his hands on the counter.

"Did you know? That I would find out. That you are... Lee Myung-chul's daughter."

Eun-jo's fingertips trembled slightly, but she didn't avoid his gaze.

"I knew you would find out eventually. Because you are like a hunting dog, Detective."

"Why did you hide it."

"Would it have changed anything if I told you? Daughter of an arsonist. Child of a murderer. Witch. These are the labels that have followed me for 10 years. You would have treated me like a criminal or pitied me as a poor victim."

"Is that the reason you exercised your right to remain silent?"

"No."

Eun-jo took out a charred aluminum box from the drawer.
Inside the box was a single partially burnt card.
**<The Tower>.**
A tower collapsing from a lightning strike and people falling. A card symbolizing destruction and sudden change.

"That night, my father gave me this card. He said everything has to be destroyed to start anew. That we have to burn down the false tower and find the real truth..."

"So he set the fire? Your father?"

"People believe that. But... my father didn't set the fire."

Ji-hyuk's eyes wavered. Claiming innocence in an already closed case.

"Do you have evidence?"

"My memory. And... the smell. That night, there wasn't a smell of gasoline in the market. A very unique scent. Sweet yet disgusting... the scent of rotting lilies."

Ji-hyuk's brow furrowed.
What he smelled at the scene 10 years ago was the scent of lavender. But Eun-jo said it was lilies.
Is it a difference in memory, or is another truth hidden?

"I followed the owner of that scent here. Because that bastard is still alive. Hiding in the shadows, preparing to light another flame."

Eun-jo's words were full of conviction.
The shadow. The target she fears but chases.
Ji-hyuk was confused. Should he believe her, or is it the delusion of an arsonist's daughter?
Then, he saw a scar revealed under Eun-jo's sleeve.
Burn marks. An indelible brand left by the flames licking past.

In an instant, something snapped inside Ji-hyuk's chest.
It was a string of suspicion, and also a string of reason.
He couldn't push her into a corner like before.
Because she was also a wounded child who survived that hell.

"Lee Eun-jo."

Ji-hyuk's voice softened.

"I don't believe everything you say. But... that shadow bastard. If the real culprit is someone else and is targeting you."

Ji-hyuk took out the **<Ace of Swords>** card and put it on the counter.
The sword that cuts the truth.

"I'll catch him. Whoever it is."

Eun-jo's eyes widened. Surprise and relief rippled.

"You are... really reckless, Detective."

"I told you, once I bite, I never let go."

Ji-hyuk smirked. He looked tired and rough, but solid.

*Ding—.*

A text came to Ji-hyuk's phone. Sender 'Unknown'.

[The tower has already started to collapse. Get ready, hunting dog.]

A photo was attached.
Ji-hyuk's back view at the burning Seobu Market 10 years ago.
And someone wearing a mask laughing in the flames.

Ji-hyuk's expression hardened coldly. *The bastard is watching. From very close by.*
Ji-hyuk looked at Eun-jo.
Now they were in the same boat. They had to hold each other's hands to not fall from the burning tower.

"It has begun."

Ji-hyuk recited in a low voice.
Eun-jo silently blew out the scented candle.
In the counseling shop fallen into darkness, only the eyes of the two people were shining bright.
The first spark had just begun to burn.
