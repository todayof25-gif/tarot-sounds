"The Hermit is not one who turns his back on the world. He is a Guide who illuminates the world."

Eun-jo's voice cut through the quiet air of the counseling shop.
The card she flipped was **<The Hermit>**.
It was the same card the Collector had shown, but this time it was different.
**Upright**.
The light from the lantern was brightening the darkness.

"The memory of Ji-hyuk you stole... it's not just 'trauma'. It was a lantern that kept him from losing his way in the darkness."

"Hmph, a lantern? Absurd sophistry."

The Collector sneered and drew one more black card.
His hand movements were dazzling like a magician's.

"Then shall we look at the second card? **The Present**."

The Collector slammed the card onto the table.
On the black card, a heart pierced by swords and bleeding was depicted.
**<Three of Swords>.**

"Your present is full of wounds. Confusion from lost memories, suspicion of people you trusted, and fear of the coming future. These wounds will never heal. Because you have already been poisoned by the 'Shadow'."

It was as the Collector said.
Ji-hyuk was feeling extreme confusion right now.
The sensation of his identity and why he was here fading away.
An emptiness like a hole in a corner of his chest was sickening him.

"Ji-hyuk."

At that moment, Eun-jo placed her hand over the back of Ji-hyuk's hand.
It was warm.
That warmth awakened Ji-hyuk's freezing senses.

"Look at me."

Eun-jo's deep eyes turned to Ji-hyuk.
That gaze was a stronger painkiller than any words.

"Wounds... are proof that you are alive."

Eun-jo flipped her second card.
A picture of a large wheel turning, and angels reading books at the four corners.
**<Wheel of Fortune>.**

"Fate turns around and around. Joy comes after sadness, and healing comes after wounds. The current confusion is not a stop. It's just a cycle to move in a better direction."

Eun-jo's gaze sharpened toward the Collector.

"You tempt him saying you'll stop the pain, but that's like stopping life itself. Because there is no growth without pain. Ji-hyuk hasn't stopped right now. He's just catching his breath to roll the wheel of fortune."

"......You're a smooth-talking parrot."

The Collector's brow furrowed.
A crack appeared in his relaxed expression for the first time.

"Then let's look at the final **Future**. This will decide everything."

The Collector drew the last card.
Just as he was about to flip the card with a smile of conversion...

"Wait."

Ji-hyuk spoke.
His voice was cracked, but his eyes were coming back to life clearly.
Eun-jo's warmth and her words filled the empty space of memory with new will.

"I'll draw it."

"What?"

The Collector was flustered.

"It's my fate. I won't let a bastard like you decide it as you please."

Ji-hyuk reached out his trembling hand.
There was no memory. But the sensation remained.
The heat he felt in the flames 10 years ago, and the warmth of the small hand that held him.
That feeling was transmitted through his fingertips.

Ji-hyuk drew a card from Eun-jo's deck.
And he flipped it without hesitation.

A picture of the Goddess of Justice holding scales and a sword in the sky.
**<Justice>.**

"Justice...?"

The Collector's face distorted.

"Balance and truth. Cause and effect."

Eun-jo recited low.

"What is lost always returns to its rightful place. That is the law of the universe. The memories you stole, and the sins you committed."

*Clang-!*

At that moment, the glass of the pocket watch in the Collector's pocket shattered.
The watch stopped, and dark red smoke rose from it.
The smoke flowed towards Ji-hyuk.

"Ugh...!"

Ji-hyuk clutched his head.
A wave of memories rushing in.
Hot flames, collapsing pillars, and the voice of a child crying for help.
That terrible yet precious pain stuck in his brain again.

"Haa... Haa..."

Ji-hyuk raised his head, gasping for breath.
The light had returned to his eyes.
The emptiness disappeared, and the hunting dog's fierce eyes were burning again.

"I remember. That night, it wasn't that I couldn't save you."

Ji-hyuk said, looking at Eun-jo.

"That day I... carried you out even while getting burned to save you. What I wanted to forget wasn't guilt, but that scorching pain."

Ji-hyuk's gaze pinned the Collector.

"Thanks to you, it's become clear. My scars are medals."

"T... This is impossible. The Dark Arcana broken!"

The Collector stepped backward.
He was no longer a mysterious phantom thief.
He looked shabby like a third-rate swindler whose magic trick had been exposed.

"You lost. Get lost."

Ji-hyuk pointed to the door.
The Collector ground his teeth and pressed down his hat.

"Just you wait... The Shadow won't let this slide."

"Tell him. Come anytime. I'll burn him this time."

The Collector disappeared with black smoke.
Silence returned to the counseling shop.

"Are you okay?"

Eun-jo wiped the cold sweat on Ji-hyuk's forehead.
Her touch was cool yet affectionate.

"Thanks to you. You were right."

Ji-hyuk smirked.
His head was still throbbing, but his heart was lighter than ever.

"No growth without pain... sounds pretty plausible, huh?"

"I didn't make it up. Nietzsche said it."

"Ah, really? Geez, I have quite a philosophical detective partner."

Ji-hyuk took a sip of the warm tea Eun-jo brewed.
Fragrant lavender scent lingered at the tip of his nose.
It felt like his heart, which had been scattered like broken glass shards, was gathering into one again.

"Welcome back, Detective."

Eun-jo clinked her glass against his.
*Clink-* The clear sound woke the stillness of the night.

The shadows of the two overlapped into one under the candlelight.
The Wheel of Fortune began to turn again.
This time not alone, but together.

(End of Episode 16)
