"Mmm..."

When Ji-hyuk opened his eyes, the first thing he felt was an unfamiliar freshness.

His head was clear.
It was a lightness as if he had been reborn, as if all the toxins in his body had been washed away.

"What time is it?"

Ji-hyuk checked his phone out of habit and was startled.

[2:30 PM]

"Crazy... I slept for 12 hours?"

He couldn't believe it.
Sleeping for two hours a day was considered a lot for him.
Even then, he would usually wake up soaked in cold sweat from nightmares.

But today, he didn't even dream.
It was a perfect, deep sleep.

Ji-hyuk sat blankly on the bed, retracing his memories of the previous night.
The smell of rain, the purple sign, the woman with the strange atmosphere, and...

"Starlight Tea."

As soon as he drank that tea, he had fallen asleep as if he had passed out.
That was where his memory ended.
When he opened his eyes, he was in his bed at home.

"Wait, how did I get home from there?"

Ji-hyuk jumped up and went out to the living room.
Wallet, phone, gun. Everything was in its place.
The only thing missing was the ten years of accumulated fatigue.

*Is it drugs?*

His detective instincts kicked in.
A substance that could blow away ten years of insomnia with a single cup couldn't be normal.
If it was an illegal drug...

Ji-hyuk changed his clothes and headed straight to the police station.
His head was full of thoughts about that strange tea shop.

***

"Senior? Why do you look so good today?"

Detective Park, the youngest in the Violent Crimes Unit, asked with round eyes.
It was because his zombie-like senior, who always had dark circles down to his chin, looked like a human being for once.

"Park. Have you ever heard of 'Midnight Counseling'?"

"Counseling? You mean a psychological counseling center?"

"No, it's like a tea house. In the alley behind Seobu Market."

"Behind Seobu Market... Isn't that a redevelopment area full of empty houses?"

Ji-hyuk's brow furrowed.

*Empty houses?*
The vivid sensation from last night was not a hallucination.
It definitely existed.
That scent, and that woman.

Ji-hyuk drove straight to Seobu Market.
The alley in broad daylight looked completely different from the night before.
Trash rolled around, and hideous abandoned houses with 'Scheduled for Demolition' stickers were everywhere.

*It was here.*

Ji-hyuk retraced his memory and stood at the end of the dead-end alley.
But there was no purple sign or antique door.
Instead, on the tightly closed old iron gate, only a dusty piece of paper was attached.

[Business Hours: Midnight - 5 AM]

"......It was real."

Ji-hyuk let out a hollow laugh.
A shop that disguised itself as if it didn't exist during the day, only opening its doors at night.
It was suspicious. Too suspicious.

He waited in his car for darkness to fall.
As the clock hands pointed exactly to midnight, a faint purple light began to leak from inside the alley.

***

*Jingle—.*

"Welcome."

The same scenery as yesterday.
The same scent as yesterday.
And the woman sitting in the same seat as yesterday, Seo Eun-jo.

"Excuse me for yesterday. I fell asleep without even saying goodbye."

Ji-hyuk approached the counter, pretending to be calm.
But inside, he was on high alert.

"It's okay. You needed sleep."

Eun-jo answered calmly while wiping a teacup.

"That tea, what are the ingredients? I could request an analysis from the National Forensic Service."

"Go ahead. It will probably just come out as 'Mixed Herbal Tea'."

Her attitude was so confident that it actually took the wind out of his sails.
Ji-hyuk sighed and sat down.

"Fine. The effect was certain. I slept soundly for the first time in 10 years."

"That's a relief."

"So, how much is the tea? It can't be free."

As Ji-hyuk tried to take out his wallet, Eun-jo waved her hand.

"I don't accept money."

"Then what do you accept?"

"Stories."

Eun-jo rested her chin on her hand and looked at Ji-hyuk.
Her gaze was so deep that Ji-hyuk felt for a moment as if his inner thoughts were being exposed.

"I am a counseling center director. My job is to listen to the worries of those who cannot sleep. Yesterday, you looked so exhausted that I changed the order... but today, we should do it properly."

She spread the Tarot cards she had shown yesterday on the table again.

"Aren't these Tarot cards?"

"That's right. Mirrors of the heart. Here, pick one."

Ji-hyuk picked a card as if possessed.
It was the card he had seen just before falling asleep yesterday.
A clown standing on a cliff.
**<The Fool>.**

"The Fool card, huh?"

Ji-hyuk smirked.
He thought it suited him perfectly, living his life rolling around like a fool throughout his detective career.

"The Fool can go anywhere because he knows nothing. Because he has no fear."

Eun-jo interpreted the card in a low voice.

"You are standing on the edge of a cliff right now. But you are not afraid of falling. Do you know why?"

"Why?"

"Because you've already fallen before."

Ji-hyuk's eyes wavered.

Ten years ago, that day.
The memory of letting go of the girl's hand falling from the burning building rooftop.
That small body falling endlessly away from his hand.
That trauma tightened around his heart.

"You... what do you know?"

Ji-hyuk's voice turned sharp.
But Eun-jo didn't flinch.

"The cards tell me. What's in your backpack isn't travel gear... it's guilt."

It was a bullseye.
Ji-hyuk bit his lip.
This woman is dangerous.
This wasn't just simple psychological counseling.

"It's time to put that burden down. If the Starlight Tea helped you sleep, this card will help you wake up."

Eun-jo pushed the card toward Ji-hyuk.

"This will do for today's tea payment. I've taken a little bit of your heavy guilt."

Ji-hyuk couldn't say anything.
The hand holding the card was trembling slightly.

*Is this woman trying to heal me, or dissect me?*
The only certainty was that he could no longer escape this shop.

(End of Episode 2)
