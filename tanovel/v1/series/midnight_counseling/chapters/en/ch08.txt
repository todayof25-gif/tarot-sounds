"Let's report it to the police."

Ji-hyuk holstered his gun and spoke firmly.
The bullet had only cut through the empty air, hitting nothing.
But the feeling that someone was there was certain.
Cold sweat was still seeping into his palms.

"Report? Report what?"

Eun-jo laughed hollowly while sitting on the floor.
Her tear-streaked face was a mix of terror and self-mockery.

"That a ghost appeared? Or that an invisible guest dropped a book without anyone knowing and disappeared? Detective, are you in your right mind? Would the police believe that?"

"There's graffiti. Murderer, witch... this is clear blackmail."

Ji-hyuk held up the book with the red scribbles like evidence.
The red letters were vivid like blood.

"The culprit might have left fingerprints, or used the CCTV blind spot to enter. If we call forensics, something will come up. Don't you know how good forensic technology is these days?"

"No!"

Eun-jo screamed and roughly snatched the book from Ji-hyuk's hand.
Her eyes were desperate, bordering on tragic.

"If the police get involved... he will run wild with even more excitement. And you will get hurt too, Detective. This is my business. It's karma I have to bear. No one should intervene."

"Karma?"

Ji-hyuk's brow furrowed deep.
 The fire 10 years ago, the surviving child, the witch, and karma.
Puzzle pieces rolled around in his head disorderly, but they hadn't fit into a single picture yet.
But his gut was telling him. All of this is connected.

"Who exactly are you? What do you have to do with that fire 10 years ago? And who is that shadow bastard!"

"......"

Eun-jo kept her mouth shut tight.
Her silence was a stronger affirmation than a yes.
She seemed ready to take the secret to her grave.

"Fine. If you don't want to talk, don't."

Ji-hyuk sighed and put on the coat he had hung on the chair.

"But I have no intention of backing down either. I can't stand seeing a lunatic running wild in my jurisdiction, right in front of my eyes. It's a matter of pride as a detective."

"Detective..."

"If you hate an official investigation, I'll catch him unofficially. I'm a hunting dog that never lets go once I bite."

As Ji-hyuk turned to open the door as if he had made up his mind, Eun-jo called him urgently.

"Wait."

Eun-jo walked unsteadily to the counter.
She rummaged through a drawer for a while and took out a card.
She handed the card to Ji-hyuk with trembling hands.

An image of a giant hand extending from the clouds, firmly grasping a shining sword.
A laurel wreath of victory hung on the tip of the sword, and palm leaves were falling on both sides.
**<Ace of Swords>.**

"Take... this."

"Is it a charm?"

"No. It's a weapon."

Eun-jo wiped her tears and looked straight into Ji-hyuk's eyes.
Her gaze had become much firmer than before.

"This sword has the power to pierce through to the truth. It cuts away lies and illusions and lets you see only the truth. But be careful. It's a double-edged sword... so if you wield it wrong, the user can be cut too."

"Scary."

Ji-hyuk smirked, took the card, and cherished it in his inner pocket.
His chest felt heavy near his heart.

"Don't worry. I'm more used to stabbing others than getting cut. It's an occupational hazard."

***

After Ji-hyuk left, Eun-jo remained alone in the empty counseling shop.
The candle had almost burned down.
She stood by the window and looked at the raindrops on the glass.
Her pale face reflected in the glass overlapped with the face of the young girl crying to be saved in the flames 10 years ago.

"I'm sorry, Detective..."

Hot tears flowed down Eun-jo's cheeks again.

"The person you have to stab with that sword... might be you yourself. That truth might be too painful..."

She knew.
That what the shadow was aiming for wasn't simple revenge.
That bastard was trying to awaken the terrible truth deeply sealed in Ji-hyuk's memory, the truth he was most afraid to face.

***

Next morning, Gangnam Police Station, Violent Crimes Team 2.
Ji-hyuk couldn't sleep a wink all night.
Without the Starlight Tea, the hellish sleepless night had begun again.
His eyes were sunken, and his beard had grown dark.
Like a madman, he was stuck in the reference room digging through the 'Seobu Market Fire Case' file from 10 years ago.

"Senior? What are you doing here? Aren't you off today?"

Detective Park, the youngest, came into the reference room with two cups of instant coffee.
He freaked out seeing Ji-hyuk buried in a dust pit.

"Park, you're here. Give me some coffee."

Ji-hyuk reached out like a zombie.
He was desperate for caffeine.

"No, why are you looking at this old case again? This is already closed."

"I just need to check something."

"Senior, did you perhaps hear that rumor?"

"What rumor?"

"In the Seobu Market redevelopment area. The rumor that ghosts appear every night. They say a man in a raincoat walks around the empty shops flicking a lighter. It's chaos among the homeless."

Ji-hyuk's hand paused.
*Man in a raincoat. Lighter. Fire.*

"And about the fire case 10 years ago... there was that suspect. The man who supposedly set the fire and jumped off the roof to kill himself, Lee Myung-chul."

"Yeah, I know. He had a daughter."

"Yes. There's talk that the daughter was alive. Although they couldn't find the body, false rumors were going around that she became a 'witch' dreaming of revenge hiding somewhere..."

*Witch.*
The red scribble written in the book last night.
[WITCH] [MURDERER].
Everything was falling into place.
Ji-hyuk jumped up. His chair fell backward with a loud noise.

"Park, let's go to Seobu Market right now."

"Huh? Now? I have to write a report..."

"Shut up and follow me. We're going to catch a criminal."

Ji-hyuk touched the Tarot card tucked in his inner pocket.
**<Ace of Swords>.**
The time has come to draw the sword of truth.
Even if that sword ends up aiming at his own heart, he couldn't avoid it.

(End of Episode 8)
