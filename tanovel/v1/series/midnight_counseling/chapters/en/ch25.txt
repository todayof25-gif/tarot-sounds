The water-soaked floor was slippery like an ice rink.
Ji-hyuk barely dodged the knife Jin-oh swung and twisted his arm to break it.
*Crack*, a chilling sound was heard, but Jin-oh didn't seem to feel pain.
The strength of a madman exceeded imagination. Jin-oh dangled his dislocated arm and strangled Ji-hyuk with his remaining hand.

"Die! If you are gone, Eun-jo can be saved! I will burn you!"

Jin-oh's grip grew stronger.
Ji-hyuk's vision blurred. Breath was choked.
Cold water streams pouring from the sprinklers hit his face, making it hard to even open his eyes.
At this rate, he would be done for. It was the moment his consciousness was fading.

"Stop it, Oppa!"

Eun-jo's scream-like shout rang out piercing through the sound of water.
In her hand was not a knife or weapon, but a Tarot card.
It was the card that fell from Ji-hyuk's pocket.
A man hanging upside down. **<The Hanged Man>**.

"Look at this. It's the card you sent. Look straight!"

Eun-jo thrust the card in front of Jin-oh's eyes.
Jin-oh's gaze wavered. Confusion appeared in his eyes glistening with madness. Taking that opportunity, Ji-hyuk pushed him away with all his might.
Jin-oh stumbled and stepped back.

"Y... Yes. The Hanged Man. One who endures pain and gains enlightenment. That is me. A savior sacrificing for you!"

Jin-oh shouted, panting.

"No."

Eun-jo shook her head coldly.

"This is not Upright. It's **Reversed**. Just like the world you stand in is upside down."

"What?"

"Do you know what it means when **<The Hanged Man>** is reversed? 'Meaningless sacrifice', 'vain effort', 'stubbornness'. Your actions are not salvation. It's just self-satisfaction."

Eun-jo's voice was trembling as if wet with rain, but her eyes were not shaking.
It was the moment as a counselor she shattered the client's illusion and made him face it.

"You misunderstood Mother's words as burning other people. What Mother told you to burn wasn't 'people', but the 'madness in your heart'. You saved no one 10 years ago, nor now. You only made everyone unhappy."

"No... it can't be... I received a revelation..."

Jin-oh stepped backward. His firm belief began to crack.
He clutched his head and made a beast-like crying sound.
It was the moment the tower of delusion built over 10 years collapsed.

"Aaaargh! Shut up! It's all lies!"

Jin-oh screamed and ran out the open front door.
Ji-hyuk tried to get up to chase him, but collapsed on the floor due to leg pain.
It wasn't broken, but the ligament seemed severely torn.

"Ji-hyuk!"

Eun-jo ran and supported the fallen Ji-hyuk.
Red handprints remained vivid on Ji-hyuk's neck.

"Are you okay? Speak to me! Can you breathe?"

"Cough... Hack... I lost... the bastard. Damn it."

Ji-hyuk slammed the water-soaked floor as if frustrated.
But Eun-jo shook her head and hugged Ji-hyuk tight.

"It's okay. You are alive. That's enough."

Eun-jo's body was trembling uncontrollably. It was trembling mixed with fear and relief.
Ji-hyuk carefully patted her back over her wet clothes.
In the dark living room, under the pouring water streams, the two relied on each other's body temperature.
It was cold and damp, but strangely, their hearts were hot.

***

After the commotion ended, Ji-hyuk's officetel became literally a battlefield.
Police who arrived late cleared the scene and left.
Fortunately, the fire was extinguished early, but the house became a sea of water.

Ji-hyuk returned after receiving simple treatment at the emergency room.
Wearing a neck brace and bandages wrapped around his leg.
Arriving home, Eun-jo was squatting in front of the door where the police line was set up.
It seemed she refused the police's offer to send her to a temporary shelter as she had nowhere to go.

"Why aren't you going inside."

"I don't know the password... The door lock is broken."

Eun-jo smiled awkwardly and stood up.
Ji-hyuk sighed and took out the key to open the door.

"Come in. It's a mess, but it's our house."

"Our... house?"

"Yeah. You and I, we're in the same boat now."

The house was flooded and smelled of half-burnt furniture, but the two sat side by side on the wet sofa without caring.
Ji-hyuk handed a warm canned coffee he bought at the convenience store to Eun-jo.

"I'm sorry. You got hurt because of me..."

Eun-jo lowered her head and just fiddled with the canned coffee.

"It's not your fault. And knowing the culprit's identity is a big harvest. Lee Jin-oh... He's the guy from 10 years ago, right?"

"...Yes. He was an older brother who lived next door. He often came to our house to avoid his parents' abuse... Mother took care of him like a real son. But... I think he was exposed to wrong doctrines for too long. He brainwashed himself to avoid the pain of abuse."

Eun-jo's voice was wet.
The wound of being betrayed by someone she trusted like family was deeper than expected.

"Is he right? Did I make Mother die because I was afraid of fire?"

Eun-jo muttered self-deprecatingly.
Then Ji-hyuk wrapped his arm around Eun-jo's shoulder with his bandaged hand and pulled her towards him.

"No. It's the bastard's bullshit. Don't listen. Your mother sacrificed herself to save you. That is love, not your sin. If there is a sin, it lies with that crazy bastard who distorted that love."

Ji-hyuk's firm voice dug deep into Eun-jo's heart.
Eun-jo leaned on Ji-hyuk's shoulder and burst into tears she had held back.
Ji-hyuk hugged her silently. His shirt became wet with tears again.

Outside the window, the rain stopped and dawn was breaking.
Hoping Eun-jo's tears would wash away the old guilt just as the rain that fell all night washed away the city's dust.
Ji-hyuk flipped over the wet Tarot card on the table.
**<Judgement>**.
The trumpet sound announcing resurrection and salvation.

"Look at this. The time for judgment has come."

Ji-hyuk recited low.
Lee Jin-oh ran away, but he cannot hide forever.
The bastard will return more violent like a wounded beast.
But he wasn't afraid. Because now they weren't alone.

Ji-hyuk pledged looking at Eun-jo in his arms.
Whatever happens, this time he will protect her.
New resolution burned like dawn light in his eyes.

(End of Episode 25)
