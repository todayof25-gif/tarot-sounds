Old factory district in Seongsu-dong.
A small sign reading 'Arcana Art' was visible between red brick buildings.
Ji-hyuk checked his pistol and carefully opened the door.

*Jingle—.*

"Welcome. Did you make a reservation?"

An old man wearing a white gown was inspecting cards with a magnifying glass.
Inside the shop, thousands of Tarot cards filled the walls.
The smell of ink and the unique smell of paper coating vibrated.

"Police. I came to ask something."

Ji-hyuk showed his badge and held out the evidence bag.
The **<The Tower>** card found at the fire scene, charred black but not burnt.
The old man adjusted his magnifying glass and examined the card closely.

"Huh, this... It is indeed made by me. Special order with 'Egyptian Blue' pigment and fluorine coating treatment. It was a very picky job."

"Who ordered it?"

"Well, I don't know the face because we only traded non-face-to-face. He always sent items via quick service and deposited money with cryptocurrency. He used the nickname L.O."

"L.O...?"

"Lost One. It's like a slang used in cults or pseudo-religions to call their believers. Saying lost souls or whatever."

Ji-hyuk muttered. Lost One.
It wasn't just a nickname for ordering items. It was a twisted identity defining himself.
The old man rummaged through a dusty ledger and showed the delivery address list.

"The address was different every time. Unmanned lockers at subway stations, under park benches, even next to trash cans... He was a very elusive friend. But once, the quick service driver returned it by mistake, and the return address written then was..."

The old man's wrinkled finger pointed to the bottom row of the ledger.

[12-4 Murim-ri, Soheul-eup, Pocheon-si, Gyeonggi-do, Former Hope Nursing Home]

"Hope Nursing Home? Isn't that place closed 10 years ago? Where the residents died in a fire accident..."

"That's right. It's a haunted house rumored to have ghosts. Ordering Tarot cards from there, I thought at first a ghost would wail."

Ji-hyuk's spine chilled.
Closed nursing home, fire, and the intersection of 10 years.
All clues pointed there. The bastard was hiding in that place of death.

Ji-hyuk immediately called the Violent Crimes Team Leader.

"Team leader, found the suspect's hideout. It's the abandoned building of 'Hope Nursing Home' in Pocheon. Please send backup."

- "What? Hey, Kang Ji-hyuk! Don't go alone! It's dangerous there! It's a restricted area at night!"

"No time. I have to catch him before he notices and runs away. I'll go first and block the retreat."

Ji-hyuk hung up leaving the team leader's shouting behind and got into his car.
And after hesitating, he called Eun-jo.

"Uh, Eun-jo. I think I'll be late. Eat first and sleep."

- "Is something wrong? Why is your voice like that? Breathing is rough too..."

Eun-jo's voice trembled. She intuitively sensed an ominous vibe.

"No. I caught the culprit's tail. It'll be over soon. Lock the door tight, and never open it to anyone."

- "Ji-hyuk, wait! The card I drew today... was **<Page of Swords>** reversed. It's a trap. You shouldn't go!"

"I'll listen later. Hanging up."

Ji-hyuk hung up feeling guilty.
Eun-jo's warning lingered in his ears, but now there was no time to hesitate.
Whatever the card's message was, he had to rush in like a hunting dog without caring about fire or water.

Ji-hyuk's car sped madly towards Pocheon.
It started to rain.
It was pouring rain. The sound of wipers wiping rainwater and creaking scratched his nerves.
Like that night 10 years ago, it was a heavy rain that seemed to swallow the world.

Hope Nursing Home located deep in the foothills of Pocheon exuded a spooky atmosphere.
Weeds grew as tall as a person blocking the entrance, and broken windows were gaping open like black mouths.
It looked like the skeleton of a rotting giant.

Ji-hyuk entered the building holding a pistol and a lantern.
Old wheelchairs and torn charts were rolling on the floor. The smell of wet mold vibrated.
Passing through the dusty hallway, he sensed a presence somewhere.

*Scrape— scrape—.*

The sound of someone dragging something on the floor.
Ji-hyuk held his breath and aimed the muzzle toward the sound.
His heart beat like it would burst.
The sound was coming from the end of the stairs leading to the basement.
Basement level 1. Boiler room.
The heavy iron door was slightly open, and faint red light leaked through the crack.

'Bingo.'

Ji-hyuk kicked the door open and entered.

"Freeze! Police!"

But what was there was not a person.
Photos covering the wall.
Eun-jo's photos, Ji-hyuk's photos, and photos of the burning counseling shop.
Hundreds of candid photos were plastering the wall. It was a stalker's altar made of obsession and madness.
And experimental tools were scattered on one side of the desk. Dried hallucinogenic mushrooms, poisonous herbs, and 'specially made scented candles'.
That strange scent Ji-hyuk smelled in Episode 7, the identity of the incense that made people helpless and see hallucinations was exactly this.

On the floor, special shoes with sponges added to the soles were also placed. Tools to kill footsteps and leave no traces.
The Shadow wasn't a ghost. He was a criminal using thoroughly prepared tricks and drugs.
And a laptop was turned on on the desk in the middle of the room.
Real-time CCTV footage was being transmitted on the screen.

Ji-hyuk froze on the spot looking at the screen.
That was... his officetel living room.
Eun-jo sitting on the sofa hugging her knees and waiting for a call with an anxious expression was clearly visible.

"T... This bastard..."

The bastard knew from the beginning.
That Ji-hyuk would come here, and that Eun-jo would remain alone.
This place wasn't a hideout, but an elaborate trap to lure Ji-hyuk.
At that moment, letters were typed one by one in the chat window on the laptop screen.

[L.O : A hunting dog should guard the house. You came out for a walk too far.]

At the same time, a dull sound like a lump of iron was heard behind Ji-hyuk.
*Thud—!*
The iron door of the boiler room closed, and the sound of a lock locking was heard from outside. *Click.*

"Damn it! Open up! Hey you bastard!"

Ji-hyuk kicked the door and rammed it with his shoulder, but the thick fire door didn't budge.
Inside the screen, the sound of the officetel front door lock being unlocked was heard.
*Beep- beep- beep- tiriring.*
Eun-jo startled and looking at the front door was seen.

[L.O : Don't worry. I'll purify your partner. This time very 'perfectly'.]

"No! Eun-jo! Run away! Don't open the door!"

Ji-hyuk screamed toward the screen until his throat burst. But that sound couldn't reach.
Into the screen, a shadow wearing a black raincoat was entering slowly, very slowly.
Holding a Tarot card symbolizing death in one hand, and a lighter with a blue flame flickering in the other.
