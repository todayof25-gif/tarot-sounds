Everything collapsed.
After Ji-hyuk and Eun-jo barely escaped, the counseling shop building collapsed with a roar.
Old wooden pillars creaked and snapped like screams, and the roof poured down.
The 'Midnight Counseling' shop, the small castle that had protected Eun-jo for 10 years, where the scent of warm tea and mysterious cards resided, turned into a pile of black ashes overnight.

"......"

Eun-jo stared blankly at the burning building, covered in a blanket given by a rescue worker.
Fire trucks sprayed streams of water endlessly, but the building, already reduced to a skeleton, was beyond recovery.
Acrid smoke covered the night sky completely. The smell of burning pierced the nose instead of lavender scent.
Red flames flickered in her hollow eyes.
Home, workplace, and precious memories all disappeared.
It felt like she had returned to that cold and scary night 10 years ago when nothing was left.

"It's all over now..."

Eun-jo's voice was cracked. Was it because she inhaled too much smoke, or because her heart was burnt?
It was despair.
Then, a hand blackened with soot and blood grabbed Eun-jo's hand tight.
A rough but warm hand.

"I told you it's not over."

Ji-hyuk's voice was firm.
He helped the dazed Eun-jo stand up and put her in the passenger seat of his car.

"Where are we going?"

Eun-jo asked in a weak voice.
There was nowhere to go. She had no family, no friends, no home to return to.
Feeling of being left alone in the world.

"My house."

"Pardon?"

"You have nowhere to go. You can't go to a sauna in this state, and hotels or motels are dangerous. The bastard might be watching nearby."

"But..."

"Don't argue and follow me. You said you live in my 'territory'. My house is my territory too."

Ji-hyuk stepped on the accelerator as if to prevent further argument.
The car sped through the dawn road.
Watching the fire scene receding in the rearview mirror, Eun-jo swallowed tears quietly.
Her face reflected in the window was unfamiliar. Covered in soot, messed up hair. Shabby like the fall of a witch.

Ji-hyuk's officetel, arrived after a 30-minute drive, was... bleak as expected.
Gray wallpaper, windows covered with blackout curtains.
The furniture was just a lone bed and an old leather sofa.
Unopened bills were piled up on the dining table, and opening the refrigerator, it was full of only water bottles.
A space only for sleeping, without any smell of human living.
But to Eun-jo, it felt safer than any fancy hotel.
At least here was a 'Hunting Dog' protecting her.

"Wash and come out. Hot water works well."

Ji-hyuk took out the smallest t-shirt and track pants from his closet and threw them to her.
Entering the bathroom, Eun-jo smiled bitterly looking at the mirror.
Ji-hyuk's clothes were too big for her.
The t-shirt came down to her knees like a dress, and she had to roll up the sleeves several times.
She looked like a child wearing adult clothes.
But the smell of fabric softener from the clothes was strangely reassuring.

When Eun-jo washed and came out, Ji-hyuk had taken out a first-aid kit and was waiting.
He silently sat Eun-jo on the sofa and examined her wounds.
Arms and legs scratched by broken glass, and bruises from escaping.
Ji-hyuk soaked a cotton ball with disinfectant and carefully wiped the wounds.
Every time Eun-jo frowned at the stinging, his hand paused.
It was fascinating that those ignorant hands that beat up criminals could move so delicately.

"It'll leave scars. On such tender skin."

Ji-hyuk muttered as if upset.
His breath was close enough to touch her skin.

"It's okay. Scars are... proof of survival."

Eun-jo returned the words Ji-hyuk had said and smiled faintly.
Ji-hyuk stared at her blankly for a moment.
Wet hair, wearing his loose clothes, and eyes hurt but not collapsed.
Something surged up in his chest.
He wanted to protect her. So she wouldn't break, wouldn't get hurt anymore.

"......"

Ji-hyuk coughed and hurriedly turned his gaze away.

"You must be tired, sleep. You can use the bed. I'll sleep on the sofa."

"It'll be uncomfortable. And the owner should use the bed."

"I'm used to it. I sleep in the car too when on a stakeout. And you are a patient right now. Listen to the doctor."

Ji-hyuk forcibly pushed Eun-jo into the bedroom and closed the door.
Eun-jo lay carefully on the unfamiliar bed.
Ji-hyuk's smell came from the pillow. Cool but heavy musk scent, and a faint smell of cigarettes.
It wasn't the acrid smoke smell of 10 years ago, but the body odor of a living person.
As that smell came deep into her lungs, tension released and uncontrollable drowsiness poured down.
The fact that she wasn't alone, even though she lost everything, led her to deep sleep.

Ji-hyuk, lying on the living room sofa, couldn't sleep.
He took out a Tarot card from his pocket.
It was the only card he picked up unburned from the debris of the burning counseling shop.
Specially coated material. Smooth surface without even soot.
A tower collapsing from lightning strike, and falling people.
**<The Tower>.**

"It must collapse to be built anew... huh."

Ji-hyuk muttered while fiddling with the card.
He remembered the interpretation Eun-jo gave. The Tower card means destruction and disaster, but at the same time, it means the moment when things built on a wrong foundation collapse and the truth is revealed.
The counseling shop collapsed. The refuge where Eun-jo hid, and the trauma Ji-hyuk ignored collapsed together.
Now all that remained was to start over on the ruins.

*Shadow. What you broke wasn't a building. It was our shackles.*

Ji-hyuk's eyes shone brightly in the darkness.
He jumped up and turned on his laptop.
The hunting dog had no time to rest.
This <Tower> card the bastard left on purpose.
Looking closely, there was a very minute mark in the corner of the back of the card. A mark carved by scratching with something like a needle, not printed by a machine.
An eye-shaped symbol.

"Found it. Your tail."

Dawn was breaking.
Blue light coming through the window crack shone on Ji-hyuk's face.
The real chase was from now on.

(End of Episode 21)
