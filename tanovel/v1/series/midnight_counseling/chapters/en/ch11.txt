5 AM.
When Ji-hyuk left the counseling shop, the rain that had been pouring all night had thinned.
The cold dawn air cooled his heated head, but the ball of fire in his chest remained.

"A survivor of the fire 10 years ago..."

Ji-hyuk got into his car and grabbed the steering wheel.
The **<Tower>** card Eun-jo showed him, and her scar. And her confession.
All of it swirled in Ji-hyuk's mind.
She was a victim, and at the same time, the only witness.
And now...

"An accomplice, huh."

Ji-hyuk let out a hollow laugh.
A detective and a suspect's daughter joining hands to chase an invisible criminal.
If the Inspection Division at the National Police Agency knew, they would collapse holding the back of their necks.
But he had no regrets.
Anyway, since he got the nickname 'Hunting Dog', he had lived like a dog off its leash.

Arriving home, Ji-hyuk collapsed onto his bed without washing.
His body felt heavy as a thousand pounds, but his mind was alert.
Normally, he would never have been able to sleep in this state.
But today was different.
Even without drinking Starlight Tea, his consciousness blurred as if sinking into a deep bog as soon as he closed his eyes.
Like someone firmly inviting him into a world of dreams.

***

*Crack, crack.*
It was a familiar sound.
The sound of wood burning and concrete cracking.
And the smell of acrid smoke piercing his nose.

*That dream again.*

Ji-hyuk frowned and opened his eyes.
The dream of the fire scene that had tormented him for 10 years.
But this time it was different.
Unlike before, where he only watched the burning building from a third-person perspective, this time he was standing right in the middle of the flames.

It was hot.
He vividly felt the heat that seemed to melt his skin.
Broken glass shards and collapsed debris were stepped on under his feet.

"Save me!"

A scream.
Ji-hyuk turned his head reflexively.
At the end of the smoke-filled hallway, under a collapsed pillar, a small child in a school uniform was curled up.
The girl he couldn't save 10 years ago, and whose identity he found out today.
It was Eun-jo as a child.

"Wait! I'm coming!"

Ji-hyuk ran through the flames.
But no matter how much he ran, the distance didn't narrow.
It felt like the hallway was stretching endlessly, like running on a treadmill.

It was then.
From behind the girl, someone walked out of the black smoke.
A man wearing a black raincoat and a white mask.
It was that 'Shadow' who attacked Ji-hyuk in Episode 8.

The man approached the girl while flicking a lighter on and off in his hand.
*Click, click.*
That sound grated on his nerves like a heartbeat.

"Found you, Witch."

The man whispered in a raspy voice and tried to grab the girl's hair.

"No!"

Ji-hyuk shouted.
In that moment, something unbelievable happened.
Someone appeared blocking the girl's front.
A woman with long straight black hair, wearing a purple modern Hanbok.
It was the current Eun-jo.

The adult Eun-jo was blocking the front as if protecting her childhood self.
In her hand was the **<Queen of Swords>** card.
The card shone sharply like a dagger.

"Don't touch her."

Eun-jo's voice was chilly but trembling.
The shadow laughed grotesquely.

"Hoh. You've grown quite a bit. But are you still afraid of burning?"

The shadow thrust the lighter flame toward Eun-jo's face.
The fire flared up.
Eun-jo screamed and stepped back.

*Bang-!*

At that moment, with a roar, the lighter in the shadow's hand flew away.
A pistol was in Ji-hyuk's hand before he knew it.
White smoke rose from the muzzle.
Shooting a gun in a dream. The dream was moving according to his will.

Ji-hyuk strode over and blocked Eun-jo's front.
He could feel Eun-jo's rough breathing behind his back.

"Are you okay?"

"Detective...?"

Eun-jo looked up at him with surprised eyes.
Ji-hyuk's back was reflected in her pupils.
A solid and broad back. It was the very back that carried her and ran on that day 10 years ago.

"I won't lose you this time."

Ji-hyuk growled.
He aimed the gun muzzle at the center of the shadow's mask.

"Who are you."

Instead of answering, the shadow let out a nasty laugh.
And it began to scatter like smoke.
It became thick smoke and wrapped around Ji-hyuk and Eun-jo.

"See you again, Hunting Dog. Because the dream is just beginning."

In the spot where the shadow disappeared, only a single **<The Moon>** card remained.
A dog barking under the moonlight, and a crayfish submerged in water.
An uncertain and chaotic world of the unconscious.

"Detective...!"

The smoke cleared, and the floor collapsed.
Ji-hyuk and Eun-jo fell into endless darkness.
They reached out their hands to each other, but couldn't reach.

"Haa!"

Ji-hyuk gasped for breath and bolted up from the bed.
His whole body was soaked in cold sweat.
His heart beat like it would burst.
It was a dream. But it was too vivid.
The smell of gunpowder, the heat, and even Eun-jo's trembling breath.

*Ding—.*

Then, the cell phone on the bedside table rang.
A text message arrived.

[Sender: Seo Eun-jo]
[...Just now, that dream. Did you see it too, Detective?]

Ji-hyuk stared blankly at the screen.
Goosebumps rose up his arms.
It wasn't a simple dream.
She and he were in the same dream.

Ji-hyuk sent a reply with trembling hands.

[Yes. That bastard... I saw him.]

A moment later, a reply came.

[Come over. Tonight. I have something to tell you. Why we became connected.]

Ji-hyuk looked out the window.
Dawn had not yet broken. The city lights were still twinkling precariously.
He could tell.
Part 2, which will start now, will be a real maze where the boundary between reality and dreams has collapsed.

Maze of Dreams.
The entrance has just opened.

(End of Episode 11)
