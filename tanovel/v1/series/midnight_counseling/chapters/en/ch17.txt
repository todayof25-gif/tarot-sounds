Memories sometimes become thorns that pierce the heart, but only when those thorns are pulled out can new flesh grow.
The memories of 10 years ago that returned to Ji-hyuk were no longer pain.
They were a clear milestone telling him why he had to live and whom he had to protect.

3 AM.
Peaceful silence hung over the counseling shop where the Collector had disappeared with black smoke.

"I'll do it. You rest, Detective. You just went through something big."

Eun-jo reached out to take the broom.
But Ji-hyuk stubbornly gripped the broom handle tight and didn't let go.

"Don't treat me like a patient. Apart from my head ringing a bit, I'm fine. And... you cleaned up the mess inside my head. Let me do this much."

Eun-jo burst into a chuckle at Ji-hyuk's blunt words.
She didn't stop him anymore and watched him sweep silently.
His broad back, which was always rough and aggressive, looked exceptionally reliable today.

When the cleaning was done, Eun-jo brought out warm honey milk.
A sweet scent filled the counseling shop.

"No alcohol? I feel like I'd fall asleep instantly if I downed a straight whiskey."

"Alcohol is prohibited inside the counseling shop. And you need sugar more than alcohol right now, Detective."

"Tch. Stiff as always."

Ji-hyuk took a sip of milk.
As warmth wrapped around his stomach, the taut tension snapped and drowsiness washed over him.
He fiddled with the teacup and opened his mouth slyly.

"Earlier... thank you."

"Pardon?"

"The interpretation of The Hermit. You called him a Guide. Thanks to that, I came to my senses. That I wasn't trapped, but resting for a while."

Ji-hyuk dropped his gaze into the teacup as if embarrassed.
His wavering eyes were reflected on the milk foam.
The tips of his ears were slightly red.
To think that Detective Kang Ji-hyuk, who didn't blink an eye in front of criminals, would be shy at a single word of thanks.
Eun-jo thought that appearance was quite 'human' and cute.

"There's nothing to thank me for. The cards just show things as they are. That's the person you originally are, Ji-hyuk. Someone who can't turn away from people in the darkness and eventually holds their hand. You were like that 10 years ago, and you are like that now."

Eun-jo's voice soaked in softly like warm spring rain.
Ji-hyuk raised his head and looked at Eun-jo.
Her white face shone dreamily under the yellow tungsten lighting.
That terrified, yet wanting-to-live gaze that looked at him in the flames 10 years ago.
Now, that gaze was filled with trust instead of fear.

"You are..."

Ji-hyuk chose his words for a moment. His Adam's apple bobbed slightly.

"I thought you were someone I had to protect. 10 years ago, and even now. Someone weak, hurt, who would break without me. But I realized today. That you were protecting me."

Ji-hyuk's hand crossed the table and covered Eun-jo's hand.
Rough and clumsy hand, a detective's hand with calluses.
But the temperature contained within was hot as lava. That heat traveled through Eun-jo's cold fingertips to her heart.
Eun-jo's heart sank with a thud.

"So from now on, it's the opposite."

"Opposite?"

"I mean I'll be your Guide. So you don't lose your way in the darkness, I'll take the lead and light the way. Just like you did for me."

Ji-hyuk took a Tarot card out of his pocket and placed it on the table.
It was a card he accidentally saw while organizing the deck after the duel with the Collector earlier.
A stern king sitting on a solid stone throne wearing a red cloak.
**<The Emperor>.**

"The Emperor protects his territory absolutely. The power to not be shaken by any enemy invasion and protect his people. That is the Emperor's authority and duty."

Ji-hyuk looked straight into Eun-jo's eyes and spoke like a declaration.

"This counseling shop, and you, Seo Eun-jo. From now on, you are my territory. Shadows, phantom thieves, whatever. If they enter without permission, I'll smash them all."

It was a confession.
A vow much heavier and more reliable than the common words "I love you".
Faint moisture gathered in Eun-jo's eyes.
She didn't pull her hand away, but rather held Ji-hyuk's hand back.

"The Emperor... is a bit of a dictator."

"I tend to manage my turf thoroughly. Any complaints?"

Ji-hyuk grinned. It was a boyish yet confident man's smile.
The moment she saw that smile, Eun-jo knew.
That the tight knot of fear that had been constricting her for 10 years was unraveling.

She wasn't alone.
The strongest and most reliable shield was standing in front of her.

"Then, following the Emperor's orders, please sleep here tonight. Don't doze off while driving."

"Well, if you insist."

Ji-hyuk laid his body on the sofa as if he couldn't win.
Actually, the tension released, and he was too tired to even lift a finger.
The blanket Eun-jo brought smelled faintly of lavender.
It was her scent.
Ji-hyuk closed his eyes smelling that scent.
Even without drinking Starlight Tea, he fell into a deep sleep in less than a minute.

Eun-jo looked down at sleeping Ji-hyuk's face for a long time.
Sleeping peacefully with the wrinkles between his brows smoothed out.
She carefully reached out and swept back his messy bangs.

"Good night, my Emperor."

A small whisper melted into the night air.

But on that peaceful night, the two didn't know.
That the sea before the storm is the calmest.
Outside the counseling shop, countless eyes were watching them in the darkness.

In a dark room, someone's smartphone screen flashed.
A post was uploaded to an online community board.

[Title: Isn't that fortune teller shop behind Seobu Market really creepy?]
[Content: I went there and passed out after drinking some weird tea. My friend says she sees things after getting a card there. That woman... isn't she a witch? She seemed like a complete cult leader. Shouldn't we make a victim's group?]

The view count was rising rapidly.
Malicious comments started to run in real-time.
The poison sprinkled by the Shadow was spreading like an epidemic through people's fingertips.
Invisible arrows were being aimed at the counseling shop.

(End of Episode 17)
