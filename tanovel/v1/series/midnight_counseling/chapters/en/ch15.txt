"Ji-hyuk? Ji-hyuk!"

Eun-jo's urgently calling voice echoed as if heard underwater.
Ji-hyuk stumbled and gripped the table. It felt like his mind had gone completely blank.
Just a moment ago, he was clearly recalling that day 10 years ago, the night the first flame of the serial arson case soared.
But now, it felt like there was a huge hole where that memory used to be.

"Who... are you?"

Ji-hyuk glared at the man in front of him and asked.
The man wearing a fedora smiled brightly and put his pocket watch in his pocket.

"My introduction is late. I am called 'The Collector'. I collect only very precious things."

"Collector? Don't make me laugh. You're a thief. What did you steal from my head!"

Ji-hyuk tried to grab the man by the collar, but his body didn't listen.
His legs gave out and he collapsed onto the spot.

"A thief. That is hurtful. I merely took away your pain. The memory of fire you suffered from so much, the trauma you wanted to forget... I erased it cleanly."

The man's voice was slippery like a snake.

"Think about it. The days you couldn't sleep, suffering from nightmares every night. The guilt for the people you couldn't save in those flames. Now there is no such thing. You are free."

"Bullshit... cut it out..."

Ji-hyuk gritted his teeth. But honestly, undeniable relief washed over him.
The feeling that the heavy stone pressing on his heart every night had disappeared.
When was the last time breathing was this comfortable?

"No, Ji-hyuk! You can't let go of that memory!"

Eun-jo grabbed Ji-hyuk's shoulders and shook him.

"That memory isn't just simple pain. It's a compass that tells you who you are and why you fight! If you lose that... you will lose your way."

Eun-jo's eyes were desperate. She knew.
That losing memories is like gouging out a part of oneself.

"Hoh, as expected, 'The Counselor' knows."

The Collector looked at Eun-jo as if intrigued.

"Memories are maps of the soul. Without a map, a traveler is bound to wander forever. Just as 'The Shadow' desires."

"Shadow..."

Eun-jo bit her lip. It was indeed his doing.
To neutralize Ji-hyuk, he targeted his most powerful weapon and weakness, 'memory'.

"Give it back."

Ji-hyuk raised his staggering body.
His eyes were blurry, but the embers inside hadn't gone out yet.

"It's my pain. It's my share to bear. It's not something a bastard like you can take away as you please!"

"Hahaha! What an interesting person. Usually, people are grateful when I take away their pain."

The Collector giggled.

"Fine. If you want it, I can return it. However, there is a condition."

The Collector placed the pocket watch on the table.
The hands of the watch were turning backward.

"Let's play a game. According to the rules of the counseling shop, we determine the winner with Tarot cards."

"Game?"

"Yes. If you win, I will return your memories. But if I win... I will take all the rest of your memories, even the memories of Eun-jo. You will become an empty shell and be a slave to the Shadow forever."

It was madness. But there was no choice.
If he didn't retrieve his memories, Ji-hyuk would eventually collapse. His identity as a detective, and his will to protect Eun-jo, everything.

"I accept."

As Ji-hyuk tried to accept, Eun-jo blocked his way.

"No! This is a trap. We don't know what tricks he'll use."

"Eun-jo."

Ji-hyuk held Eun-jo's hand gently.

"Trust me. And... trust your cards."

Ji-hyuk's eyes directed at Eun-jo without wavering.
Eun-jo hesitated for a moment, then slowly nodded.
She held Ji-hyuk's callused hand tight. Their body temperatures met.

"Okay. Let's start the game."

Eun-jo glared at the Collector and sat down.
The Tarot deck spread out in her hand with a *shhhk*.
Like a knight drawing a sword, her eyes shone coldly.

"The rules are simple. Draw three cards to read the past, present, and future. The side that pierces through destiny more accurately wins."

The Collector took out a black Tarot card from his bosom.
It wasn't a normal card. It was a cursed card 'Dark Arcana' emitting shadow energy.

"I will go first."

The Collector flipped a black card.
An old man wearing a cloak and holding a lantern was depicted. But the lantern was broken, and the old man was standing on the edge of a cliff.
**<The Hermit>.**

"This is your past. A lone wolf. The times you wandered alone in the darkness trusting no one. The guilt of surviving alone in the flames isolated you from the world."

It was accurate. Ji-hyuk's heart sank with a thud.

"And the present."

The Collector tried to flip the second card.
Then, Eun-jo spoke.

"Stop."

Eun-jo's voice cut through the space coolly.

"Your interpretation is wrong."

"What?"

"That lantern isn't broken. It's just too bright that it's invisible only to your eyes."

Eun-jo smiled looking at Ji-hyuk.
That smile reminded Ji-hyuk of a fragment of memory he had forgotten, no, had been stolen.
The small warmth he felt in the flames that night. The hand someone reached out.

"Ji-hyuk's solitude wasn't for running away. It was time to become stronger, to protect someone. Because The Hermit is not one who turns his back on the world, but a Guide who illuminates the world."

Eun-jo flipped her own card.
It was **<The Hermit>** like the Collector's card, but it was **Upright**.
The lantern was shining brightly, and the old man's expression was gentle like a sage.

"Now, it's my turn."

Eun-jo's counterattack began.

(End of Episode 15)
