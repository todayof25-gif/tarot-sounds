[Location: The last house in the alley behind Seobu Market. Looking for party members to go 'educate' her tonight (1/5)]

Ji-hyuk's finger scrolling down the smartphone screen grew rough.
Dozens of comments were being posted in an instant.
Groundless rumors. Malicious slander.
But people weren't interested in the truth. They just needed a stimulating 'prey' to chew and bite.

"Don't look. You'll spoil your eyes."

Ji-hyuk placed the phone face down on the table.
Eun-jo was calmly wiping a teacup. But her knuckles gripping the cloth had turned white.
She knew it too.
That the Shadow had started to move.
Just like he branded her as an arsonist's daughter 10 years ago, this time he was trying to brand her as a 'Witch'.

"I'm used to it. People believe what they want to believe."

"This is different. They are moving systematically. If we trace the IPs, something will come up."

Ji-hyuk growled.
Then, the door opened and a guest entered.

*Jingle—.*

The bell sound sounded exceptionally precarious.
The woman who entered covered her face completely with a thick mask and a hat.
Shoulders hunched tight. Anxious eyes looking around.
Like a person who committed a sin, she sat in the corner seat of the counseling shop as if hiding.

"Welcome."

When Eun-jo approached, the woman startled and trembled.

"Excuse me... is this that... counseling shop?"

"Yes. It's a place for those whose hearts are heavy."

"I came after hearing rumors. That there is... a witch."

The woman's voice was shaking.
Half fear, half curiosity. No, desperation to grasp even a straw seemed bigger.

"A witch... people do call me that."

Eun-jo smiled bitterly and sat opposite her.

"Witches curse people, but they also save people with herbs. Which one do you want?"

"Save... me. Please save me."

The woman took off her mask.
It was a young face in her early 20s, but her eyes were hollow and her lips were all chapped.
She was an ordinary person who recently got 'doxxed' on social media.
A single photo taken of her sitting in a seat for pregnant women on the subway was the trigger.
Actually, she was in the early stages of pregnancy, but just because her badge wasn't visible, she instantly became a 'clueless Gen Z girl' and received nationwide criticism.

"I get thousands of DMs a day. Die, go die... even my home address was dug up and graffiti is on my gate. I can't go out because I'm scared. Do I... really have to die for this to end?"

The woman sobbed.
Invisible finger murder.
It resembled the situation Eun-jo was going through now chillingly.

Eun-jo silently offered warm 'Chamomile Tea'.
It was a herbal tea with a calming effect.
And she spread the Tarot cards.

"Pick a card. The ones judging you are not them. It's you yourself."

The card the woman picked depicted an angel blowing a trumpet and people rising from coffins.
**<Judgment>.**

"It's the Judgment card. This card means resurrection, awakening, and true liberation."

"The interpretation is... unexpected. I thought I was being punished."

"No. The trumpet sound is a signal announcing the truth. It means to open the coffin lid and come out. If you hide, people will think you are a real sinner."

Eun-jo's eyes became strong.

"Speak confidently. That you did nothing wrong. That you just sat down to protect the life in your belly."

"But... what if no one believes me?"

"I believe you. And here, there is a reliable witness too."

Eun-jo pointed to Ji-hyuk.
Ji-hyuk nodded with a pretend serious expression.

"Cyber defamation, insult, violation of anti-stalking laws. We can sue them all. I'll help you collect evidence, so don't be scared and let's break through head-on."

At Ji-hyuk's firm words, the woman's eyes wavered.
The fact that she wasn't alone. The fact that she had someone on her side.
Just that alone gives a person the strength to stand up again.

The woman left the counseling shop with a much more comfortable face.
But the storm was just beginning.
It was less than a minute after the woman left.

*Crash—!*

With a sharp rupture sound, the counseling shop glass shattered.
Glass shards flew everywhere.

"Ack!"

Eun-jo screamed and collapsed.
What flew in was a red brick. On the brick, it was written in large white paint.

[BURN THE WITCH]

"Eun-jo!"

Ji-hyuk embraced Eun-jo.
Beyond the broken glass, from the dark alley, the sound of a motorcycle exhaust faded away with giggling laughter.
Indiscriminate terror.
They were wolves sent by the Shadow.

"Are you okay? Are you hurt anywhere?"

Ji-hyuk's voice trembled.
Eun-jo nodded, but her gaze was fixed on the brick rolling on the floor.
The Molotov cocktail thrown at her house 10 years ago.
The nightmare of that time overlapped with the brick.

"It has begun..."

Eun-jo muttered.
Finger murder became a stone and flew in.
Now the counseling shop was no longer a safe zone.

Ji-hyuk gritted his teeth and glared out the broken window.
Hot flames flickered in his eyes.

"Look forward to it, you bastards. You messed with the wrong people."

The Emperor's rage was boiling over.
