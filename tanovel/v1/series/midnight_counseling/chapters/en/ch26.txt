4 AM.
Ji-hyuk was glaring at the laptop screen without sleeping a wink all night.
Data sent from the Cyber Bureau was flowing down like a waterfall on the screen. He was chasing Lee Jin-oh's digital traces.
The bastard was elusive like a ghost. The nursing home server was destroyed, and he avoided CCTV on the escape route by only picking blind spots. Even detour connections using burner phones and VPNs, it was the meticulousness of a planned criminal who prepared for 10 years.

*But there is no perfect crime.*

Ji-hyuk put strength into the hand holding the mouse. His throbbing leg screamed, but he ignored it. The bastard is obsessed with the delusion of 'purification'. He will definitely try to complete the ritual.
Then, the door opened quietly.

"Ji-hyuk."

It was Eun-jo. Her face was much calmer. The fear of last night washed away, and a transparent silence unique to a person who passed through deep sadness surrounded her.
In her hand was an old and scorched brown leather notebook.

"You weren't sleeping?"
"I couldn't sleep. What is that?"
"Mother's... counseling log. It was mixed in the bundle of documents you brought from the nursing home yesterday."

The files Ji-hyuk took from the boiler room. Seo Ji-soo's personal notebook was inside it. It yellowed over 10 years, but the words 'Counseling Record' were clear.
Eun-jo opened the notebook with trembling hands.

"Mother... knew everything from the beginning. How dangerous Jin-oh Oppa's condition was. And my pain too."

Mother's handwriting was dense on the page Eun-jo pointed to.

[March 15. Eun-jo had a nightmare again. The child screams every night. The symptoms get worse on days when my husband makes a drunken frenzy. Eun-jo thinks all of it is her fault. My poor daughter... I need to be stronger.]

[April 20. Forming rapport with Jin-oh is not easy. This child is pathologically obsessed with fire. The trauma of abuse has degenerated into a distorted belief called 'purification'. To him, fire is not destruction but salvation. It is a dangerous belief.]

Eun-jo's voice began to tremble little by little. She caught her breath and turned to the next page. It was the most important passage.

[May 29. Today I told Jin-oh. "You live only if you burn everything." The child opened his eyes wide and looked at me. I tried to explain. It didn't mean to set a real fire. It meant you can only live anew if you burn the 'hatred' and 'guilt' in your heart. But... the counseling bell rang, and Jin-oh went out with a strange smile. Did my words reach him? Or did I plant a misunderstanding?]

Tears fell from Eun-jo's eyes and spread on the notebook.
"I was stupid... I didn't know that..."

Eun-jo covered her face with both hands.
The curse-like prophecy that tormented her for 10 years. The guilt of believing she was being punished for fearing fire and disobeying the prophecy. But the truth was the opposite. Mother desperately wanted her daughter and disciple to escape the yoke of pain and burn away terrible self-hatred.

"Mother wanted to save me."

Eun-jo's crying sound filled the officetel. It wasn't sadness. It was the sound of liberation where a huge rock named guilt, 10 years old, crumbled down.
Ji-hyuk approached silently and wrapped his arm around Eun-jo's shoulder. Clumsy comfort was unnecessary. Like waiting for the rain to stop, staying by her side until she dried her heart herself. That was the best he could do.

After a long time, Eun-jo raised her head. Her wet eyes were clear as if washed.
She took out a card inserted between the notebook pages. Mother's keepsake.

**<The High Priestess>.**

A woman wearing blue clothes sitting between black and white pillars, holding a scroll of wisdom. A picture of the moon placed at her feet and pomegranates ripening behind the veil.
A card symbolizing 'intuition', 'unconsciousness', and 'hidden truth'.

"Now I know. What Mother tried to give wasn't foresight."

Eun-jo said quietly, cherishing the card in her chest.

"The eye to see the invisible truth, the wisdom to forgive oneself... That was the real magic. Jin-oh Oppa interpreted it literally and set the fire, but now I know the meaning."

She stood up and approached the window. As she pulled back the blackout curtains, gray dawn light poured in.

"I will burn it too. The fear inside me. And even Oppa's wrong belief."

At that moment, Ji-hyuk's mobile phone on the desk vibrated.
'Cyber Bureau'.

"Yes, this is Kang Ji-hyuk."
- "Found him, Lieutenant! The bastard connected with a specific IP for 10 minutes just now."

Ji-hyuk's eyes turned sharp. The hunting dog's instinct revived.

"Location?"
- "It's unexpected... It's not an overseas detour. It's a PC cafe in Seongbuk-dong, Seoul. The search keywords are 'Seongbuk-dong house fire', '10th anniversary', 'abandoned building demolition schedule'."

Seongbuk-dong.
Ji-hyuk hung up and looked at Eun-jo with a firm expression.

"Seongbuk-dong? Why the hell is he going there..."
"He's going back."

Eun-jo said with a voice full of conviction.

"Seongbuk-dong... is where our house, 'Hope Counseling Center' was 10 years ago. The place where all tragedies began."
"Don't tell me, is he targeting that place again?"
"Yes. To Jin-oh Oppa, that place is a holy ground. It's an altar where he must complete the salvation ritual he failed. Besides... tomorrow is the 10th anniversary."

All puzzles fit together.
Lee Jin-oh tries to complete the ritual he failed in time for the 10th anniversary. The madness to overcome the complex of failing to save Eun-jo by burning it certainly this time.
Ji-hyuk picked up his jacket.

"Get ready to go. Let's go first and wait before the bastard calls."

Ji-hyuk checked his pistol magazine and said.

"Aren't you scared?"

At Ji-hyuk's question, Eun-jo smiled radiantly. It was a sorrowful yet strong smile.

"No. I used to be scared. Fire, and the Shadow. But not anymore. I have a reliable 'Emperor' by my side."

Ji-hyuk coughed as if embarrassed and headed to the front door.
The two got on the elevator. While going down to the underground parking lot, Eun-jo held the **<The High Priestess>** card tight. It seemed like the priestess in the card was whispering to Eun-jo, 'You already know the answer'.

Starting the car, the engine growled like a beast.
Ji-hyuk stepped on the accelerator towards Seongbuk-dong.
Behind the black sedan cutting through the dawn road, the red sun was rising. As if predicting the coming final judgment.
Now the hide-and-seek is over. There was no fear for those who realized the truth.

(End of Episode 26)
