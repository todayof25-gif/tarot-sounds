The office at 4 PM was as quiet and heavy as if underwater.

*Clack, clack.* Only the sound of typing resonated dryly.

Since lunch, Doyun hadn't glanced at Sia even once.

Necessary work instructions came dryly only via messenger text.

[CEO Cha Doyun: Revise the draft to Plan B and resend.]

The period at the end of the sentence felt like a declaration of war: 'I will only talk business with you now.'

'He must be angry. I ignored all his worried words and met Sunbae secretly.'

Sia bit her lip tight. She tasted rusty blood in her mouth.

Even if she had no choice because of Minwoo's threats, it was true that she lied. Her heart throbbed thinking of the betrayal he must have felt.

'I have to fix this. I can't let him misunderstand.'

Around quitting time, as employees left the office one by one, Sia mustered up courage.

In front of the CEO's office, she took a deep breath and knocked.

"CEO, I have something to tell you."

No answer. When she turned the knob, the door opened smoothly.

The room was empty. On the ownerless desk remained only a pile of documents to be signed, cold coffee half-drunk, and his jacket left alone.

He seemed to have stepped out for a moment.

Sia approached to put the revised draft on the desk.

Then, a yellow envelope sticking out from the pile of documents caught her eye.

It was a plain envelope without any label, but the opening was gaped, revealing the contents slightly.

A familiar face. It was her face.

'Huh…?'

Her hand moved on its own. Reason said she shouldn't look, colliding with the instinct to check, but instinct won.

Sia pulled the photos out of the envelope with trembling hands.

*Flutter.*

A dozen photos spilled onto the desk.

Sia’s eyes shook as if an earthquake had hit.

The photos contained Sia’s every move.

Before joining the company, reading a book alone at a cafe.
College days, leaving the club room with Minwoo.
Even a few days ago, eating cup noodles alone in front of a convenience store.

And an A4 paper that was underneath the photos.

[Risk Management Report: Target Lee Sia]

- Analysis of the target's main routes and interpersonal relationships.
- Request to identify past relationship with Kang Minwoo (CEO of Art & Design).
- Special Note: Emotional and prone to being easily swayed by others.

Her mind went blank. Breath got stuck in her throat.

Minwoo's words rang in her ears like tinnitus. *Beeee—*

"Cha Doyun isn't meeting you because he really likes you. It's all planned. You're just a toy."

Her fingertips turned cold.

Doyun’s sweet confessions, "I was drawn to you from the first moment," "It seems like destiny," were those lines actually staged based on thorough data?

Did he see me not as someone to love, but as a 'target' to analyze and conquer?

"What are you doing in someone else's room?"

A chilling voice stabbed her from behind. A coldness that seemed to drop the office temperature instantly.

Startled, Sia dropped the report she was holding to the floor.

Doyun stood at the door. Backlit, his expression wasn't visible, but the aura he emitted was like sharp shards of ice.

"Doyun-ssi… what is all this?"

Sia’s voice trembled. Tears welled up, blurring her vision.

"This is even before I joined. Why did you investigate me? Target, risk… am I a danger element you need to eliminate? Or a game character you need to conquer?"

Doyun walked over slowly. *Click, click.* The sound of his heels stuck in her brain.

He looked down at the records of Sia’s naked past scattered on the floor with indifferent eyes.

There was no sign of panic or apology. It was crueler because he was so confident.

"Because I wanted to know."

His answer was dry.

"It's a natural procedure to check what kind of person I'm keeping by my side, and what risks there are. You don't like my method?"

In truth, Doyun had lost his reason after witnessing Sia meeting Minwoo at lunch earlier.

The woman he trusted lied and met her ex. To hide that sense of betrayal, he deliberately adopted a more defensive attitude. He chose to pretend to be cold rather than revealing his pathetic jealousy.

But to Sia, those words were a confirmation kill.

"Method…? The way you check someone's heart is just a background check?"

Sia shouted out of spite.

"I believed you were destiny. I thought you were sincere… To you, I was just an analysis subject."
"Don't be mistaken."

Doyun smiled coldly.

"Destiny? You aren't being honest enough to wrap it in such romantic words either. Why did you hide meeting Kang Minwoo at lunch today?"

Sia’s eyes widened.

"That was…"
"Because you hide it, I have no choice but to verify. Since I don't know what you're doing behind my back."

His words became a dagger and gouged Sia’s chest.

Trust was broken.

The emotion believed to be love was covered in the muddy water of suspicion and surveillance, becoming unrecognizable.

She felt like she could hear the sound of the glass window cracking. *Crack.*

"…I'm scared."

Sia stepped back. *Drip*, a tear flowed down her cheek.

"I didn't know you were this kind of person. It gives me goosebumps."
"Don't go."

Doyun warned in a low voice, but Sia shook her head.

"No. Now I know. We weren't the 'Right Direction'. It was the wrong direction from the start."

Sia ran out of the office as if fleeing.

Doyun didn't chase her.

Left alone in the empty office, in the dark, he slammed his fist roughly on the desk.

*Bang!*

A picture frame fell to the floor and the glass shattered.

Doyun’s distorted face was reflected on the broken shards.

The self-loathing that he made the person he wanted to protect the most bleed with his own hands was swallowing him up.
