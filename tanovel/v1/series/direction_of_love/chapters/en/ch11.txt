The lingering feeling of their first kiss was intense.

Even after their lips parted, Sia couldn't open her eyes for a while. She wished that moment, where hot breaths tangled and body heat touched, could last forever.

In the air mixed with the scent of rain, cocoa, and Doyun’s body, Sia finally admitted it.

She already liked this man deeply. She had tried to deny it and run away, but the dam of emotions had collapsed.

"…You can't run away now."

Doyun whispered, resting his forehead against hers. His voice was an octave lower than usual, oozing a strange sensuality.

Sia nodded with a flushed face.

"Yes… I won't go."

At that answer, Doyun planted a short, satisfied kiss on her lips. *Smack.* The sound rang explicitly in the living room.

"Ah, seriously, CEO Cha…!"
"Ms. Lee."
"Yes?"
"Let's change how we address each other first. Outside the company."

Doyun intertwined his fingers with hers.

"Call me Oppa. Or Doyun-ssi."
"O-Oppa is a bit…"
"Then Doyun-ssi?"
"That’s also a bit awkward…"
"Practice. I'll make you do it until it sticks."

That night, Sia ended up sleeping at Doyun’s house. Of course, 'wholesomely'. Doyun gave up his bed and slept on the living room sofa, but just the fact that he was across one door made Sia toss and turn all night.

She barely fell asleep at dawn, and when she opened her eyes, it was 7 AM.

Unfamiliar ceiling, unfamiliar bed scent. 'My god, did I really sleep at CEO Cha Doyun’s house?'

Reality hit hard. She kicked the blanket, got up, and slightly opened the door.

Clattering sounds came from the kitchen.

"You're up?"

Doyun turned around wearing an apron. Veins stood out on his forearms where he had rolled up his shirt sleeves. Bathed in the morning sunlight, he looked unrealistically handsome.

"Ah, yes… Good morning."
"Sleep well? I hope it wasn't uncomfortable. Come sit. I prepared a simple breakfast."

Toast, scrambled eggs, and coffee were set on the table.

"You cook too?"
"I've lived alone for a long time, so I do survival cooking."

Sitting opposite him eating breakfast, she felt the illusion of being newlyweds. Doyun spread jam on toast and placed it on Sia’s plate.

"Eat up. It's Monday, so it'll be busy."
"Ah, Monday…"

Sia sighed. Reality rushed in as soon as she thought of work.

"By the way, CEO. Us… dating, we should keep it a secret at the company, right?"

Doyun’s hand stopped at her words. He put down his coffee cup and stared at Sia.

"Why? I want to announce it on the company broadcast right now."
"What?! Absolutely not! Are you crazy?"

Sia waved her hands in horror. "You, the person who keeps public and private matters separate? You said office romance is prohibited."

"I made the rule, so I can abolish it."
"CEO!"
"Okay, okay. Just kidding."

Doyun smirked and wiped a breadcrumb from the corner of Sia’s mouth.

"I don't want you to be in a difficult position either. Let's keep it a secret. Instead,"

He leaned his upper body forward, closing the distance.

"When we're alone, I'm going to do whatever I want. As much as I've held back."
"…Whatever you want? Like what?"
"Like this."

He stole Sia’s lips without warning. Her heart wouldn't survive past the morning.

On the way to work. Naturally, they took Doyun’s car together.

"P-Please drop me off over there! In front of the convenience store!"
"We can just go to the underground parking lot together."
"What if someone sees us! I'll get off here."

Eventually, Sia got off a block away before the company building was visible and ran. Doyun smiled bitterly watching her retreating figure.

"Someone might think I'm going to eat her."

As soon as she arrived at the office, Sia sat down and caught her breath.

"Oh, Ms. Lee! Good morning! But why are you so surprised? Did you commit a crime?"

When Mina greeted her, Sia jumped like a thief caught red-handed.

"N-No. I ran because I thought I’d be late…"
"But your face is glowing today? Did something good happen over the weekend?"
"Huh? No, no! I just rested well…"

Sia hurriedly turned on her monitor. Her heart pounded. Was secret dating always this thrilling?

Morning meeting. The conference room door opened, and Doyun entered.

Perfect suit fit, cold expression. He was so dry that it was hard to believe he was the same man who wore an apron and toasted bread just a while ago.

"Alright, let's start the meeting. Check weekend issues first."

He didn't even look at Sia. 'Wow… he deserves an Acting Award.' Sia felt a mix of disappointment and relief.

"For this brand renewal, let's finalize Plan B and develop it. Assistant Manager Lee Sia will take the lead."
"Pardon? Me?"
"Why, are you not confident? I saw in the last presentation that your direction was good."

Doyun spoke coldly as he flipped through documents.

"I give opportunities to capable people. There is no personal feeling involved, so don't misunderstand."

Employees nodded at the words 'no personal feeling involved'. But only Sia knew.

Under the desk, the tip of Doyun’s shoe was tapping against hers. *Tap.*

Sia looked at him in surprise, but Doyun was calmly looking at the screen.

'This man, seriously…'

His secret teasing didn't stop even during the meeting. A cold boss above the table, a lover playing footsie underneath.

This breathless double life began. Her heart felt like it was shrinking, yet the subtle vibration transmitted between her legs sent shivers down her spine.

"Assistant Manager Lee, why is your face so red? Are you sick?"
"Ah, no! The heater is a bit hot…"
"Is that so? Assistant Manager Kim, lower the temperature."

Doyun pretended not to know and gave instructions. A very faint smile hung on his lips.

That fox-like man. How can I win against him?

Sia sighed and cooled her flushed cheeks. Future office life was going to be truly dynamic.

Crossing the line meant accepting this thrilling danger. And Sia was ready to enjoy that danger willingly.
