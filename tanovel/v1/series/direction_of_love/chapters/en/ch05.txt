After work, as soon as Sia got home, she spread her tarot cloth on the bed.

Her heart was still pounding. The incident in the pantry… 'If there were no eyes watching, I would have kissed you.'

That low voice, the hot breath, the feel of his arm around her waist.

It was clear. Cha Doyun was hitting on her. With serious intent.

"Haa… this is driving me crazy."

Sia shuffled the cards.

One question. 'CEO Cha Doyun, what on earth is he thinking? What will happen to us?'

She carefully drew three cards. Past, Present, Future.

First, [THE TOWER].
A crumbling tower. "The past is correct. My peaceful office life has been completely shattered."

Second, [THE DEVIL].
A man and woman bound by chains. Intense temptation, bondage, physical desire.

"Desire?" The sticky tension in the pantry came to mind. The comment about being 'boiling with desire.' Does it mean falling into irresistible temptation?

Third, [WHEEL OF FORTUNE].
Fate turning round and round. An unavoidable flow.

"The tower crumbles, you fall for the devil's temptation, and finally, you ride the wheel of fortune?"

The interpretation was clear. 'You’re already hooked. You can't run away.'

"…No way."

Just then, her phone rang. [CEO Cha Doyun].

His timing was impeccable.

"H-Hello?"
- Are you home? Why does your voice sound like that? Thinking about me again?

A low laugh traveled through the receiver.

"N-No! It’s just… I’m tired."
- Liar. You know we have field work tomorrow, right? You're coming with me. My driver is on vacation.
"What? Do I have to drive?"
- No. I will. You sit in the passenger seat and talk to me.

Another work order that sounded like a date request. The 'Devil' card flickered in her vision.

"…Understood. What time should I be there?"
- 9 AM. I'll come to your house. Actually, I'm already on my way.
"What?!"

Startled, Sia looked out the window. A familiar black sedan was entering the narrow alleyway.

- You're at the window. Come down. Let me see your face.
"N-Now? I’m not wearing makeup…"
- Doesn't matter. You’ll be pretty anyway.

This man, his brakes were definitely broken.

Sia hurriedly ran down the stairs. Wet hair, loose t-shirt. She was defenseless, but she didn't hate the way her heart was bursting.

Under the alley streetlamp, she saw Doyun leaning against his car door. Like a scene from a movie, it felt unreal.

"Seriously… you have no manners. Showing up without notice."
"I couldn't help it because I missed you."

Doyun strode over and tucked Sia’s wet hair behind her ear.

"Ms. Lee. Did you do a tarot reading?"
"Huh? How did you know?"
"Your expression tells me. So, what did it say?"

Playful eyes shining. She couldn't exactly say, 'I'm falling for the devil's temptation and getting crushed by the wheel of fortune.'

"Just… it said to watch out for bad men."
"Bad men, huh."

Doyun smirked and took another step closer.

"It’s not wrong. Because I plan to be a bit bad."
"…Do what?"
"Confusing you. I’m going to stop doing that starting today."

He gripped Sia’s shoulders.

"From now on, I’m going to shake you openly. So brace yourself."

At his declaration of war, the Wheel of Fortune in Sia’s head began to turn with a loud creak.

Ah, I'm screwed. This is an unavoidable 'Destiny.'
