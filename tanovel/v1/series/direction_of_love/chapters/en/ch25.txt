[False happiness does not last long.]

The moment she opened the box with ominous red writing, a fishy smell wafted out. It wasn't dead flowers or animal carcasses. It was the miasma emitted by paper bundles that were more malicious and fatal.

"What is this…?"

Sia picked up the contents of the box with trembling hands.

Dozens of photos and printouts. There, project drafts Sia recently conducted at Momentum were cleverly compared with works of unknown foreign artists. It was data maliciously cobbled together in composition, color, and object placement.

[Plagiarism Artist Lee Sia. Accusing your fake success. Don't hide behind a dirty sponsor and speak with skill.]

Sia’s face turned pale at the phrase written on the very last page. Her fingertips trembled.

"P-Plagiarism? I swear I never did that! I've never even heard of this artist's name!"

Sia shouted in injustice. Tears welled up.

Doyun scanned the photos silently. Even to an expert's eyes, it was meticulous. Traces of manipulation were evident, cutting the context of the original and picking only the most common elements from Sia’s design to make them look similar. Someone determined to bury her.

"I know. That it's fabricated."

Doyun spoke coldly. His voice was cold like ice, but the object of that anger wasn't Sia.

"I checked your work logs, sketchbooks, and reference mood boards. This is a malicious attack with someone targeting you. It's the work of bastards who want to see you crumble."
"But… if something like this spreads…"
"It won't spread. Because I'll block it. I won't let even a single rat escape."

Doyun called the chief secretary.

"Full mobilize the security team to secure lobby CCTV, search quick service companies to find the sender. Right now."

At that moment, the company-wide mail notification sound rang simultaneously. *Ding, ding, ding.*

The chief secretary, the receptionist in the lobby, and passing employees all took out their phones.

[Urgent Tip: The ugly truth of Momentum's next project main designer Lee Sia]

The lobby filled with murmurs in an instant. An anonymous letter was sent to all employees before it could even be blocked.

Sia almost collapsed as her legs gave out.

"What do I do… everyone saw it…"

Hearing 'parachute' because she was the fiancée was hard enough, but now she was about to take on the disgraceful stigma of 'plagiarism artist'.

Employees' gazes focused on Sia standing in the middle of the lobby. Sticky gazes mixed with suspicion, contempt, pity, and curiosity. Suffocating.

Doyun blocked Sia’s front and wrapped her like a giant wall.

But Sia let go of Doyun’s clothes.

"I won't hide."
"Sia-ya."
"If I hide here, it's really admitting it. Like with Minwoo Sunbae… I won't run away."

Sia put strength into her trembling legs and stood straight. And spoke to Doyun.

"CEO, give me a chance."
"Chance?"
"The 'Star City Landmark' bid competition next month. Please let me participate as the main designer."

Doyun’s eyes widened. The Star City project was the largest scale business ever, on which Momentum's life and death depended. Moreover, the competitor was 'Vertex' Design, infamous for being number one in the industry and aggressive sales.

"It's dangerous. Public opinion isn't good right now, if you fail… the criticism you'll have to bear then is beyond imagination."
"I won't fail. I'll prove it with skill. That I'm not a knockoff but real. That my design isn't fake, I want to prove it with my own hands."

That determination seen in the study in Episode 23 was seen again in Sia’s eyes. She was no longer a fragile flower hiding behind someone's protection.

Doyun smirked and ruffled her hair.

"Okay. Cha Doyun’s woman should have this much guts."

He shouted loudly enough for the lobby to ring so all employees could hear.

"Attention everyone! This plagiarism suspicion is malicious black propaganda by a competitor, and the legal team is already preparing rebuttal data and complaints. We will track the distributor to the end and hold them legally responsible!"

At his charisma, the murmurs subsided.

Doyun wrapped his arm around Sia’s shoulder and declared.

"And Assistant Manager Lee Sia will compete head-on with skill as the main designer of this Star City project. Those who doubt her skill, check with the result. Those who have complaints can write a resignation letter and leave now."

It was a declaration of war.

Sia clenched her fists. To the enemy in the dark trying to frame her, and to the prejudice of the world, she would show them clearly.

That not just my 'Direction of Love', but my 'Direction of Career' is also made by me.

That night, Sia stayed in the empty office and held a tablet pen all night.

The agony of creation and the tenacity to wash away the stigma were burning her. A magnificent and original landmark shape was being created on the screen.

But she didn't know. That the eye of the webcam shining faintly on the desk was watching her.

That someone accessed the cloud account linked to her tablet and was stealing her ideas in real-time.

[Access IP: 192.168.X.X (Bypass Access)]

Someone sitting in front of a monitor in the dark smiled fishily and was downloading Sia’s design file.

"Draw hard. Since it will all be mine anyway."
