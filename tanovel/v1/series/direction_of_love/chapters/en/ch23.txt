[Chairman]

The air in the living room froze cold at the three syllables displayed on the phone screen.

Doyun let out a short sigh and answered the phone. His brow furrowed.

"Yes, Father."
- …

Heavy silence flowed over the receiver. That silence gave more pressure than yelling or reprimands. After a while, a low and husky voice came through.

- Come home. Bring that girl.
"But she is still physically…"
- I give you 1 hour. If you're late, I'll send people myself.

*Click.* The call ended heartlessly.

Doyun put down the phone with a stiff expression. His gaze was directed at the empty air.

"The Chairman… what did he say?"

Sia asked carefully. Because she read an unusual current in Doyun’s expression.

"He said come. With you."
"…Now?"
"No need to go. I'll block it. You are still a patient. And it's obvious what Father will say."

Doyun spoke firmly, but Sia read deep agony in his eyes. The complex feelings of a son standing before a huge mountain called father. And his loneliness from being pressured under that shadow all his life.

Sia got up from the bed and headed to the closet.

"Let's go."
"Lee Sia."
"You said I'm your fiancée. You said I'm the person to live with you for a lifetime. I can't hide behind you forever."

Sia’s eyes didn't waver. Going through the Minwoo incident, she was no longer a coward running away. Because she realized she had to become strong too to protect the person she loved.

"And I'm quite popular with elders. Don't worry."

Sia tried to smile brightly. Doyun stared at her blankly for a moment, then hugged her tight.

"I'm sorry. Making you go through this. If Father says anything, hide behind me. I'll block it no matter what."

1 hour later, in front of a huge mansion in Seongbuk-dong.

The magnificent iron gate opened automatically and the car entered. Even the sound of the gardener's shears held tension. A silence and intimidation as if entering a different world.

The study they entered guided by the housekeeper was imbued with the smell of old paper and heavy mahogany.

Behind a huge desk, an old gentleman sat with white hair but eyes blazing like a beast. It was Chairman Cha, the head of Daejin Group.

"Greetings. I am Lee Sia of Momentum."

Sia bowed 90 degrees politely. Chairman Cha scanned Sia slowly over his glasses. It was a gaze that seemed to pierce through to her insides like an X-ray.

"Sit."

Short command. The two sat side by side on the sofa. Doyun held Sia’s hand tight under the table.

"I watched the press conference well. It was quite a spectacle."

Chairman Cha said, putting down his teacup. The tone was calm, but the sharpness contained within flew like a dagger.

"Declaring an engagement in front of the whole nation without a word of consultation. You smeared mud on my face properly. There is a lot of talk in the board of directors too."
"Is protecting my woman smearing mud on your face?"

Doyun retorted without losing. Taut tension hung in the air.

"Protecting your woman is fine. But the method was rash. The group's stock price is fluctuating. Tens of billions won are going back and forth because of your 'love' play."
"Momentum's sales have been rising for 3 consecutive quarters. The stock price will recover soon."
"Silence!"

Chairman Cha yelled. *Thunder.* Sia’s shoulders flinched at the loud resonance.

He turned only his gaze to glare at Sia.

"Did you say Ms. Lee Sia?"
"Yes, Chairman."
"My son deserves to fall for you. You look decent. Like a fox."

Words she couldn't tell if they were praise or insult.

"Right, I did a little background check on you. Graduated from a local university visual design department, living with a single mother. Hobby is Tarot reading?"

Chairman Cha snorted as if dumbfounded.

"Do you think you fit with our Doyun? I'm asking if you think the levels match."
"Father! What are you saying in front of a person!"

Doyun stood up in rage. But Sia grabbed Doyun’s arm and sat him down. And looked straight at Chairman Cha. Instead of fear, firm determination stood in her eyes.

"It is true that I am lacking if looking only at background. I must be far short of your standards, Chairman."
"You know it yourself. Good that you grasp the subject quickly."
"However, I do not love the money or background Doyun-ssi has. I love the human Cha Doyun. And…"

Sia caught her breath for a moment.

"I am certain that I am the only person who can make him most comfortable by his side."
"Comfort? Love? Does that crap feed you? Cut the romantic crap."
"Yes. Even if it doesn't feed him, at least I become a place for Doyun-ssi to breathe. Have you ever given Doyun-ssi room to breathe, Chairman? You have always only tightened his windpipe."

At Sia’s bold counter-question, the air in the study stopped. Even Doyun looked at Sia with surprised eyes.

Chairman Cha's brow furrowed, and his eyebrows twitched.

"…Ha."

A cough of disbelief. As silence lengthened, cold sweat flowed down her back.

After a long time, Chairman Cha opened his mouth.

"You said you read Tarot cards?"

It was an unexpected, random question.

"Yes."
"Then read my fortune once. Whether I will accept you as a daughter-in-law, or throw you out. Predict with those capable cards of yours."

This was a test. To test her nerve, and that 'destiny' she believed in.

Sia took Tarot cards out of her bag. Her fingertips trembled minutely, but she took a deep breath and shuffled the cards. *Shwrrk*, the sound of cards shuffling sounded exceptionally loud.

She fanned out the cards.

"Please pick just one card. As your heart goes, Chairman."

Chairman Cha picked up one card indifferently.

Checking the picture, Sia’s eyes shook.

It was the [JUDGEMENT] card. A picture of people in coffins waking up and cheering as an angel blows a trumpet.

"Now, recite it. What does my destiny say."

Sia swallowed dry saliva. Everything depended on this one interpretation.
