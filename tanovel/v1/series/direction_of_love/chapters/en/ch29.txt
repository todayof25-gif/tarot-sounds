Morning after the proposal.

Winter sunlight came in slanted through the curtains and shone warmly on the bed. As soon as Sia opened her eyes, she checked her left ring finger. Shining Tiffany diamond ring. It wasn't a dream. The cold yet heavy touch on her finger told her it was reality.

"Did you sleep well?"

Next to her, Doyun was propping his head up, looking down at Sia. With disheveled hair and a locked voice as if he hadn't been awake long, but his eyes were warmer than the morning sun.

"Yes. I slept so well I didn't even dream. What about you, CEO?"
"I dreamt. A dream of having a wedding with you. But I tripped because I was too nervous doing the groom's entrance."

Doyun smirked (smiled) and kissed Sia’s forehead lightly.

"Now it really feels real. That you are becoming my wife. If I knew it was this good to have you next to me when I wake up in the morning, I would have proposed earlier."
"Che, you always ran away with the excuse of overtime."
"That was to prepare the surprise. Unfair, really."

The two prepared brunch leisurely. The savory smell of toasting bread and coffee aroma filled the house. A normal weekend sitting opposite at the table eating, but the air was quite different from yesterday.

"But where should we do the ceremony? Is a hotel too cliché?"
"I like a small wedding. Inviting only close people and enjoying it like a party."
"Good. Then how about the garden at the Seongbuk-dong main house for the venue? Father is serious about gardening. He'll die of happiness if the daughter-in-law says she'll marry there."

Smiles didn't leave the faces of the two talking about a small future.

Finishing the meal and sitting side by side on the living room sofa drinking coffee, Doyun suddenly proposed.

"Sia-ya, can you read Tarot once?"
"Tarot? What Tarot?"
"Our marriage life. I'm curious how we'll live in the future. Let's see just for fun."

For the man who used to snort saying "I don't believe in superstition" to suggest reading Tarot first. The power of love was great, or maybe he was that curious and excited about the future.

Sia took out old Tarot cards from the drawer. Cards stained with her touch for a long time, having been with countless worries and choices.

"Okay, calm your mind and… pick just one card thinking about our future. Don't think too deeply, just as your hand goes."

Doyun chose a card carefully. His fingertips seemed to tremble minutely too. An expression more serious than when signing a multi-billion won contract.

The moment she flipped the card he picked, Sia’s eyes widened.

[WHEEL OF FORTUNE].

It was the very card that predicted the meeting of the two in Episode 1, and hinted at an unstable future in Episode 5. A picture where a giant wheel turns, with a Sphinx on top and Anubis hanging below.

"This… came out again."

Sia muttered.

"Why the face? Is it bad?"

Doyun asked worriedly, examining Sia’s expression.

"No. I used to be scared of this card. Because it felt like being swept away by a huge force of destiny. Like being swept by a wave I couldn't go against no matter how much I struggled. Fickle destiny that falls endlessly when bad, though good when good."

Sia stroked the card quietly with her finger. Angels were watching reading books at the 4 corners.

But it looked different now. Rather than fear, a certain conviction arose.

"But it's different now. This wheel… we are turning it."
"We?"
"Yes. Destiny doesn't orbit a fixed track, but we grab the handle and drive. If it rains you will hold an umbrella, and if the road is blocked I can look at the map. The wheel might go down sometimes, but I'm not scared because I know it will come back up."

Sia placed the card on Doyun’s palm.

"This card now means 'Future we make together', not 'Inevitable destiny'. Whether upright or reversed, as long as we are together, every direction will be the right answer."

Doyun smiled and held Sia’s hand tight. Deep trust dwelt in his eyes.

"Good interpretation. Like it. As expected, our Assistant Manager Lee, no, Author Lee's interpretation is clear."
"Right? I told you I'm a capable fortune teller. I'll take the reading fee as love for life."
"Okay. From now on my life's navigation is you, Lee Sia. The direction you point is my path. Even if I lose my way sometimes, you grab me again."

Doyun hugged Sia deeply. Heartbeats touched and beat like one.

"I love you. More than destiny, more than choice."
"Me too. I'm glad you are my destiny, and my choice."

Outside the window, first snow was falling before they knew it.

Rain that fell yesterday stopped, and quiet white snowflakes danced and settled as if covering all the noise of the world.

It wasn't passionate like the shower of summer 3 years ago, but it was the purest blessing blessing the new beginning of the two.

Sia looked out the window in Doyun’s arms.

The Wheel of Fortune will now turn without stopping. In the direction they want, 'The Direction of Love'.
