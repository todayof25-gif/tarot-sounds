"This is market research for trend analysis. You know that, right?"
"Yes, yes, I understand perfectly."

Sia answered soullessly. Called out on a weekend again.

The location was the hottest pop-up store in Seongsu-dong. It had a grand justification of 'identifying consumer needs', but to anyone looking, it was an essential date course for couples.

Doyun wore a dark navy knit with a beige coat, and the fit was so good that women passing by turned around to look at him. Some even openly scanned him up and down.

'He really is… handsome.'

Sia was stealing glances at him with a mix of jealousy when she got caught.

"Is there something on my face?"
"No! Just… the clothes suit you. You look like a model."
"That's a relief. I put effort into shopping at the department store yesterday just to impress you."

He threw another hook nonchalantly. Sia cleared her throat and looked away.

"O-Overwrite there! A new perfume pop-up! Let's go!"

They toured the pop-up store, went to a famous bakery cafe, and waited in line to eat bread. Doyun stood behind Sia without complaining, blocking the crowd for her.

When cream from the bread got on her lips, he naturally wiped it off with his hand.

"Clumsy. Do you get it on you every time you eat?"
"Ah, thank you…"

Current touch was so gentle that Sia thought her heart stopped.

Toward evening, as they were heading to the parking lot after having fun, the sky suddenly darkened and a shower began to pour. *Splash*, thick raindrops soaked the asphalt.

The two hurriedly took shelter under the eaves of a nearby stationery store.

"Wow, sudden rain…"
"It wasn't in the forecast. Troublesome."

Doyun looked up at the sky, perplexed. The convenience store was far, and the car was in the parking lot. The rain was getting heavier, blurring their vision.

Then, Doyun took off his coat.

Just like Sia did for the cat in front of the convenience store three years ago.

This time, he spread his coat over Sia’s head.

"Use this. We just need to run to the parking lot."
"What about you, CEO? You'll get soaked, no way!"
"I'm fine. It's better than you catching a cold."
"Absolutely not! Let's use it together."

Sia grabbed one end of the coat and pulled him inside.

Under the beige coat, their shoulders touched. In the narrow space, the sound of rain seemed to block out the world. Close enough to hear each other breathing.

"…It's tight."
"It's fine. Let's go! One, two, three!"

The two ran into the rain. Hopping over puddles, arms around each other's waists, relying on each other's body heat as they ran.

Rain splashed and their shoes got soaked, but strangely, laughter kept bubbling up.

When they arrived at the parking lot, the two faced each other, gasping for breath.

Doyun’s hair was wet, dripping water. His shirt clung to his body, revealing a solid silhouette. Sia was a mess too.

"Look at you."
"You're not one to talk, CEO."

They burst into laughter at the same time. *Haha*, laughter echoed in the parking lot.

Then, slowly, the laughter subsided. The air grew heavy.

Doyun’s rain-soaked eyes stared deeply, very deeply, at Sia. Raindrops flowed down his sharp jawline. Sexy.

He slowly reached out and swept back Sia’s wet bangs. The contrast between the cold rain and his hot fingers.

"Ms. Lee."
"Yes…"
"I… can't stand it anymore."

His voice rang low, buried in the sound of rain.

The atmosphere shifted. Not the air of a playful date, but a dangerous, wet air.

Doyun’s head tilted slowly.

This time, she couldn't dodge, nor could she make excuses. Just like her clothes soaked in rain, her heart was already thoroughly soaked in him.
