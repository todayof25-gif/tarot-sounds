Sia opened her eyes, clutching her throbbing head.

The ceiling wasn't unfamiliar. Fortunately, it was her own room.

"Ugh… my head."

Thirst washed over her. She stumbled up to drink water when memories of last night flashed by like a film reel.

'You're a cold robot to everyone else, so why only to me…'
'If I'm nice to you, just accept it. Don't doubt it.'
'You dense woman. I'm showing it with my whole body right now. That I like you.'

I like you. I like you. I like you.

Doyun’s voice rang in her ears. And those burning eyes, the feeling of lips almost touching.

"Aaaah! Are you crazy, Lee Sia! You whined to the CEO? In the hallway?!"

Sia kicked her blanket. She wanted to hide in a mouse hole.

How can I face him? He said, 'Answer withheld. I'll hear it again tomorrow morning when you're sober.'

I want to run away. But I have to go to work. The sorrow of a salary earner.

Company lobby. Sia crept in like a stray cat, looking around.

The elevator arrived, and just as the doors were about to open.

"Hold on!"

Someone ran over. Familiar footsteps.

Cha Doyun.

'I'm doomed.'

Sia pressed herself against the wall as much as possible. Doyun entered and pressed the button. Just the two of them.

"Good morning, Ms. Lee."

He was in his usual neat suit. The disheveled look from last night was nowhere to be found.
But the subtle warmth in his voice remained.

"Y-Yes… Good morning, CEO Cha."
"How is your hangover? Did you drink honey water?"
"Ah, yes to just… whatever…"
"Is your answer ready?"

A fastball straight in. Sia’s heart dropped.

"Um, CEO Cha. Yesterday I was so drunk… I think I made a mistake…"
"A mistake?"
"Yes. I mean, my memory blackout… I don't remember well…"

It was a cowardly lie. But she wasn't ready to answer that confession right now.

Doyun’s eyes narrowed.

"You don't remember?"
"No, no! Not a thing! Really!"
"I see. I confessed 'I like you', but it's sad that you forgot it all."
"…I'm sorry."
"Then let's do it again. Until you remember."

Doyun took a step closer. The elevator dinged at the 15th floor.

"Don't think about running away. I told you. I'd chase you to your front door."

He smiled meaningfully and got off first. Sia almost collapsed as her legs gave out.

This man doesn't know how to give up.

While working at her desk, a messenger notification popped up.

[CEO Cha Doyun: Keep lunchtime open. Let's go for hangover soup.]

One-way traffic that gave no room for refusal.

Sia sighed and organized her documents, then found a note tucked in her diary. When did he put this here?

The note had a short message in Doyun’s sharp handwriting.

[Just in case you pretend not to remember, I'm leaving evidence. Me liking you, I'm serious. - Cha Doyun]

Evidence. Such an analog method.

Sia chuckled.

This man who seemed only cold, the more she knew him, the cuter he was.

And she had to admit it. The moment she saw the note, the ticklish feeling in her heart.

Running away might be meaningless now.
