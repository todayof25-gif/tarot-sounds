Day 3 of secret dating.

Sia was going between heaven and hell twelve times a day.

Heaven was Cha Doyun’s gaze, voice, and secret touches, while Hell was the chewiness of her heart fearing 'it's over if we get caught.'

[PM 02:00]

Right after lunch, a drowsy afternoon.

Sia was suppressing a yawn while looking at her monitor when a messenger notification rang.

[CEO Cha Doyun: (Photo)]

The photo was of the roof garden.

[CEO Cha Doyun: In 5 minutes. Need coffee recharge.]

Sia looked around. Everyone was focused on work. She quietly got up, pretending to grab her tumbler, and headed for the emergency stairs instead of the elevator.

Stairs up to the 15th-floor roof. She was out of breath, but her steps were light.

As soon as she opened the iron door and stepped out, someone grabbed her wrist and pulled her.

"Ah!"

Before she could scream, she was dragged into a blind spot in the corner of the roof, behind the vending machine. A familiar scent. It was Doyun.

"Aren't you too late? It's been 5 minutes."
"It was hard coming up the emergency stairs. I was afraid of running into someone on the elevator."

Doyun chuckled and tidied Sia’s messed-up bangs.

"Good job. Thanks to that, no one saw."

He took the tumbler from Sia’s hand and drank a sip instead.

"Ah, that's mine…"
"Indirect kiss. Do you dislike it?"
"…Seriously, you're cheesy."

When Sia glared, Doyun giggled and wrapped his arms around her waist. A hug under the blue sky on the open roof. Sia glanced at the door, worried someone might see.

"Don't be anxious. This is a CCTV blind spot. I checked."
"You're meticulous, really."
"Did you think I'd try an office romance without this much preparation?"

Doyun lowered his head to find Sia’s lips. A short, deep kiss.

"Cheer up for the afternoon. See you after work."

He lightly patted Sia’s butt (Pervert!) and went down the roof first.

Sia cooled her burning face and returned to the office a long time later.

[PM 06:10]

Quitting time. Employees packed their bags one by one.

As Sia was packing her bag, Mina spoke to her.

"Ms. Lee, what are you doing today? Should we grab chicken and beer?"
"Huh? Ah… I have prior plans today. Sorry."
"Oh my, with whom? Perhaps… a boyfriend?"

Mina's intuition was scary. Pricked, Sia stuttered.

"N-No! Just a friend, a friend!"
"Suspicious~ You leave right on time these days. Okay, let's eat next time."

That was close. Sia fled the office and headed to the underground parking lot.

Zone B3. She saw Doyun’s car.

She looked around and quickly hopped into the passenger seat.

"Safe."
"This is like a 007 operation."

Doyun in the driver’s seat smiled and checked her seatbelt.

"What should we eat for dinner? I reserved a good place today."
"What is it?"
"That pasta place you were singing about."
"No way! Isn't that place fully booked?"
"Your boyfriend used his ability a bit."

It was the moment Doyun was about to start the car.

Far away, the elevator doors opened and someone walked out.

"Huh? Isn't that Department Head Kim?"

Sia gasped in shock, reclining her seat as far back as possible and ducking down.

"Huk, this is crazy!"

Department Head Kim grumbled as he walked to his car, which happened to be parked right next to Doyun’s.

He stopped opening his door and noticed Doyun’s car.

"Huh? Isn't this the CEO's car?"

He knocked on the window. *Knock, knock.*

'I'm doomed.' Sia crumpled herself under the dashboard.

Doyun sighed quietly and rolled down the window halfway.

"You haven't left yet, Department Head?"
"Oh my, CEO! Are you working late?"

Kim tried to bend down and peer inside the car. Doyun naturally leaned over, blocking the view of the passenger seat.

"Yes, I'm on my way to a client meeting."
"Ah, I see. You're working hard. But in the passenger seat… you seem to have a lot of luggage?"

Sia’s bag was sitting alone on the passenger seat. A mistake she hadn't managed to clear away.

Sia held her breath. If caught, it's over. 'Why is a female employee's bag in the CEO's car?' Rumors would spread instantly.

Doyun’s voice rang out calmly.

"Ah, this? It's a sample bag to give to the client. The design is a bit flashy, isn't it?"
"Aha, I see. I thought it was a woman's bag. Haha."

Kim nodded without suspicion, said goodbye, and got into his car.

One minute until Kim's car drove away. That one minute felt like a year.

"He's gone. Come out."

Sia sat up with messy hair. Tension released, leaving her body weak.

"Wow… I really thought I was going to have a heart attack."
"Sample bag, how was my quick wit?"
"I'll praise you. But I really think my lifespan shortened."

When Sia made a crying face, Doyun took her hand and kissed the back of it.

"Sorry. I'll hide you more perfectly next time. Or…"

He laughed playfully.

"Should I just reveal it? That you're my woman."
"Absolutely, absolutely not!"
"Okay. Instead, dinner is on you tonight, Ms. Lee. As compensation for the heart attack."
"Che, rich guy."

The car exited the parking lot and joined the flashy city center.

Although it was a series of suffocating hide-and-seek games, Doyun’s hand on the steering wheel and Sia’s hand overlapping it did not come apart.

It was a night more thrilling because it was a secret, and more affectionate because they were afraid of getting caught.

But Sia didn't know.

That Department Head Kim's car dashcam had captured a blurry image of Sia getting into Doyun’s car.

And that the footage was running like a time bomb, waiting to explode at any moment.
