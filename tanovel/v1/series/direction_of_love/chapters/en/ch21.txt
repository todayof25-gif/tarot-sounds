When a tense string snaps, the suppressed pain rushes in like a dam bursting.

After Minwoo was arrested and all the commotion settled, Sia fell asleep as if fainting in the passenger seat of Doyun’s car. It was severe body aches that came with relief.

Doyun brought her to his officetel. Hyena-like reporters were already camping in front of Sia’s studio apartment.

"…Mmm. It hurts…"

Laid on the bed, Sia tossed and turned, groaning. Cold sweat flowed down her temples.

Doyun put his hand on her forehead with a worried face. She was burning up like a fireball.

"The fever is too high."

He hurriedly rolled up his shirt sleeves and headed to the bathroom. Bringing a towel soaked in cold water, he wiped Sia’s forehead and neck with careful touches.

Usually, she would have reacted sensitively to someone else's touch, but now she only breathed faintly as if relying on Doyun’s touch.

"No… don't come…"

Sia muttered, frowning as if in pain. In her nightmare, she was trapped in the hotel room again, trembling at Minwoo's fishy laughter.

"No… don't go… Doyun-ssi…"

Unconsciously, her hand flailed in the air. A gesture desperately looking for someone.

Feeling a heart-wrenching pain, Doyun quickly grabbed that hand and brought it to his cheek.

"I won't go. I'm here. I'm not going anywhere."

He put his lips to Sia’s ear and whispered constantly.

"I'll protect you. No one can touch you. So don't be afraid."

His low voice seeped into Sia’s ear like a lullaby. A firm and warm voice, as if trying to drive away the darkness in the nightmare.

Doyun didn't leave Sia’s side for a moment all night.

When the fever rose, he changed the wet towel, wiped away her cold sweat, and when she whined about being thirsty, he moistened her lips with gauze soaked in water.

Anxiety, which wasn't seen even when directing multi-billion won projects, filled his face.

The cold CEO Cha Doyun was gone. Only a desperate man trying to protect his lover suffering from fever remained there.

It wasn't until the next afternoon that Sia lifted her heavy eyelids.

An unfamiliar ceiling with soft indirect lighting turned on. And a familiar citrus scent brushing her nose tip.

Turning her head, she saw Doyun dozing off sitting on a chair next to the bed.

His face, sleeping with his head dropped in an uncomfortable posture, had become gaunt overnight. Stubbles had grown dark on his chin, and his lips were dry.

'Because of me… did he not sleep a wink?'

Memories came back in patches. The touch of the cold towel wiping her burning body, the voice constantly whispering that it's okay.

Sia teared up. She carefully reached out to brush back the disheveled hair on his forehead.

At that moment, Doyun’s eyes opened wide.

"You're awake?"

His voice was locked and cracked, but his eyes examining Sia shone alertly.

He jumped up and checked Sia’s forehead first.

"Ha, thank goodness. The fever went down. Do you feel a bit alive now?"

He let out a long breath in relief. Only then did his tension release, and he flopped back onto the chair.

"CEO… I'm sorry. Suffering just because of me…"
"Saying sorry again. I told you. Don't feel sorry to me."

Doyun put on a stern expression, then brought a bowl of porridge he had kept in a thermos on the table.

"Sit up. You have to eat something. It's not good to take medicine on an empty stomach."

He helped Sia sit up and spooned the porridge himself, blowing on it. *Hoo, hoo.*

"I'll eat it. I have hands…"
"Open your mouth. Enjoy it when you're treated like a patient. I'm going to bill you for everything later."

Sia had no choice but to open her mouth. As the warm and soft vegetable porridge went down her throat, her stinging stomach felt soothed.

As her body became comfortable, anxiety in her heart began to creep up.

Sia’s gaze turned to her phone turned off on the nightstand.

'Articles… what happened to the comments. People must be cursing us, right?'

"Um… what about the company? The reporters…"
"Turn off your concern. Don't look at your phone either. I'm handling everything."

Doyun firmly put the phone away.

"But… I heard weird rumors spread all over the internet. Sponsors and what not…"

Sia’s voice started trembling again. She was more afraid of the blow Doyun would take than her own honor. Afraid that the tag of 'Young CEO's inappropriate private life' would follow him forever.

"Image is life for your company, CEO, but because of me…"

Doyun put down the spoon and held Sia’s two cold hands tight.

"Lee Sia. Look at me."

Intense black eyes bound Sia’s shaking eyes straight.

"I don't care if dogs bark outside. They leave when they get tired of barking. The important thing is the fact. And the fact is, that I love you."
"…!"
"I'm going to correct everything. I mobilized the legal team fully to file complaints, and planned all response scenarios. So you just rest well here and build up your stamina."
"Stamina?"
"Yeah. Because you'll need a lot of strength to fight back with me later."
"Fight back?"

Doyun grinned. It was that arrogant but charming hunter's smile he showed in the beginning.

"We're going to date publicly. Very ostentatiously, loudly. So all of South Korea knows."
"What?!"

Sia’s eyes grew round like a rabbit.

"Don't want to? It's no use even if you don't. I already planned everything. Dating in hiding is over now. I intend to show the world clearly whose woman you are."

Doyun planted a short kiss on Sia’s lips. *Smack.*

"So get better quickly. It doesn't look good if my partner is feeble next to me."

At his playful but sincere words, Sia burst into a small laugh. *Pfft.*

This man, really unstoppable. Reckless and wilful, but that's why he was more reassuring than anyone in the world.

Cold winter rain was falling outside the window, but Doyun’s embrace was warmer than ever.

The fever tormenting her body had passed, but now a hot fever of another meaning was about to begin. A sweet, incurable fever called love.
