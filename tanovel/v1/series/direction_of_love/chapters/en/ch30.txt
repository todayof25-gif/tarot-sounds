Seasons turned and turned, and spring came again.

The wide garden of the Seongbuk-dong main house was filled with colorful spring flowers. Befitting a garden Chairman Cha tended himself, not a single flower or tree was planted in vain. It was a perfect May afternoon with cherry blossom petals fluttering and lilac scent spreading gently.

Today, Doyun and Sia’s wedding is held here.

"Isn't the bride's waiting room too open? Everything is visible."

Sia, wearing a dress, said laughing. The bride's waiting room was under a white canopy installed in the middle of the garden. It was a structure open on all sides so she could make eye contact with guests.

"Because I didn't want to lock you up. So you can run away whenever you want."

Doyun, wearing a tuxedo, answered playfully and arranged Sia’s veil. His appearance wearing a bow tie was much nicer than usual, and also looked a bit nervous.

"But what do I do? I have absolutely zero thought of running away. Where would I go leaving this good man?"
"That's a relief. I have absolutely zero thought of letting you go either. There is no divorce in my dictionary, you know right?"

Doyun kissed the back of Sia’s hand. The spot his lips touched was warm.

Guests arrived one by one. Sia’s friends, Momentum employees exclaiming "Wow, daebak", and political and business figures including Chairman Cha. Even in the bustling crowd, the two's gazes were fixed on each other.

From afar, Chairman Cha approached coughing. *Ahem.*

"Hmm, flowers bloomed a bit less. Need to pay more attention next year. Changed the fertilizer and it's not good…"

It was a gruff tone, but his eyes were smiling happily. He sneaked up to Sia who would become his daughter-in-law and handed her a small box.

"This… is what your mother-in-law used to use. She left it from a long time ago to give to you, but I'm giving it only now. She earnestly requested to give it if I got a daughter-in-law."

Inside the box were antique and elegant pearl earrings. They were more beautiful because they bore traces of time.

"Chairman… no, Father. Thank you. They are really pretty."
"Okay. If Doyun that guy rots your insides (upsets you), tell me immediately. I'll scold him severely."

Chairman Cha walked away with hands behind his back as if shy. That back view didn't look lonely like before. Sia’s nose tip stung.

The wedding began. Instead of a fancy officiant's speech, the two decided to read vows to each other they wrote themselves.

First, Sia grabbed the microphone. Her hand trembled a bit, but her voice was clear.

"I was a person who believed in destiny. I thought the future the cards told was fixed, and only tried to avoid misfortune. But meeting you, I learned. Destiny doesn't fall from the sky, but is something we choose and create every moment. Thank you for letting me choose the destiny called you, and for staying by my side. I will become a wife who gets hit by rain with you when it rains, and laughs with you on sunny days."

Warm applause burst out from the guests. Friends wiping tears were seen.

Next, Doyun grabbed the microphone. He stared deeply at Sia for a moment and opened his mouth.

"I was a dry person who believed only in efficiency and reason. I disliked things like love because they were unpredictable variables. But the moment I saw you holding an umbrella over a small cat in front of a convenience store on a rainy day 3 years ago, all my logic collapsed."

Doyun’s voice locked slightly.

"You are the most beautiful variable that visited my life, and the only right answer. If you lose your way I will become a map, and if you are tired I will become shade where you can rest. I will become your umbrella for life and protect your side. I love you."

As Doyun’s voice resonated in the garden, tears eventually flowed from Sia’s eyes.

The two put rings on each other. Tokens promising eternity sparkled under the sunlight.

"Groom, Bride march!"

With the host's energetic voice, the two walked the flower path. Cherry blossom petals poured down from the sky like rain.

If the rain 3 years ago was cold, today's flower rain was warm and fragrant.

Only late at night after the ceremony ended did the two arrive at their newlywed home.

At the entrance, in the most visible spot, a frame was hanging.

[THE LOVERS].

It was the very card Sia dropped in Episode 1, which connected the two. Now it wasn't a simple piece of paper, but became an heirloom symbolizing eternal love.

"It's Upright."

Doyun said looking at the frame, loosening his tie.

"Yes, perfect upright direction. The best love luck."

Sia wrapped her arms around Doyun’s waist.

"But I think we don't need to read Tarot anymore."
"Why? Aren't you curious? Like tomorrow's fortune."
"No. Because there's no need to know the future in advance. Whatever future comes, if I'm with you, I think it will all be okay. If reversed comes out we can turn it back, and if a bad card comes out we can overcome it together."

Doyun faced Sia and smiled brightly.

"That's the right answer. As expected my wife is smart."

He kissed Sia. Curtains fluttered in the spring breeze, and moonlight shone long on the two.

Destiny is not fixed.

If walking with a loved one, even if the path is winding, all those footsteps are the right direction.

Now, this moment. Our direction is facing each other.

This is exactly, the 'Direction of Love' we create.

[Final Episode End]
