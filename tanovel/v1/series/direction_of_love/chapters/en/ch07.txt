"Alright, cheers everyone! For the success of this project!"
"Cheers!"

*Clink!* Glasses collided with a cheerful sound.

Team dinner. Normally, Doyun would have fled after the first round, but today he was sticking around until the end. Right next to Sia.

He kept grilling meat, serving lettuce wraps, and pouring water, acting as 'Sia’s Personal Attendant.' Thanks to him, Sia didn't know if the meat was going into her mouth or her nose.

"CEO Cha, I'll do it. You don't have to…"
"Eat more. You seem to have lost weight recently, it bothers me."
"…"

The employees' gazes were secretly fixed on the two. Eyes filled with 'No way, are they?'.

Embarrassed, Sia kept downing her drinks. The somaek tasted quite sweet.
One glass, two glasses. As the alcohol circulated in her blood, the tension loosened, and suppressed emotions raised their heads.

Round 2, Karaoke.

While the atmosphere ripened and everyone shook tambourines, Sia stumbled toward the restroom.

After washing her face with cold water and coming out, she found Doyun standing in front of the smoking room at the end of the hallway. With his top two shirt buttons undone, leaning against the wall without smoking, look just like a pictorial.

"Are you okay? You drank a lot."
"Oh? It's the CEO…"

Sia grinned and approached him. Perhaps it was the alcohol, but words she would never say sober popped out.

"CEO Cha… why are you so nice to me?"
"…"
"You're a cold robot to everyone else, so why only to me… grilling meat, driving me home, getting jealous."

Sia grabbed Doyun’s shirt collar and shook it.

"Seriously confusing people…"
"Ms. Lee."
"Actually, I'm a little scared. Scared that you're playing a joke. That you're doing this for fun."
"…"
"But… I keep fluttering. I shouldn't be."

Tears welled up in Sia’s eyes. Doyun’s eyes darkened. He took Sia’s hand and placed it on his chest.

*Thump, thump, thump.*

His heart was beating wildly under Sia’s palm.

"Does a heart beat like this for a joke?"
"…Huh?"
"Do you think I'd get jealous, obsessed, and act like this for fun? Do I look that free?"

Doyun wrapped his arm around Sia’s waist and pushed her against the wall. The hallway light cast a deep shadow over his face.

"If I'm nice to you, just accept it. Don't doubt it."
"B-But…"
"You dense woman. I'm showing it with my whole body right now. That I like you."

Like.

Those words pierced Sia’s brain. It felt like sobering up instantly.

"What? Just now… what did you…"
"If you didn't hear it, forget it."
"No, I heard it! I definitely heard it!"
"Then answer me. Yes or no."

Doyun’s face came closer. Close enough for their noses to brush.
His gaze fixed on Sia’s lips.

"If you don't answer, I'll take it as a yes and kiss you."
"Um, CEO Cha… this is the hallway…"
"I don't care. No one is watching."

He slowly lowered his lips. Sia closed her eyes. She couldn't refuse. No, she didn't want to refuse.

Just as their lips were about to touch, hot breath mingled.

"Oh? Ms. Lee! I was wondering where you went!"

Mina's voice came from the end of the hallway.

Startled, Sia pushed Doyun away. Doyun licked his lips in disappointment and sighed.

"…Great timing."

He tidied his clothes and whispered to Sia.

"Answer withheld. I'll hear it again tomorrow morning when you're sober."
"…"
"Don't think about running away. I'll chase you to your front door."

Doyun walked away first. Sia slid down the wall, her face bright red.

Her heart felt like it would explode.

This man is dangerous. But… I like it so much.
