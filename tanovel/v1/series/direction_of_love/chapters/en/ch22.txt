9 AM the next morning.

The air in the hotel waiting room was like a taut bowstring.

Doyun, dressed in a dark navy suit like armor, knelt on one knee in front of pale-faced Sia.

"Sia-ya."

He wrapped his warm hands around Sia’s two cold hands.

"Except reporters are packed outside right now. If we go out, flashes will go off like crazy. There will be people making up words that don't exist."

Sia swallowed dry saliva. She was afraid. Afraid that the stones the world threw at her wouldn't stop.

But Doyun’s eyes looking straight at her were solid without wavering.

"You trust me, right?"
"…Yes."
"Whatever I say today, it's all sincere. It's not acting, nor a show. So don't be surprised and stick right by my side."

Doyun paused for a moment, then asked carefully.

"And… can I nail it down to the world that you are my woman, the person I'll be with for a lifetime? If you don't want to, I won't. I'll just take legal action and end it."

Sia’s eyes widened.

This man was asking for Sia’s opinion first even in that tremendous scandal right now. As if Sia’s heart was more important than his honor.

At that delicate consideration respecting her, the fear in Sia’s heart melted away like snow.

"…Let's do it."

Sia answered quietly but clearly.

"Instead, promise me. That you will never let go of my hand."
"I promise. I won't let go for life."

Doyun kissed the back of Sia’s hand like a vow. The touch of his lips was hot.

"Let's go. To shut up those noisy mouths."

***

The hotel grand ballroom was crowded with countless reporters.

As Doyun took Sia’s hand and went up to the podium, white spots flashed before her eyes at the incessant flash barrage.

When Sia flinched, Doyun gave strength to the hand he held and hid her slightly behind his back.

That broad and solid back felt like a shield blocking all attacks from the world.

Standing in front of the microphone, Doyun’s expression was cold. The noisy hall was instantly engulfed in silence at the cool charisma overwhelming the venue.

"Hello, I am Cha Doyun, CEO of Momentum."

A low, resonant voice flowed through the speakers.

"I stood here to clarify my position regarding the malicious rumors currently circulating on the internet."

The reporters' eyes glistened like hyenas.

"To give you the conclusion first, the content is 100% fabricated falsehoods. It has been revealed through police investigation that it was a criminal act committed by competitor CEO K bearing a personal grudge."

At Doyun’s signal, evidence appeared on the screen behind.

Messages Minwoo fabricated, originals of the illegally filmed photos, and even recordings of calls threatening Sia.

The hall buzzed in front of the naked truth.

"The victim was merely a target of a thoroughly planned crime. Yet, some media are not hesitating to commit secondary victimization by turning the victim into a 'perpetrator'."

Sharp anger dwelt in Doyun’s voice.

"Especially the rumor that Assistant Manager Lee Sia 'had an inappropriate relationship in exchange for promotion' is an act insulting Momentum's system. She rose to that position by skill."

He paused for a moment and looked back at Sia.

'It's okay.' At his eyes nodding, Sia also gained courage and showed a faint smile.

Doyun stared at the front again.

"But the real reason I stood here today is separate."

He adjusted his tie for a moment and calmed his breathing.

"I want to correct the expression 'inappropriate relationship' among the contents of the rumor."

A silence where one could hear a pin drop.

Doyun lifted the hand he was holding and intertwined his fingers with hers, showing it off to the whole world.

"Ms. Lee Sia is not a simple employee to me. She is my lover, and my fiancée who will draw my future together."
"What?!"
"Fiancée? Is that true?"

The press seating turned upside down. Flashes exploded like fireworks.

"Therefore, acts insulting my person with low-class words like 'sponsor' or 'entertainment' will be considered an attack on my family from now on. For all malicious rumors occurring after today, I will hold them legally responsible to the end without settlement or leniency."

His declaration was clear.

Touch her and you die. This woman is mine, so watch your mouth.

It was the most powerful and arrogant, yet the most romantic warning in the world.

Sia’s eyes became hot.

He stood her, who just wanted to hide from people's gazes, under the fanciest lighting and announced 'she is my person'. It was a certainty more intense than any proposal.

On the way down from the podium after the press conference ended.

Doyun whispered playfully in Sia’s ear.

"Did you see? Was I a bit cool?"

Sia burst into a small laugh with a tear-stained face. *Pfft.*

"Yes. Annoyingly, you are quite cool."
"Then no take-backs now. The whole nation stood witness. You can't run away now."
"I won't go. Anywhere."

Sia hugged Doyun’s arm tight.

"I'm going to stick to your side like gum for the rest of my life, CEO."

The two looked at each other and smiled brightly.

It was a warm smile like sunlight shining on a spot where a storm had passed.

But happiness always accompanies trials.

Doyun’s phone, back in the waiting room, rang loudly.

The three syllables of the caller's name on the screen froze the warm air in an instant.

[Chairman]

It was Chairman Cha, Doyun’s father and the group's head.

Doyun’s expression hardened stiffly.

The real war was starting now.
