It was a small sense of incongruity.

'Huh?'

Sia paused her pen on the tablet. The layer order of the file she had definitely just saved had changed subtly.

The 'shadow effect' layer she hadn't turned off was deactivated. The clock showed 3:15 PM. She had pressed save at 14 minutes past.

The mouse cursor moved 1 pixel on its own for a moment. It was a very brief instant, but the designer's sensitive senses didn't miss that foreign feeling.

Her spine chilled.

Someone is watching. My monitor, my work, even the sound of my breathing.

Goosebumps rose all over her body at the creepiness that invisible eyes were stealing a glance at her from beyond the monitor.

Sia calmed her trembling hand and tried to act slowly, very naturally. Just in case the other party noticed.

She turned off the tablet power and pulled out the LAN cable. *Thud.*

Only when the physical connection was cut did the breath she held burst out.

"Haa…"

Sia packed her documents and headed to Doyun’s room. Her heart was pounding so hard her ribs hurt.

"CEO, I have something to tell you."

As soon as she closed the door and faced Doyun, her legs gave out. Surprised, Doyun approached.

"What's wrong? Your face is white as a sheet."
"My computer… is weird. I think someone is watching. In real-time."

"A mirroring backdoor is planted in the cloud account. It was hidden so cleverly that general scans didn't catch it. We traced the IP back… they are bypassing, but the destination matches the band of competitor 'Vertex'."

Doyun’s eyes hardened sharply. The number one architectural design firm in Korea, and the strongest competitor for this Star City project.

"Doing this crap because they can't win with skill? Dirty bastards."

Blue veins stood on the back of Doyun’s clenched fist.

"I'll cut the connection immediately and hand it to the police. Sue them for industrial espionage charges."

The security team leader was about to pick up the handset, but Doyun raised his hand to stop him.

A cold gambler's light seeped into his eyes. It was the eyes of a beast that caught an opportunity to turn the tables.

"No. Leave it."
"Pardon? But it will continue to leak."
"Sia-ya."

Doyun looked back at Sia. A fishy smile hung on the corner of his mouth.

"Want to scam someone with me?"
"Scam?"
"Those bastards must believe what you are drawing now is real. Let them keep peeking. We'll give them a 'poisoned chalice' that is charming enough to drive them crazy with greed, but one they must absolutely never take."

From that day, the secret operation, code name 'Trojan Horse', began.

It was a 'show-off' design that Vertex would likely like.

Doyun took his personal laptop and headed to a hotel suite near the company with Sia. It was a bunker for just the two of them, with the internet perfectly blocked.

Doyun smiled, handing over sandwiches and warm coffee he ordered via room service.

"It might be stifling but endure it. You can't leave here until the project ends."

The two put their heads together all night and refined the real design. Doyun became an assistant, not a CEO, packing Sia’s paints, massaging her shoulders, and adding ideas.

Under the stand light, only the sound of pencils scribbling and the two people's breathing filled the room.

"This concept is 'Connection'."

"Connecting buildings and nature, people and people. Not rising high to give intimidation, but spreading low so anyone can come in and rest. A space like that."

"I designed it so that if you connect the grids of the blueprint, a specific pattern appears. A constellation you can't tell just by looking, but is clearly visible when looking down from above."

Star shape. It was 'THE STAR' of Tarot cards, and the symbol that gave hope to Sia.

"It's my signature. The fake file they stole doesn't have this 'soul'. A detail that exists only in the real file."

4 AM, unable to hold back pouring sleep, Sia leaned on Doyun’s shoulder and fell asleep.
