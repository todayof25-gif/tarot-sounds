Overtime only showed its end past 2 AM.

Sia’s eyes were bloodshot from processing the massive amount of work Doyun ordered, and her wrists ached as if they would snap.

"Haa…"

Sia’s sigh settled in the heavy silence of the office.

Tilting her head back to rotate her stiff neck, Sia suddenly realized her collarbone area felt empty. The cold metallic touch she habitually fiddled with was missing.

The 'The Star' card-shaped pendant Doyun had hung on her himself against the night view.

"Huh? Where did it go?"

Startled, Sia jumped up from her seat. The chair pushed back with a loud noise, but she didn't have time to care.

She turned her desk upside down, kneeled on the floor, and swept the carpet with her hands. She even searched the trash can.

She frantically went back and forth between the pantry and the restroom she visited today.

It wasn't there. Nowhere.

"No… please… I can't lose that…"

Although the relationship was shattered, that necklace was the only object that held Doyun’s sincerity. If she lost even that, tears welled up at the ominous feeling that her connection with him would truly be cut off forever.

'Don't tell me, the CEO's office?'

When she went to submit her resignation earlier, the moment Doyun got angry and tore the resignation letter. Did it fall during the physical struggle that wasn't quite a struggle?

Doyun was out of the office for a dinner meeting.

Sia grabbed the doorknob of the CEO's office with trembling hands.

'Just checking for a moment and coming out.'

When she opened the door carefully, the darkness of the ownerless room welcomed Sia. Only the moonlight of Seoul coming through the window quietly illuminated the desk.

Sia turned on her phone flashlight and searched the floor.

"Found it!"

A small light twinkled in the corner of the desk leg. Sia picked up the necklace with relief. The thin chain was broken. The broken state looked like their relationship, making her heart ache.

She put the necklace in her pocket and was about to leave, but the yellow document envelope on the desk caught her eye again.

That 'Investigation Report' that threw Sia into a pit of despair a few days ago.

Doyun didn't clean it up and left it there. As if he didn't want to forget.

Sia reached for the envelope again as if possessed. Back then, she had run out after seeing only the 'spy cam' photos in the front because the shock was too big.

She wanted to confirm. How thoroughly she was played. Only then did she think she could organize her feelings.

She took out the documents with trembling hands and turned them over one by one.

But as she went to the back pages, the contents defied Sia’s expectations. It was a record of 'worry', not 'surveillance'.

[Special Note: Relationship with Kang Minwoo (Important)]

[Captured circumstances of Kang Minwoo (Current CEO of Art & Design) attempting psychological domination (gaslighting) on Lee Sia during college days.]

[Anonymous informant testimony: "Minwoo Sunbae made a bet with Sia. Sia didn't know and was practically a slave."]

[Conclusion: High possibility that it remains a deep trauma for Lee Sia herself. Psychological and physical protection is desperate upon contact with Kang Minwoo.]

Her hand holding the report trembled violently. And on the very last page, Doyun’s handwritten memo on a post-it note came into view.

The date was right after Sia joined.

'- I will be entirely responsible for business meetings with Kang Minwoo.'

'- Handle it secretly so she doesn't get hurt again by finding out about past wounds.'

'- Hiring recommendation reason: Competence and character verified. Above all, I want to protect her smiling face.'

"Ah…"

A deep sigh escaped Sia’s mouth. Her legs gave out and she collapsed.

He knew. He knew all the wounds Sia suffered from Minwoo, the painful past.

That's why he overreacted so much when Minwoo appeared. The reason he acted sharply during the meeting and in Yangpyeong wasn't just because of jealousy.

He wasn't monitoring me; he was trying to protect me. Pretending not to know so my pride wouldn't be hurt, while blocking that dirty mud alone from behind.

Showing the person he wanted to protect the most meeting the perpetrator who abused her again. She couldn't even imagine how much Doyun’s heart must have torn, how much betrayal he must have felt.

"Ha, like a fool… I'm really a fool."

Sia hugged the report to her chest and sobbed. Regret and guilt rushed in like waves, hitting her chest.

"What are you doing there."

A low, locked voice was heard in the darkness.

Sia raised her head. Doyun stood at the door. Facing against the light, his expression wasn't visible, but the sound of his rough breathing broke the silence.

"Doyun-ssi…"
"Leave that and get out."

Doyun approached and tried to snatch the report from Sia’s hand. Sia gripped the report tight and didn't let go.

"I saw it. I saw everything."
"…"
"Why didn't you say anything? You knew everything… You were trying to protect me… Why act like the bad guy? Why didn't you say a single word of excuse?"

At Sia’s question, Doyun’s action stopped. The moonlight coming through the window illuminated his face. The rims of his eyes were red and bloodshot.

"If I said it."

Doyun’s voice cracked.

"If I said it, you would hurt. I didn't want to dig up the wound you wanted to cover. I wanted to protect your pride."

It was sincere. This man was considerate of Sia and loving her with all his heart from beginning to end.

Sia stood up and hugged Doyun tight. Buried her face in his chest.

"I'm sorry… I'm really sorry. Not knowing your heart…"

Doyun didn't push her away. But he didn't hug her back either. His arms hung limply. It seemed his wounds hadn't healed yet.

But Sia could feel it. That his heartbeat transmitted through his shirt was beating as fast and fiercely as hers.

Can broken mirror shards be put back together? Even if her hands were cut and bled, Sia had to put them back. That was the duty of the beloved.
