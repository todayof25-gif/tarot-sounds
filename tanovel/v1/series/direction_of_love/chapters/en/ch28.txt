"What is this?"

Sia held out the receipt with a trembling hand. Her fingers holding the edge of the paper turned white. Moisture already filled the corners of her eyes, looking like they would burst any moment.

Doyun’s eyes widened. A flustered look was evident.

"Where… did you get that?"
"It was in your pocket. While looking for headache medicine…"

Sia’s voice trembled.

"50 million won. Tiffany Diamond Ring. You bought this in one payment, but why didn't you say a word to me? You bought it without even asking for the size."
"Sia-ya, that is…"
"Tell me. Do you have another woman? was Assistant Manager Kim right? Heard you went around looking for hotels. Did you set up another household behind my back?"

Injustice and anxiety burst out at once for Sia, and she screamed.

"Or… are you preparing to break up? Is it a parting gift or something!"

Doyun sighed deeply, then swept his hair back. Guilt for making Sia cry seemed bigger than the despondency of his perfect plan being busted.

"Haa… seriously Lee Sia. A woman like you is really… why does your imagination run only in such a negative direction."

He approached and wiped Sia’s tears with a rough touch.

"Dummy. Where is another woman. It's overwhelming to handle just you alone. For my life, you alone are enough and overflow."
"Then what is it! Why did you hide it! Making a person feel miserable!"
"I did it to surprise you. To give it at the most perfect timing in the world, at a moment no one expected. Don't you know surprise?"

Doyun smirked and held Sia’s hand. His hand was warm and solid.

"Since I got caught, let's go now. My perfect plan is ruined, but to a more meaningful place."

Where Doyun’s car stopped wasn't a fancy hotel, nor a nice restaurant.

It was in front of an old and ordinary convenience store in a deserted alley near their company. An extremely everyday space where one streetlight flickered.

"Here…?"

Sia asked as if puzzled. Tears stopped, but the tip of her nose was still red.

Doyun took something out of the car trunk. It was a transparent vinyl umbrella turned yellow with an old handle.

"Remember? Summer 3 years ago, the day rain poured like crazy here."
"3 years ago?"

Sia traced her memory. 3 years ago was before she joined the company. No, before she even knew Doyun.

"Back then I was full of annoyance in the car. An important meeting was canceled, it was raining, and the AC was broken. I was spitting out curses because I didn't like anything in the world. But some woman was crouching over there."

Doyun pointed to the corner in front of the convenience store, next to the AC outdoor unit.

"She was holding an umbrella over a stray cat. While getting soaked in the rain herself. Not knowing her skirt and shoes were getting wet in muddy water."

Ah.

A memory she had forgotten flashed through Sia’s head like black and white film.

[Meow, meow.]

A kitten trembling wet in the rain, unable to even open its eyes. The day she couldn't bring herself to leave, so she opened the umbrella in her bag to prop over the cat, and ran into the rain covering her head with her bag instead.

"That umbrella you were using then, I gave money to the convenience store part-timer and asked him to keep it. To return it if I ever met the owner of that umbrella again. No, actually I just wanted to keep even a trace of you."

Doyun opened that old umbrella and covered Sia’s head. *Pat, pat.* Instead of the sound of raindrops falling, the breathing of the two filled the inside of the umbrella.

"It was from that day. That the direction of my world was fixed on you. Because you poured down like rain into my dry world."
"Doyun-ssi…"
"When I saw you again at the job interview, I thought my heart would explode. thought it was destiny. This is a chance God gave. So wanting to keep you by my side, I acted childishly and bullied you. Like an elementary schooler anxious for attention."

Doyun’s eyes were also moist. He took a velvet box out of his pocket.

It was the Tiffany box Sia misunderstood.

The box opened, and a dazzling diamond ring glittered in the night air. But in Sia’s eyes, Doyun’s sincerity shone brighter than the ring.

"Lee Sia. My past crush, current partner… and will you become my future wife?"

Doyun knelt on one knee.

"I will become an umbrella blocking all raindrops for the rest of my life. No, I'll get hit by them with you."

Sia nodded instead of answering and hugged Doyun’s neck. Tears flowed down endlessly. This wasn't tears of sadness.

It was emotion for overwhelming happiness, and finally completed destiny. Like a puzzle piece finding its place after going around.

"Yes… yes, I'd love to. I'll be with you. Whether it rains or snows."

Doyun put the ring on Sia’s finger. It fit perfectly. He didn't ask for the size, but he already knew everything about her.

Under the streetlight in front of the convenience store, inside the old vinyl umbrella, the two kissed deeply.

It was a more perfect proposal than a fancy hotel, than any event. A miracle-like moment created by 3 years of time and Doyun’s sincerity.

A star embroidering the night sky twinkled exceptionally bright in the distance. Like that day 3 years ago, and like their future that would unfold from now on.
