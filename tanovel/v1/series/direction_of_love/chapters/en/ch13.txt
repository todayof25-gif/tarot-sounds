"Keep your evening free today. Must. No excuses."

It was a message from Doyun right after she got to work. A dry sentence without a single emoticon, but a solemnity different from usual could be felt between the lines.

'What is it? Did he book a course meal or something?'

Sia couldn't focus on work all morning. Even during meetings, her eyes kept meeting Doyun’s, and every time, he would smile mysteriously. Her heart tickled so much it was driving her crazy.

After work, Doyun’s car passed through the congested Gangbyeonbuk-ro and began to leave downtown Seoul.

The destination was Bukak Skyway. As they drove up the winding mountain road and reached the Palgakjeong Pavilion at the summit, the sparkling night view of Seoul unfolded panoramically beneath their feet.

When they got out of the car, the cool night air brushed her cheeks.

"Wow… Amazing. It's so beautiful."

Sia leaned against the railing and admired the night view. Tens of thousands of lights sparkled like diamond dust scattered, or stars landing on earth. Every time the wind blew, the lights seemed to sway.

"I knew you'd like it."

Doyun stood next to Sia and took off his jacket, draping it over her shoulders. A faint woody and musk scent wafted from the jacket that held his warmth. Sia instinctively pulled the collar tight and inhaled the scent.

"Do you come here often?"
"No, it's my first time. And you're the first person I've come here with."
"Eyy, liar. This is such a famous date course. I heard everyone comes here to see the night view of Seoul."
"It's true. I hate inefficient movements, so I never understood spending time to come to places like this. Traffic jams, crowded."

When Doyun replied with a serious face, Sia burst into laughter. *Pfft.*

"But why did you come today? Enduring the inefficiency you hate so much."
"Because I wanted to show you. You smiling like a child like this."

At his words, Sia’s laughter stopped. Doyun wasn't looking at the night view, but deeply at Sia. As if holding all the lights of the world, his eyes sparkled.

"Sia-ya."

It was the first time. That he called her by name, not 'Ms. Lee' or 'Assistant Manager Lee'.

Her heart dropped with a thud at the two syllables of her name rolled out affectionately.

"Yes…?"
"We started dating, but I felt like I hadn't confessed properly. The order felt reversed, and it bothered me the whole time."
"You did it… in the rain back then. Asked to go out."
"That was a notification. My personality is impatient so I just committed it first. And you were dragged along in confusion."

Doyun took a small velvet box out of his pocket. He opened the box with trembling hands.

It wasn't a flashy diamond ring, but a simple and sophisticated rose gold necklace. The pendant shone in an octagonal shape, resembling 'The Star' from tarot cards.

"This…"
"You said the Star means hope in tarot cards, right? A guide that tells the direction when lost, and hope that shines in the darkest night."
"…"
"To me, you are that."

He went behind Sia and put the necklace on her himself. The moment the cold metal chain touched her skin, his hot fingers brushed the nape of her neck. A shiver ran down her spine at his breath felt on the back of her neck.

"You entered my colorless world and it began to sparkle. Like this night view, it feels like lights are turning on one by one."

Doyun gently turned Sia’s shoulders to face him. His hands cupped Sia’s cheeks.

"Don't be anxious. I asked to keep it a secret to protect you, not to hide you. Because I hate people gossiping and you getting hurt."

It seemed he saw right through Sia’s heart, which was scared of being caught by Department Head Kim yesterday.

"When the time comes, I'll announce it to the world in the coolest way. That the person beside me is Lee Sia. So no one can say a word."
"Doyun-ssi…"
"So, just trust me and follow me. Because I will never let you go. And don't you think about running away either."

Doyun took Sia’s hand and brought it to his lips. The feeling of his lips touching the back of her hand was hot, and even reverent.

"I love you, Lee Sia."

A single phrase heavier and deeper than 'I like you'.

Sia’s eyes reddened. To her, who had only had light flings or relationships full of scars, Doyun’s weighty confession was unfamiliar but an overwhelming emotion. This man is real. He is sincere.

"…Me too. I love you too."

When Sia answered with a choked voice, Doyun smiled brightly and hugged her tight.

His embrace was wide and warm. The cold of the winter wind, and all the anxiety of the world seemed to disappear.

"Now you're really mine."

Doyun whispered in a low voice in Sia’s ear.

"Can I stamp you as completely mine?"

He didn't wait for an answer and lifted Sia’s chin.

This kiss was different from the first kiss in the rain.

It wasn't urgent, yet hot and soft. As if confirming each other's every breath, he delved deep and persistently. The sound of tongue tips tangling and saliva mixing scattered explicitly in the night air.

Doyun’s shadow overlapped with hers against the backdrop of Seoul’s splendid night view.

In this moment, they were the only two people in the world.

The anxiety of secret dating and worries about the future melted away.

Only the certainty toward each other shone like stars in the night sky.

But the two didn't know. That happiness always drags misfortune along like a shadow.

Behind where they stood, inside a black van parked in the dark, someone was watching them.

*Click, click—*

The sound of a high-performance camera shutter quietly cut through the night air. The lens was accurately aiming at the two kissing.

"Finally caught your tail, Cha Doyun."

The man in the driver's seat, Minwoo, was checking the camera screen with a sneer.

"If this bursts, your perfect image is over too. And Sia… will be mine."

His eyes glistened with madness. At the peak of happiness, a shadow full of malice was silently covering them.
