Since that day, a cold wave colder than Siberia raged in Momentum's office.

Employees couldn't even breathe loudly and only walked on eggshells.

"Hey, why is the CEO in such a mood today? It's a complete ice field. I thought I'd freeze to death when I went to get approval."
"Did he fight with Assistant Manager Lee? The atmosphere between them is no joke. I ran into them in the hallway earlier and they didn't even acknowledge each other."
"Don't tell me they broke up? As soon as the dating rumors started?"

Whispering voices came from the pantry, but Sia pretended not to hear and bit her lip as she brewed coffee. Her hands trembled and hot coffee splashed on the back of her hand, but she didn't even feel the sensation.

Sia worked like a machine, like a person whose soul had escaped.

She barely slept, and rice felt like sand in her mouth. In three days, her face had become half its size.

She didn't exchange a single word with Doyun other than what was necessary for work.

Every time their eyes met in the conference room or hallway, her heart dropped, but his gaze was cold beyond dry.

'Did he really… think of me as a toy? Did I look too easy?'

Suspicion bit its own tail and grew into certainty. The kindness, warm eyes, and delicate consideration he showed. Thinking that all of it was acting based on thoroughly calculated 'analysis data' made her feel nauseous.

2 PM, Project Midterm Review Meeting.

It was the worst setting. Doyun, Sia, and even the partner Minwoo gathered in one place.

While Sia presented the branding draft she prepared, Doyun stared at the screen expressionlessly with his arms crossed. Minwoo scanned Sia with a fishy smile from the opposite side.

When the presentation ended, Doyun’s cold vitriol flew in.

"So, is this the best you can do?"
"…Pardon?"
"The analysis is shallow. I don't feel any understanding of the target customer. Do you think just making it pretty is enough, Assistant Manager Lee?"

It was a place where all employees were present. Normally he would have praised and encouraged her, but today he hacked Sia’s proposal to pieces as if he had decided to do so.

"Redo it. At this level, it's a nuisance to our partner Art & Design too. Isn't that right, CEO Kang?"

Doyun asked for Minwoo's agreement. Minwoo giggled as if it was fun.

"Whoa, CEO Cha is too harsh. Making our junior lose spirit. I think it's not bad? It's emotional."

Minwoo's hypocritical defense was more miserable. Sia couldn't lift her head.

Lunch time, Roof Garden.

While Sia was leaning blankly against the railing looking at the sky, the unwanted guest's voice was heard.

"You look terrible. Are you eating properly?"

It was Minwoo. He appeared holding a sandwich and coffee and sat next to Sia.

"I'm not eating. Go away."
"Oppa is doing this because I'm worried. You're done with Cha Doyun, right? It was brutal during the meeting earlier. I told you, he's just playing with you."

A victor's smile hung on Minwoo's face.

"I was right, wasn't I? Now that you know the reality, are you turned off? Second-generation chaebols are all like that. They treat people's hearts like Excel data."
"…It's none of your business."
"Why isn't it. We're going to start over. I'll take you back. The hurt you got from Cha Doyun, I'll heal it all. We have a lot of memories."

Minwoo tried to slyly put his hand on Sia’s shoulder. Sia slapped his hand away roughly like shaking off a bug.

"Don't be mistaken. Even if I'm done with Doyun-ssi, I will absolutely, never meet you again. You are the worst. You're trash."

Sia shot back with venom and went down from the roof, showing the back of her head.

From behind, she heard Minwoo mocking, "You got toxic, Lee Sia. More attractive?" but she ignored it. Her clenched fingernails dug into her palms.

Back at her desk, Sia hardened her resolve.

She didn't have the confidence to endure anymore in this suffocating space, between Doyun who despised her and Minwoo who ridiculed her. Every time she saw him, the memories of love and the wounds of betrayal tangled together and hacked at her heart.

She wrote a document with trembling hands.

[Resignation Letter]

It took 30 minutes to type just those two words.

5 PM.

Sia knocked on Doyun’s door. Her heart felt like it would explode.

"Come in."

Still a dry voice. Sia took a deep breath and opened the door.

Doyun didn't even look up, busy reviewing documents. As if he had no interest in who entered.

"What is it."

Sia placed the white envelope on the desk without a word.

Only then did Doyun’s gaze move from the documents to the envelope, and then to Sia’s gaunt face. His brow furrowed.

"What is this?"
"It's my resignation letter."

Sia’s voice was trembling minutely, but her eyes were firm. Eyes of a person with nowhere else to retreat.

"I will wrap up just this project. I made the handover files, and until a successor is found…"
"Who said you could."

Doyun cut her off and stood up roughly. The chair pushed back, making a sharp *screech*.

"What are you doing right now? I told you I don't have time to accept a child's tantrums."
"It's not a tantrum. It's my sincerity. I can't work looking at your face anymore. I feel like I'm going to die of suffocation."
"If you can't do it, endure it. Even if you suffocate, bear it. You started this."

Doyun grabbed the envelope roughly and tore it right in front of Sia’s eyes.

*Rip— Riiip—*

The sound of paper tearing cut sharply through the silent office. White paper pieces scattered to the floor like snow.

"I can't accept this resignation. No, I won't."
"CEO! This is tyranny!"
"Don't run away. Don't hide cowardly either. Work by my side, doing as I say. Until you pay for your sins, until I let you go, you can't take a single step as you please."

Doyun gripped Sia’s shoulders tight. A grip strong enough to hurt.

A chilling obsession lingered in his shaking eyes.

It was no longer sweet love. It was cold anger twisted with betrayal and love-hate.
