The air inside the study was pulled taut.

Chairman Cha, Doyun, and Sia. The gazes of the three were focused on one Tarot card placed on the table.

[JUDGEMENT].

A strong image where people rise from coffins and cheer as Archangel Gabriel blows a trumpet under a gray sky.

"Judgment, huh…"

Chairman Cha muttered, twisting the corner of his mouth. His eyes over the glasses shone sharply.

"Does it mean I judge and condemn you guys? Discussing punishment in front of me impudently?"

Doyun’s fist clenched tight. He tried to raise his body as if wanting to take Sia out immediately.

But Sia held Doyun’s hand tight under the table. Her hand was trembling minutely, but her eyes did not avoid and stared straight at Chairman Cha.

"No, Chairman. In Tarot, 'Judgment' does not mean simple punishment."
"Then?"
"This means 'resurrection', 'reunion', and 'decision'. The time when dead relationships awaken. It means the time has come to break with the ways of the past and respond to a new calling."

Sia swallowed dry saliva. She stared at the wrinkles deeply dug in Chairman Cha's brow. As if reading the loneliness and regret hidden in those wrinkles.

"You are standing at a crossroads now, Chairman. Whether to remain as a cold 'Judge' controlling and oppressing your son like the past, or to acknowledge your son's choice and resurrect as a true 'Family'."
"…What?"
"This card is asking. Will you lock yourself in the coffin of stubbornness, or will you listen to the trumpet sound and wake up?"

Heavy silence flowed in the study.

Doyun stopped breathing. He sharpened all nerves fearing his father would be enraged and throw the teacup.

But Chairman Cha didn't move a muscle. He glared at Sia for a long time, then slowly lifted the teacup to his mouth.

*Clack.* The sound of the teacup placed on the saucer rang exceptionally loud.

"…Impudent."

Chairman Cha recited low. An unknown emotion was mixed in his voice instead of anger.

"Very impudent. Looking straight into my eyes and trying to teach me about my life."
"I apologize. I had to tell you the truth the card shows."

When Sia bowed her head, Chairman Cha gave a scoffing laugh. *Pfft.*

"I'm not interested in fortune telling. Superstition can't change my life."

Chairman Cha leaned back and stared piercingly at Sia.

"But, your eyes. I like those."
"Pardon?"
"Doyun only knows numbers and data. He doesn't know how to read people's insides. That's why he goes around getting stabbed in the back by swindler-like guys."

Chairman Cha's gaze glanced at Doyun then returned to Sia.

"But you have a talent for seeing the invisible. You're the first one to come at me trying to read my insides, not my shell."

It was an acknowledgment of Sia’s 'insight' penetrating the essence of people, not her Tarot skills.

His animalistic sense as a businessman reacted. A calculation that this kid could fill the lacking parts of my son.

"Doyun needs someone like you who reads the board differently by his side. That thing called 'intuition', it would be quite useful when doing business."

Chairman Cha gestured to leave.

"Go. I have a headache. It's hard to listen anymore."
"Father… are you permitting it?"

Doyun asked urgently as if he couldn't believe it.

Instead of answering, Chairman Cha picked up the thick approval file again. One word thrown indifferently. *Thud.*

"Don't invite too many reporters to the wedding. I hate noisy things. And… if rumors spread that the daughter-in-law is a fortune teller, stocks drop, so don't go around reading fortunes anywhere, only read in front of me."

It was a de facto approval.

"…Thank you, Father."

Doyun bowed 90 degrees deeply. Sia followed and bowed her head.

As soon as they got into the car leaving the mansion, Doyun hugged Sia tight.

"Haa… seriously, a woman like you is…"

His heart was beating madly as if it would burst out of his ribcage.

"I really thought my heart was going to explode. Trying to teach Father there. Did your liver come out of your belly? (Did you lose your mind?)"
"I didn't teach, I interpreted. The Chairman's eyes… looked too lonely."

Sia exhaled a breath of relief in Doyun’s arms. Her hands trembled only after the tension released.

"Actually I was super scared too. My back is drenched in cold sweat right now."
"I saw. Saying everything you wanted while trembling. I thought I'd go crazy because it was so commendable."

Doyun kissed Sia’s lips deeply.

Kissing in the narrow car in front of the Seongbuk-dong mansion was more thrilling than usual with liberation and joy.

They crossed the biggest mountain. Now nothing seemed to block their way.

But destiny's prank wasn't over yet.

What was waiting for them arriving at the company lobby wasn't congratulatory greetings, but an ominous 'Black Box'.

"CEO! We have a big problem. A package from an unknown sender… arrived for Ms. Sia."

The chief secretary's urgent voice.

On top of the box, a single sentence was written in red letters.

[False happiness does not last long.]
