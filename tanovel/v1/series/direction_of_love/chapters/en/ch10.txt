The car was silent. Only the rhythmic sound of the wipers filled the quiet.

"Are you cold?"

Doyun asked gently, turning up the heater.

"Ah, I'm fine."

Sia shook her head, but her body was trembling slightly. A chill seeped in as her wet clothes dried, but the bigger reason was the man sitting next to her.

That burning gaze in the parking lot earlier. That low, cracked voice saying he 'couldn't stand it anymore.'

Her heart was jumping around on its own, refusing to calm down.

They arrived at the underground parking lot of Doyun’s officetel. A space both familiar and strange.

"I'll just go up and change. You'll catch a cold if you go home wet, so wear my clothes. You can dry off with a hairdryer."
"Ah, yes… thank you."

His house again. But this time, there was no excuse like 'urgent work' as before. It was time solely for the two of them.

As soon as they entered the officetel, Doyun brought a towel from the bathroom and handed it to Sia.

"Dry off a bit. I'll change quickly and come out."

He went into the dress room, and Sia sat on the sofa, shaking out her wet hair.

The wine bar in the corner of the living room, modern lighting, and the scent of bergamot diffuser spreading softly. Everything was like Cha Doyun. Neat, sophisticated, and a space that looked somewhat lonely.

A moment later, Doyun came out wearing comfortable gray home wear. His wet hair was roughly swept back, making him look even sexier. He held two cups of warm cocoa.

"Drink this. To warm up."
"Thank you."

As the sweet and warm cocoa went down her throat, the tension seemed to ease a little.

Doyun sat right next to her, not across. Along with the feeling of the leather sofa sinking, his warmth came over her. Sia inhaled sharply.

"Ms. Lee."
"Yes?"
"Shall we stop now?"

Her heart sank. Her hand holding the cup trembled.

"What? Stop… what?" Don't tell me he wants to quit now? Did he get tired because I played hard to get?

"Some relationship. Testing waters. Confusing each other."

Doyun set his cup on the table and turned to Sia. His eyes looked desperate beyond serious.

"You know I'm impatient. I hate beating around the bush. I can't wait either."
"…"
"Running in the rain today, I suddenly had this thought. Ah, even running miserably in the rain with this woman is this good, how good would it be to eat together, watch movies, and walk holding hands?"
"CEO Cha…"
"Lee Sia. Let's date. None of that contract stuff, just a real relationship like everyone else."

There was no fastball like this. Sia was speechless at the confession that pierced straight through to her heart.

Doyun’s hand slowly cupped Sia’s cheek. His thumb gently swept over her lips.

"If you don't like it, push me away now. I’ll give you 3 seconds."
"…"
"One."

Sia held her breath. She should push him away, but she didn't want to.

"Two."

Doyun’s face came closer. Close enough to see his long eyelashes.

"Three."

Time seemed to stop. Instead of pushing him away, Sia slowly closed her eyes. A silent signal that she accepted, no, that she wanted it.

Reading that signal, Doyun’s lips captured Sia’s.

A soft but intense kiss. The scent of sweet cocoa and rain, and Doyun’s unique cool scent mixed together, making her dizzy.

He wrapped his arm around the back of her neck, tilting his head to deepen the kiss.

"Mmm…"

A faint moan escaped Sia’s mouth. Doyun pulled away for a moment, gasping for breath, then swallowed her up hotly again.

It was the moment the first spark exploded.

Now, there is no turning back. We became real 'Lovers.' Like the prophecy of the card that fell that night.
