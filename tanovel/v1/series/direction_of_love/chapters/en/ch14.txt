They say women in love get prettier, and Sia faced a stranger in the mirror every day these days.

Her dull skin was flushed with color, and her eyes, always weary with fatigue, were full of life. People at the company also threw a word or two as they passed by.

"Assistant Manager Lee, did something good happen these days? Did you change your cosmetics? Your face is blooming."
"Right, your vibe is different these days. Are you dating? Must have met someone good?"

Every time that happened, Sia laughed awkwardly and waved her hands.

"Ah, no. I guess I'm just sleeping well these days. Taking some vitamins too."

Actually, she barely slept. Talking to Doyun until dawn every night, and going on dates with him every weekend, her stamina was at rock bottom, but she was holding on with mental strength. The power of love was truly great. Adrenaline was beating caffeine.

Doyun surprisingly had a doting side.

He tormented Sia with the work messenger whenever he had a chance.

[CEO Cha Doyun: (No emoji) Assistant Manager Lee Sia, come to the pantry for a moment.]
[Lee Sia: (Flustered) What is it? I'm in the middle of writing a report.]
[CEO Cha Doyun: Urgent agenda. Need caffeine recharge.]
[Lee Sia: You drank coffee during the meeting just now. Are you addicted to caffeine?]
[CEO Cha Doyun: Not caffeine, Lee Sia addiction. Withdrawal symptoms coming.]
[Lee Sia: …Ugh, goosebumps. I'm not going. What if someone sees.]
[CEO Cha Doyun: If you don't come, I'll go to your desk. Counting 3 seconds. 3, 2, 1.]

'Crazy, seriously!'
Eventually, when she ran to the pantry pretending she couldn't help it, he would be hiding behind the door, hug Sia tight, *smack*, plant a kiss, and disappear like the wind.

"Recharge complete. Let's work."

Only Sia was left standing blankly with a red face. It was an appearance unbelievable for the cold workaholic CEO Cha Doyun. Who knew this man had so much aegyo.

But sweet times were always short. The calm before the storm was always quiet and peaceful.

2 PM, All-hands meeting.

The air in the conference room subsided heavily, unlike usual. Doyun’s expression at the head of the table was also stiff. The playful appearance from the morning was nowhere to be found.

"Alright, please pay attention. Today's meeting will double as a kick-off meeting with an external partner. It's the partner firm that will handle visual consulting for our brand renewal project this time."

Doyun gestured toward the door with a stiff face.
"Come in."

The conference room door opened, and a man dressed neatly in a navy suit walked in confidently.

Tall height, good-natured eyes, a smile that looked friendly. A handsome man at a glance, but Sia knew the fishiness hidden behind that smile.

As soon as Sia saw the man, she dropped the pen she was holding. *Clatter.*

"Hello. I am Kang Minwoo, CEO of 'Art & Design'. It is an honor to work with Momentum this time. I look forward to working with you."

It was Minwoo.

Sia’s college senior and painful first love, who had a nerve war with Doyun in Yangpyeong. And the unwanted guest who expressed inferiority toward Doyun.

Why is he here?

Sia’s shaking eyes met Minwoo’s. Minwoo looked at Sia and smiled, raising one corner of his mouth. A relaxed smile as if he had expected this situation, no, as if he was enjoying it.

"I think I'll be collaborating closely with Assistant Manager Lee Sia in this project especially. I look forward to it, junior. No, are we partners now?"

Minwoo approached, offering a handshake. Sia’s whole body froze, and she couldn't reach out her hand.

At that moment, Doyun stood blocking the way between them. His voice was dripping with chill.

"Take a seat, CEO Kang. Meeting time is precious. Do the handshake later."

The blade was standing in Doyun’s eyes. A taut tension hovered in the conference room.

Employees who didn't know what was going on held their breath and only read the room at the bloody nerve war between the two CEOs.

The meeting was like hell.

Minwoo threw questions at Sia openly or gave off private nuances, making her difficult.

"What do you think, Assistant Manager Lee? Looking back at school days, your sense was extraordinary. That graduation project was quite impressive too."
"Ah… yes, I…"
"CEO Kang. Please refrain from personal chat. This is a public setting."

Doyun cut him off coldly, unable to stand it, but Minwoo retorted slyly.

"I'm talking about work, CEO Cha. Isn't building rapport with a partner an extension of work too? You're being too uptight."

After the meeting, the conference room where everyone had left.

Sia couldn't get up because her legs were weak, and she was slumped down. Doyun approached and held her shoulders.

"Are you okay? Your face is pale."
"Doyun-ssi… that's not a coincidence, right? How did Sunbae know…"
"He approached on purpose. I should have blocked it when they entered the bid for our company, but the board pushed for it, so I couldn't help it. They said Art & Design's portfolio was so good."

Anger and self-reproach were written on Doyun’s face. He clenched his fists.

"Don't worry. I'll take care of it. I'll block him from approaching you privately. Just endure it until the project is over."

But Doyun’s pledge was futile, misfortune was starting in a more secret and fatal place.

That night, after work, an anonymous message arrived on Sia’s phone upon returning home.

*Ding—*

[Caller ID Restricted]

[Photo]

'Who is it?'

Sia, who checked the photo without thinking, started to tremble. She almost dropped her phone.

It was a high-definition photo of her kissing Doyun at Bukak Skyway yesterday. The two faces were shown too clearly.

And a line of text followed.

[Background is good, models are good. You filmed quite a movie.]
[What would happen if this circulates on the company bulletin board or industry gossip papers? Our junior Sia, it'll be hard to go to work. CEO Cha will be quite troubled too.]

It was Minwoo. This was clear blackmail.

Sia covered her mouth, but couldn't stop the scream leaking out. Fear wrapped around her whole body.

Should I tell Doyun? But if Doyun knows, he won't leave Minwoo alone, and then Minwoo might hold a grudge and spread the photo. Or Doyun might make things bigger with his hot temper.

Doyun’s honor, the company's image, and our relationship. Everything seemed about to collapse in an instant.

The sweet dream broke, and cruel reality began to choke her.

'What do I do… what do I do…'
