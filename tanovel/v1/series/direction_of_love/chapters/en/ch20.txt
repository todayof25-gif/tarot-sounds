A high-end hotel bar in Gangnam.

Under the flashy chandelier lighting, people were talking in low voices, but Sia couldn't hear anything.

She squeezed the burner phone in her coat pocket with a trembling hand. The call button was already pressed.

'Doyun-ssi, you're listening, right? Please…'

Her heart fluctuated as if to burst, but she tried to calm her breathing and headed to the room the staff guided her to.

Corner seat of the VIP room where darkness settled heavily, Minwoo was sitting arrogantly with crossed legs. A half-empty whiskey bottle was already on the table.

"You came? Came faster than I thought. Our Sia, did you miss me that much?"

He shook his glass, clinking the ice. A fishy smile flowed down his face like a snake.

Sia sat opposite him with a pale face. All cells in her body screamed to run away, but she didn't back down.

"Take down the articles. I came as promised."
"So impatient. Have a drink first. It's a celebration drink. For us meeting again, and you returning to your place."

Minwoo pushed a glass of strong whiskey in front of Sia. Sia didn't even look at the glass.

"Don't touch Doyun-ssi. Make sure the company, and his honor too. What you want is me."
"Ha, Cha Doyun, Cha Doyun. Aren't you sick of it? You still take that bastard's side."

Minwoo's expression distorted in an instant. What lingered in his eyes was not simple obsession, but deep inferiority.

"Do you know how much that bastard ruined you? You should be grateful to me. I took off that hypocritical mask."
"No, the one wearing a mask is you."

When Sia glared with clear eyes, Minwoo frowned.

"Did you think I wouldn't know? In college, you made me a betting subject. Played with me. I know everything."
"Hoh, Cha Doyun must have spilled everything? Yeah, that's right. So what?"

Minwoo laughed shamelessly instead of being flustered.

"So what are you going to do? Anyway, you are in front of me now. And that great CEO Cha couldn't protect you."
"Why did you do it? Why do you do this to Doyun-ssi?"
"He's annoying. A bastard who has everything from birth. Born with a silver spoon without effort, everyone bowing to him… I worked myself to death to get here, but that bastard is number one just by breathing."

Minwoo threw the liquor glass to the floor. *Crash—* The shattering sound rang inside the room.

"So I wanted to take it. What that bastard cherishes, I wanted to destroy it all! Touching you was for that reason too. The way he looked at you was unusual."
"You are… truly a monster."
"Shut up! The monster isn't me, it's that bastard!"

Minwoo jumped up and approached Sia. Sia stepped back at the threatening momentum but was blocked by the wall.

"You just have to stay by my side. Quietly, like a doll. Watch clearly in my arms how Cha Doyun crumbles."

His rough hand gripped Sia’s shoulder.

It was then.

*Bang—!*

The heavy soundproof door opened as if breaking, and men in black suits stormed in. And at the lead, stood Cha Doyun.

His face was pale white with rage.

"Take those dirty hands off."

It was a low, cold voice boiling up like a reaper from hell.

"C-Cha Doyun? How are you here…"

Minwoo panicked and took his hand off.

Instead of answering, Doyun closed the distance in an instant and threw a punch at Minwoo's face.

*Thwack—!* A dull sound of flesh and bone colliding. Minwoo rolled on the floor without even screaming.

"Ugh!"
"I warned you. If you touch my person, I'll kill you."

Doyun grabbed the fallen Minwoo by the collar and raised his fist again. Eyes that completely lost reason.

"CEO! Stop! Police are coming! You'll kill him!"

The head of the security team who followed urgently tore Doyun away.

Soon, police stormed in with siren sounds. The room became chaotic.

"Mr. Kang Minwoo, we arrest you for special intimidation, defamation, and past sexual violence and illegal filming charges."
"What? Let go of this! Do you know who I am! Call my lawyer!"

Minwoo struggled, but cold handcuffs were placed on his wrists. The conversation content transmitted through the phone Sia left on became decisive evidence.

Even while being dragged away, he screamed at Doyun.

"Cha Doyun! Just watch! Do you think I'll stay still?!"

Doyun looked down coldly at Minwoo like a bug and loosened his tie roughly.

"I'll let you rot in prison for life. Look forward to it."

After Minwoo was dragged away and the silence flowed in the place where the storm swept through.

Panting roughly, Doyun approached Sia who was trembling.

Sia was slumped on the floor as her legs gave out.

"Doyun-ssi…"
"You foolish woman!"

Doyun yelled. At the loud resonance, Sia flinched and shrank her shoulders.

"Did I tell you to come or not! I told you to stay hidden behind me! Why did you come here alone fearlessly! Not knowing what that crazy guy would do!"

His voice was trembling with anger, but the rims of his eyes were wet red.

He hugged Sia tight. A hug strong enough to crush. As if to erase Minwoo's traces left on her body.

"If something went wrong with you… If anything happened to you… I would have really gone crazy. I would have died."

In Doyun’s arms, Sia burst into tears she had held back. Abs flowing out as tension released.

"I was scared… sob, that you would get hurt… that the company would be ruined…"
"Fool. Does the company matter? Does honor matter? Without you, I need nothing. It's all just scraps of paper."

Doyun didn't let go of Sia for a long time.

The sound of his heart pounding through his shirt was transmitted to Sia’s chest. It was relief that she was alive, and a vow that he would never lose her again.

All misunderstandings were resolved, and the nightmare-like shadow disappeared.

Now there was nothing blocking the two.

But in the place where the storm passed, another flower was preparing to bloom.

A flower named 'Trust', harder and deeper.
