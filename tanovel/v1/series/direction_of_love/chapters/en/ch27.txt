"Judge, I have a question."

Doyun raised his hand with a voice settled coldly. The hall became quiet in an instant.

The presenter from Vertex grabbed the microphone, hiding his flustered look.

"Yes, CEO Cha. It seems the presentation time is over to call it a question…"
"You must have admired our design? Since it seems you referred to it very impressively."

Doyun smirked and pressed the remote control.

"I will borrow the screen for a moment."

Vertex's 'Golden Tower' design blueprint and another blueprint overlapped and appeared on the screen.

"The blueprint you see now is our Momentum's initial discarded draft, Plan B. The left is Vertex's final plan."

People started murmuring. The two blueprints were identical like twins. Whispering sounds filled the hall.

"What is this? It's the same?"
"Did Vertex copy it?"

Vertex CEO Kang jumped up from his seat.

"This is slander! It's just a coincidence! As trends are similar…"
"Coincidence, huh."

Doyun cut him off.

"We prepared just for that. Evidence that it can't be a coincidence."

When Doyun pressed the button one more time, red lines connected over the complex design blueprint and revealed a huge pattern.

Lines connecting the building's columns, beams, and landscape positions were accurately drawing a 'Star' shape. It was a unique pattern embodying the Tarot card [THE STAR].

"If you connect the structure of this building, a 'Star' shape comes out accurately. It is a 'watermark' our designer Lee Sia planted as her signature. It is a structural aesthetic we intended from the design stage."

Doyun’s gaze stuck towards the Vertex presenter.

"Vertex side designer, if you designed it yourself, could you explain what the structural meaning of this star shape is? Why you had to drive a column in that specific position?"
"Ah, th-that is… for structural stability… w-well…"

The presenter became mute as if having eaten honey. Cold sweat trickled down his forehead.

He couldn't have imagined such a mark was hidden in the stolen blueprint.

That was undeniable evidence of plagiarism, no, clear 'theft'.

"You probably can't explain. Of course. Because a thief can't know the meaning of the stolen item."

At Doyun’s shout, judges clicked their tongues.

"My god, submitted something stolen from others?"
"Vertex, caught properly this time. Worthy of expulsion from the industry."

Evaluation scores didn't even need to be seen. Numbers close to '0' were stamped on Vertex's scoreboard.

Sia went up to the podium confidently. The spotlight shone on her.

"This is our Momentum's 'Real' proposal."

The screen changed, and Sia’s final design was revealed.

Different in dimension from Vertex's flashy shell, a warm and innovative design where nature and people harmonize. A 'soul' that the stolen fake couldn't imitate was contained in it.

Everyone held their breath and stared at the screen. They seemed to have even forgotten to applaud at the overwhelming skill difference.

The result was unanimous. Momentum's landslide victory.

"Congratulations, Sia-ya! You did it!"

As soon as they came out of the waiting room, Doyun hugged Sia and lifted her up. He spun her around without caring about surrounding gazes.

"Kyaa! CEO, people are watching!"
"Let them watch! We won, what's the problem!"
"We won! Did you see those people's expressions? They turned completely pale…!"

Sia cheered like a child and hugged Doyun’s neck. It was a moment where the mental suffering and fatigue of all-night work melted away like snow.

***

That night, a company-wide get-together was held at a high-end meat restaurant in Gangnam.

"For Momentum! Cheers!"

Intoxicated with the joy of victory, glasses went back and forth incessantly.

Doyun was busy receiving congratulatory greetings from employees, and Sia was catching her breath in a corner seat for a moment.

Then, Assistant Manager Kim, who was heavily drunk, sneaked up next to Sia.

"Kuh, wasn't the CEO really cool today? That charisma earlier, wow…"
"Yes, he was really cool."
"But Assistant Manager, isn't the CEO a bit strange these days?"

Assistant Manager Kim lowered his voice and whispered secretly.

"Yes? What about him?"
"No, the project is over, but he keeps going somewhere, you see? I saw him earlier too, talking on the phone saying 'hotel reservation' or 'size'. And rumors say he was witnessed at a department store jewelry corner during work hours a few days ago…"

Assistant Manager Kim narrowed his eyes and giggled.

"Maybe he got a woman? He shouldn't be cheating leaving our Assistant Manager behind~"
"Ey, no way."

Sia laughed it off, but strength entered her hand holding the glass.

An uneasy feeling spread like ink in a corner of her heart.

From a few days later, Doyun’s behavior really became suspicious.

He, who used to leave work sharply on time, was late every day with the excuse of overtime, and often went out alone on weekends saying "important meeting".

Even the appearance of hurriedly turning off the screen when Sia approached while looking at his phone, or going out to the veranda to answer calls.

'Don't tell me… no, right? How long has it been since we resolved things.'

Trauma suffered from Minwoo crept up.

Curse-like words of Minwoo, 'Love changes', 'Men are all the same', lingered in her head.

Suspicion grew like a poisonous mushroom and gnawed at Sia’s mind.

Then one weekend.

While Doyun went in for a shower for a moment, his jacket was placed on the sofa.

"Ah, should I chew some gum."

Sia rummaged through his pocket inadvertently. She just intended to find Xylitol gum Doyun enjoyed.

But what touched her fingertips wasn't a gum container, but a crumpled piece of paper.

It was a receipt.

[S Jewelry / Tiffany Diamond Ring / 5,000,000 Won]
[Date: October 24, 2024 (Thu) 14:30]

'Ring?'

Her heart dropped with a thud. Her hands trembled.

The date was last Thursday. The day Doyun said he was "busy with client meetings all day".

And decisively, Doyun didn't know Sia’s finger size. (At least Sia knew so.)

Even more, Sia only wore platinum because of metal allergies, but the receipt item code was general gold.

'It's not for me.'

The moment conviction struck, her vision went dark.

'It was a lie. He got another woman. Not knowing that, I thought about marriage alone…'
Worst imaginations unfolded in her head. All moments that were happy felt like a mirage and tears welled up.

Just then, the bathroom door opened, and Doyun came out wiping off water.

"Huh? Sia-ya, why are you standing there?"

An innocent expression knowing nothing. Seeing that face smiling while dripping water drops from his hair, Sia’s sorrow exploded.
