Saturday, a cafe in Yangpyeong. It was supposed to be a field trip, but it was practically a date.

Doyun listened intently to Sia’s words throughout the drive. Even when she mumbled trivia like, "This cafe has an 'Empress' vibe," he nodded in agreement.

"Then are you the Empress, Ms. Lee?"
"Eh, I'm still just a Page."
"You look like a Queen to me."

She was getting tired of blushing at his casual flirty remarks.

They were having coffee on the outdoor terrace when a man's voice came from the opposite table.

"Huh? Lee Sia?"

Sia looked up. It was Kang Minwoo, her college senior and former crush.
The man who had left her with nothing but scars three years ago when he went to study abroad.

"My god, seeing you here? It’s been ages."
"Oh, Sunbae…"

Sia’s expression stiffened. It wasn't welcoming; it was embarrassment and old pain that hit her first.

Minwoo plopped down next to Doyun, completely ignoring him.

"You got prettier. What do you do these days? Got a boyfriend?"
"Just… working at a company."
"Really? Let's grab a meal sometime. You used to like me, remember?"

Minwoo cackled. Sia gripped her teacup tight. Bringing that story up here, in front of Doyun?

*Clack.*

Doyun set his teacup down sharply.

"Excuse me."

A low, chilling voice. The air on the terrace froze.

"Could you please leave? We are in the middle of work."
"Ah, is he a coworker? I was just glad to see her. But working on a weekend seems a bit harsh, doesn't it? Sia has a weak body."
"You seem to know Ms. Lee well."
"I know her very well. We stuck together for four years. Sia cried quite a lot because of me."

Minwoo bragged like it was a war story. Sia couldn't lift her head.

Doyun slowly stood up. His shadow loomed over Minwoo.

"Is making a woman cry something to be proud of?"
"What?"
"You should be embarrassed. A grown man like you."

Minwoo’s face turned red.

"C-CEO Cha… stop it."
"Ms. Lee."

Doyun glared at Minwoo as he spoke.

"You cried because of a guy like this? Your standards were quite low."
"…"
"Hey, look here!"
"And."

Doyun snatched Sia’s hand and pulled her up.

"The person beside Ms. Lee right now is me. You seem drunk on past glory, so get lost."

Doyun didn't give him a chance to retort and dragged Sia out. His strides were fast all the way to the parking lot.

He stopped only when they reached the car.

"CEO Cha, are you angry?"
"…"

Doyun roughly swept his hair back.

"I am angry."
"I'm sorry. He’s just oblivious…"
"It’s not because of that bastard."

Doyun turned to face Sia. His eyes were shaking.

"I'm angry that you cried over a guy like that, and I'm insanely jealous that he has a part of your time that I don't know about."
"What…?"
"I said I'm jealous. Scared I might lose you to a stranger."

Doyun grabbed Sia’s shoulders.

"Lee Sia. Don't think about other men in front of me. Past or present."
"…"
"From now on, look only at me. I'll cover up all your memories of him."

The confession of a man blinded by jealousy was more intense than any other words.

Sia’s heart dropped with a thud. This time, it was truly dangerous.
