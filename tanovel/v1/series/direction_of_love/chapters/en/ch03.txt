"…CEO Cha, is this your house?"
"No."
"Then why…"
"This way has less traffic."

Liar. It was 11 PM. There was no such thing as traffic.

Sia gripped the handle tight. The only sound in the car was the rain. Since the 'Coat Incident,' the air between them had shifted subtly. Even the veins on the back of his hand gripping the steering wheel made her nervous.

"Um, about what you said earlier."
"Which part? Destiny? Or the part about me becoming your destiny?"
"…Yes, that."

Sia answered in a crawling voice. Doyun glanced at her through the rearview mirror.

"I meant exactly what I said. Interpretation is free."
"But you’re against office romance."
"Let me correct that. 'Unnecessary' romance is the problem. A relationship that provides positive stimulation is very welcome."
"…What kind of sophistry is that?"

"We're here."

The car stopped in front of Sia’s house.

"Thank you for the ride."
"Ms. Lee."

Doyun caught her just as she was about to get out.

"Clear your schedule for tomorrow morning."
"What? Tomorrow is the weekend."
"I know. But I think we need to overhaul this project completely. I just had a very powerful inspiration."

Doyun looked at her with meaningful eyes.

"To flesh out that inspiration, I need you, Ms. Lee. Tomorrow morning, 10 AM. Come to my place."
"…What?! Your place?"
"Running the office on a weekend is a waste of resources. I’ll make you good coffee, so don't be late."

He turned the car around without waiting for an answer. Sia stood there blankly watching his taillights fade.

Weekend? His house? Just the two of them?
It was clearly a 'dangerous proposal.' But she had neither the justification nor the courage to refuse. No, honestly… she hated herself for feeling a little expectant.

***

Next morning, 10 AM. Doyun’s officetel.

*Ding-dong.*

"You're here?"

The door opened, revealing Doyun in a gray hoodie. Wet hair hung over his forehead. He looked like a defenseless boy.

Sia inhaled sharply. 'What the… why is he so handsome?'

"Come in. I’ll get the coffee ready."

His home was neat and smelled faintly of citrus.

"Sit over there."

A moment later, Doyun sat right next to her, holding a mug.

"C-CEO Cha? Aren't we a little too close…"
"We need to be this close to look at the screen together. Focus."

He naturally rested his arm on the desk and leaned in. His arm almost brushed against hers.

*Clack, clack.*

Doyun typed on the keyboard, but the letters didn't register in Sia’s eyes. The sound of his breathing right by her ear, the scent of his shampoo, the faint heat radiating from his forearm. All her nerves were focused on him, driving her crazy.

"What do you think about this part?"

Doyun suddenly turned his head. Sia turned instinctively too.

Their noses almost touched. Doyun’s gaze dropped to Sia’s lips.

"You're not focusing, Ms. Lee. You're thinking about something else."
"N-No! It’s just… we’re too close."
"Do you dislike being close?"

Doyun swiveled his chair to face her completely. Silence. A taut tension filled the air.

"I like it. I can even hear you breathing."

He slowly leaned his upper body toward her. Sia squeezed her eyes shut. She should dodge, but her body was frozen. His breath poured hot over her lips.

"…Aren't you going to work?"

Sia squeezed out the words. Doyun chuckled and whispered in her ear.

"I am working. The work of shaking your heart."

Will I be able to survive this dangerous space today?
