Saturday night, 8:45 PM.
The hour of destiny was approaching.
I sat reverently in front of the TV.
In my hand was a Lotto ticket (5,000 won, auto-pick), and beside me was Myoreusin, busy licking a Churu.

"Myoreusin, watch closely. This is the piece of paper that will change your butler's destiny."

"Nya."

He groomed himself as if completely uninterested.
Finally, the draw began.
My heart pounded. *Thump, thump.*
Please... 1st place... Actually, I'd have no regrets even with 2nd place.
If I win 2nd place, I'll quit my job first thing and throw a 365-day Churu party.

First ball.
Number 7.
Oh! It's there! Number 7 is in the first line!

"Nice! Good start!"

Second ball.
Number 15.
It's there!

"Wow, crazy!"

My hands started to shake.
Third ball.
Number 23.
It's there again!
Three in a row.
This is... a sign. A god-given opportunity.

"Hey! Myoreusin! I think I'm gonna win! Oh my god!"

I hugged Myoreusin and shook him.
He looked annoyed and was about to hiss but held back.

Fourth ball.
Number 31.
...Huh?
It's not there.
My ticket has 32.
So close. One miss.
It's okay though. Two numbers left.

Fifth ball.
Number 40.
......Not there.
My ticket has 41.

'Why does it keep missing by just 1?!'

Anxiety began to creep in.
Last ball.
Number 45.
My ticket has... 44.

"......"

Silence.
7, 15, 23, 32, 41, 44.
Only 3 matches.
5th place. Prize money: 5,000 won.
I broke even.

"Aaaaaaaack!!!!"

I rolled on the floor and screamed.
My Gangnam apartment! My resignation letter! My Cat Tower Land!
Every dream popped like a bubble.
It's futile. Life is empty.

"Ha... right. What kind of sudden fortune would befall someone like me."

I slumped down.
Myoreusin approached and rubbed his head against my knee to comfort me. (I want to believe that, but he's probably just scratching an itch.)

"Yeah, I only have you... What use is money when I have you?"

I petted him, trying to console myself.
But his behavior was a bit strange.
He kept tapping something on the floor with his front paw. *Tap, tap.*
What is it? A cockroach?

"What is it, Myoreusin?"

Looking closely, it was a crumpled piece of paper.
Huh? That's...
It was a 'Scratch-off Lottery Ticket' I bought a while ago and shoved into the corner of my bag!
Myoreusin must have rummaged through my bag and pulled it out.

"Ah, right. I had this."

Lotto was a bust, but maybe the scratch-off?
Without much expectation, I found a coin.
And scratched furiously. *Scritch, scratch.*

[Luck] [y] [Prize]

"...Oh?"

Lucky Prize? What's this? Is it 500 million won?
With trembling hands, I checked the prize details.

[It's a 'Dud'. Better luck next time...]

"Dammit!"

I threw the ticket.
Lucky Prize my foot! It's a dud! Are they mocking me?!
Nothing is going right.
I pulled the duvet over my head and lay down.
I should just sleep. Maybe I'll win the lottery in my dreams.

That was when.
*Buzz-*

My phone vibrated.
It was Myoreusin.

[Today's Fortune: True luck lies on the back.]

"The back?"

What are you talking about?
I poked my head out from the duvet.
The ticket I threw was rolling on the floor.
Myoreusin placed a front paw on that ticket and looked at me.
His eyes seemed to say, 'You stupid human.'

"What's on the back?"

I hesitantly got up and picked up the ticket.
And flipped it over.
There was an advertisement printed in fine print on the back.
Normally, I wouldn't have even glanced at it.
But I couldn't ignore Myoreusin's words, so I read it carefully.

[Grand Opening Event!]
[Bring this ticket for a free 'Premium Health Checkup'!]
[- The Friendly Animal Hospital -]

"......!!"

Free health checkup?!
Do you know how expensive cat health checkups are?!
Just the basic tests cost 200,000 to 300,000 won, but Premium... that's at least 500,000 won?!
This is a much bigger gain than 5th place in the Lotto!

"Daebak..."

I couldn't close my mouth.
If it's 'The Friendly Animal Hospital'... that's the handsome vet's clinic next door!
It's close, saves me money, and I get to see the handsome guy's face (that's just a bonus).
Killing three birds with one stone!

"Myoreusin! Are you a genius?"

"Nya."

He swished his tail.
As expect, you were my lucky charm.

"Alright! We're going tomorrow! Let's check your health and uncover the secret of your belly fat!"

I lifted him up and showered him with kisses.
He struggled with a look of disgust, but my mood was already at its peak.
The Lotto was a dud, but I got something better.
Is this a blessing in disguise?

Next morning.
I hummed as I got ready.
Instead of the tracksuit I usually throw on, I made a big decision and wore a dress. (Don't ask why I'm wearing a dress to a vet clinic. Just for... manners... No, actually, I'm just in a good mood.)

"Let's go, Myoreusin!"

I put him in the carrier and left the house.
Waiting for the elevator, my reflection in the mirror looked pretty decent.
I had a feeling something good would happen today.

But I didn't know.
What kind of 'Destiny' I would encounter at that hospital.
And why Myoreusin guided me there.
I didn't realize then that it wasn't just a simple health checkup.

- End of Episode 4 -
