[Today's Fortune: If you go North, you will meet a Noble Person (Gui-in).]
[Additional Info: Fortune comes to those who hold back their laughter.]

That was the fortune Myoreusin gave me on the bus to work this morning.
My company is located to the North. A Noble Person at the company?
"No way."
My company is full of bosses who are more like 'Ghosts' than 'Nobles'. Especially 'Grim Reaper', Department Head Choi.

As soon as I arrived, it was a battlefield.
Everyone was looking for me, and I had to make do with a sandwich for lunch.
Suddenly, I remembered the fortune. North from the office meant the rooftop.
I opened the rooftop door to a wide-open view.
As I leaned against the railing to stretch, I sensed someone in the corner.

'Who is it?'

I instinctively hid behind an outdoor AC unit.
That familiar back. It was Department Head Choi.
He looked around furtively, then took something out of his pocket. A mirror and a comb.
And then... *Swish.*
His hand went to his head.
His seemingly voluminous hair lifted up like a lid. It was a toupee.
His real head shined coolly in the sunlight.

'Daebak...' (Jackpot...)

The man who always said, "Volume is life for a man."
Laughter bubbled up in my throat. Ah, so this is why it said 'Fortune comes to those who hold back their laughter'.
He combed his wig and whispered to it.

"Aigoo, my pretty. Look at that smooth texture."

He's talking to his wig. Just as a snicker was about to escape me...
*Whoosh-!*
A powerful gust of wind blew.

"Uh, uh, uh?!"

The wig in his hand floated up like a dandelion seed.
The heartless wind blew the wig right over the railing.

"Aaaack! That's a 2 million won piece!!"

His scream. Laughter and pity washed over me simultaneously.
I quietly stepped back. If I'm caught, I'm dead.
I was creeping away stealthily when...
*Snap.* I stepped on a dry twig.

"Who's there!"

He spun around. I locked eyes with the bald Department Head.

"......"

"Ah... Have you eaten, sir? The weather is nice. Haha..."

"Assistant Manager Seo... did you see?"

He approached me with the face of a Grim Reaper.

"Yes? See what? I was just looking at the sky."

"My head... you saw it, didn't you?"

"Head? It looks very... cool and nice! Good!"

Oops. Slip of the tongue.
Lasers shot out of his eyes. I ran for the door.

"Stop right there! Seo!!"

A rooftop chase ensued.
Just then, my phone buzzed.

[Mission: Keep the Noble Person's secret.]
[Success Reward: A Diamond-grade Lifeline.]

The Noble Person was the Department Head? The Bald Noble.
I stopped running. Running away wouldn't solve this.
I turned to face him.

"Department Head! I, Haru Seo! Am a woman known for her tight lips!"

"What?"

"I will take what I saw today to the grave! I swear!"

He glared at me with eyes full of suspicion.

"How can I trust you?"

"I will prove it. I will find 'Pretty' that fell into the flowerbed!"

His eyes widened.

"You'll find it?"

"Yes! Without anyone knowing!"

After a moment of thought, he nodded.

"Fine. If you find it... I'll take responsibility for your performance review this time."

Performance Review A? This is the 'Diamond-grade Lifeline'!
I went down to the first floor and searched the flowerbed like a maniac.
30 minutes later. I found 'Pretty' inside a thicket of azaleas.
I returned to the rooftop with a swelling heart.

"Department Head. I have brought her back."

His face brightened as I handed over the wig.
He swiftly put it on. He was back to the voluminous Department Head Choi.

"Ahem. Assistant Manager Seo. About today..."

"I don't remember anything. I had indigestion so I was sleeping."

He smirked.

"You have sense. As promised, look forward to your review. And this is a token of my appreciation."

It was a 100,000 won department store gift certificate. Wow, long live Myoreusin!
My steps were light as I walked back to the office.
The hellish company suddenly seemed bearable.

On the way home.
I stopped by the convenience store in a good mood. I bought Myoreusin's snacks, and the lottery tickets by the counter caught my eye.
My luck is good today... maybe?

"Unni, give me 5,000 won worth of Lotto, please."

I took the slip with a fluttering heart. What should I do if I win 1st place?
I hummed a tune as I returned home.

"Myoreusin~ The butler is home!"

But Myoreusin was sprawled out on the bed.

"Hey, I earned money and you don't even say hello?"

"Nya."

[You're here.]

So dismissive. He only ate when I served the snacks.

I washed up and reverently placed the Lotto ticket on the dining table.
I fell asleep imagining a happy future.
But I didn't know.
That Myoreusin was looking at my Lotto ticket with a meaningful expression.
And that his front paw... was moving ominously.

- End of Episode 3 -
