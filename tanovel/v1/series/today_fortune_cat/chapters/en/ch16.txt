The car ride back home was quiet.
Only the sound of the wipers and the rain *pitter-pattering* filled the air rhythmically.
I sat in the back seat, holding Myoreusin tight in my arms. Exhausted, he buried his face in my chest and slept dead to the world.
Warm, heavy, and breathing rhythmically.
He's alive. My cat, my family, is alive.

"......Rest assured. Nothing will happen now."

Si-woo in the driver's seat spoke while looking at me through the rearview mirror.
His voice wasn't the chilly 'beast's' voice from earlier. It was back to the soft and gentle Dr. Si-woo I knew.

"Yes... Thank you. Really, if it weren't for you..."

I couldn't finish my sentence and choked up, so he handed me a tissue.

"Don't cry. It's not your fault, Haru-ssi."

But the anxiety didn't completely go away.
The man's last words. The threat that Myoreusin was 'legally his property'.
Certification of contents? Lawsuit?
How could an ordinary office worker like me handle such things? And what about the lawyer fees?
Realistic worries swirled in my head.

*Ding-dong-*

At that moment, Si-woo's phone rang.
He answered via Bluetooth.

"Oh, Hyung. It's me."

Hyung? Family?

"What I asked for earlier. Did you pass it to Reporter Kim? Yeah. Illegal experiment data and recording files, everything. Violation of Animal Protection Act plus embezzlement... It's prime material for tonight's main news."

The corners of Si-woo's mouth curled up viciously.
The expression I saw at the abandoned building earlier flashed by.

"Yeah, blow it up right now. File a complaint under the law firm's name too. No settlement. We have to bury him socially. Cost? Put it on my tab."

"......!!"

My eyes widened.
Law firm? Put it on his tab?
When the call ended, Si-woo winked at me.

"It's resolved."

"Huh? That call just now..."

"Ah, my older brother is a lawyer. He's at a pretty successful law firm, and I had some leverage, so I used him a bit."

He said it nonchalantly, but the content of the call was tremendous.
He instantly prepared a counterattack by completely exposing the opponent.
This man... wasn't he just a neighborhood vet?
Capable and rich? Or maybe crazy rich?

"Um... the cost must be huge, I..."

"I told you. Myoreusin is my family too. Is money important when punishing someone who messed with family?"

He tightened his grip on the steering wheel.

"And the crime of making Haru-ssi cry, that's the biggest one. I'll show him what's what."

My heart sank *thud*.
To deliver a line straight out of a romance novel so calmly.
This man's guilty moments are endless.
*(TN: "Guilty human/man" is a slang for someone whose charm is a crime.)*

* * *

When we arrived home, it was a mess.
Because I rushed out, the front door was open, and rain had blown in, soaking the living room floor.
Above all... the balcony window latch was broken.
The window Myoreusin had opened to leave.

"This... it might be dangerous for you to stay alone, Haru-ssi."

Si-woo examined the broken window and spoke seriously.

"We have to wait until daylight to call a repairman, but it's raining tonight... and that guy might come back."

A chill ran down my spine at those words.
It felt like the man in the black raincoat was watching me from outside the window.
Myoreusin seemed anxious too, sticking close to my legs and refusing to leave.

"What do I do... Should I go to a sauna or something..."

I was about to pack a bag when Si-woo grabbed my wrist.

"Haru-ssi."

"Yes?"

"Um... it's a bit awkward to say in this situation, but."

He scratched the back of his head and avoided my eyes. The tips of his ears were slightly red.

"I have a spare room at my place."

"......Excuse me?"

"No, I don't mean anything weird! Since it's right next door, I can react immediately if something happens, and Myoreusin needs stability, and we have to care for Gureum... so considering everything..."

He rambled excuses.
To see the great Si-woo Kang flustered. It was cute.

"I'd be grateful. But... are you really okay with it? I might be a burden."

"Burden? It's an honor. Actually, my place is a bit too big for living alone. It gets lonely."

He smiled brightly.
A smile clear like the sky clearing after rain.

"Then... I'll impose on you for a bit, Neighbor Oppa."

When I playfully called him 'Oppa', he paused.

"Oppa... I didn't know that word sounded so good. Please use it often."

We packed our things.
Myoreusin, Gureum, change of clothes, and toiletries.
It looked like a couple fleeing by night, or maybe newlyweds going on a honeymoon.
A strange excitement bloomed along with the sound of rain.

We took the elevator one floor... wait, no, we just moved to the immediate next door.
Same front door, but the world inside was completely different.
When he opened the door, a spacious living room, modern interior, and soft diffuser scent welcomed us.
It looked like a model home.

"Welcome. Welcome to my home."

He brought out slippers for me.
Myoreusin walked in confidently as if it were his own house and took a spot right in the middle of the most expensive-looking leather sofa.
As expected, his adaptability is the best in the universe.

"Nyaong."
[Given a house. Pass.]

Myoreusin purred in satisfaction.
I smiled without realizing it.
Yeah, this is heaven.
But I didn't know.
How heart-fluttering and dangerous(?) the first night alone with a man (excluding cats) would be.
And that Si-woo's feelings for me were hidden in every corner of his house.

- End of Episode 16 -
