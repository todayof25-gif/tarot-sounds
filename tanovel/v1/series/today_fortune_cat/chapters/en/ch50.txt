3 years later.

"Hyung! Let's go together!"

Park pouring with warm spring sunshine.
Four year old kid was running waddlingly.
And in front of him cat running rhythmically fluttering elegant fur, Myoreusin was there.

"Nyaang!"
[Slow, kiddo! Run faster!]

Myoreusin looked back glancingly and adjusted speed.
High level technique stopping and waiting if Byeol-i ran dangerously as if to fall, and running away again if chased.

"Wow... Look at that stamina. Really energetic."

Si-woo watching that sitting on mat admired.
Myoreusin looked rather healthier than 3 years ago thanks to 'Project M's supplement.
Fur flowed with gloss, and movement was nimble.

"I know right. Seems stamina is better than Byeol-i."

I leaned on Si-woo's shoulder and drank Iced Americano.
Peaceful.
Last 3 years big and small things happened to our family.

Byeol-i started walking, started speaking, and entered daycare center.
Si-woo's animal hospital rumored as "Vet reading cat's mind" hit jackpot. (Of course the secret was Myoreusin's coaching.)
I finished parental leave and returned to company living fiercely as working mom.

And Myoreusin.
Still reigns as 'King' of our house.

"Mom! Dad! Hyung caught a butterfly!"

Byeol-i ran panting and boasted.
Under Myoreusin's front paw following behind, a yellow butterfly was fluttering.

"Oh, Myoreusin! Hunting skill didn't die?"

When Si-woo praised Myoreusin shrugged and trembled mustache.
Then lifted front paw slightly and let butterfly go.
Butterfly flew to sky *Pal-lang Pal-lang*.

"Bye! Butterfly Bye!"

Byeol-i waved hand.
Myoreusin also looked up at sky quietly until butterfly disappeared.
That side profile was somewhat touching.

"You know, Honey. Sometimes feels like a dream."

"What is?"

"Just everything. Meeting you, becoming family with Myoreusin, birthing Byeol-i... wondering if all is dream."

Actually was so.
Me ordinary office worker, installing suspicious cat app, falling in love with neighbor man, and raising child like this.
Looking back everything was series of miracle-like coincidences.

First meeting with Myoreusin who grabbed my ankle to get one Churu.
Chase with thugs trying to kidnap Myoreusin.
Confession at hospital confirming heart with Si-woo.
And small angel who came to us, Byeol-i.

Nose tip stung at memories passing like kaleidoscope.

Si-woo grabbed my hand tight.
His hand was warm and solid.

"Not a dream. And won't wake up in future too."

"......"

"We will live like this continuously. Myoreusin, Byeol-i, you too. Until we become grandma, grandpa bickering."

His calm confession dug deep into chest.
Tears welled up unconsciously.

"Chi... Who is grandma. I am still youthful?"

"Haha, right. Let's say our hearts are eternal youth."

We looked at each other and laughed.
It was then.

*Ding-*

My phone and Si-woo's phone rang simultaneously.
'Today's Fortune' notification received after really long time.
Recently busy parenting didn't send often.

We checked phone simultaneously.

[Today's Fortune: Forever, Sunny.]
[Interpretation: No dark cloud in my dictionary. I guarantee your future.]
[Advice: So don't worry and just be happy. This is an order.]

"......Pfft."

Laughter burst.
Lifting head, Myoreusin was staring at us intently.

"Nya."
[Understood?]

The guy threw one word chicly, and turned body towards Byeol-i again.

"Hyung! Let's ride slide this time!"

"Nyaaang."
[Annoying really... Ride just this once.]

Myoreusin grumbled and approached Byeol-i.
Whether knowing such Myoreusin's heart or not, Byeol-i held Myoreusin's tail slightly and smiled brightly "Yaong! Hyung best!".
Two people... no, back view of two lifeforms was loo lovely.

Cherry blossom petals scattered in spring breeze.
Child and cat running between pink petals.
And our couple guarding behind them.

Couldn't be better.

"Let's go, Honey. Our Captains are calling."

Si-woo got up from seat.
I also brushed off and got up holding his hand.

"Yes, let's go."

We moved steps towards flower path.

My luck, my family.
Today's fortune, no tomorrow's fortune, day after tomorrow's fortune too.

Will be eternally 'Sunny'.
