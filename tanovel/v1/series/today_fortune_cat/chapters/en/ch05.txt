'The Friendly Animal Hospital'.
Even the sign name is sweet.
I stood in front of the hospital holding the carrier containing Myoreusin.
If not for the free checkup coupon, I wouldn't have dared to visit this premium clinic.

"Let's go, Myoreusin. Don't be scared."

"Nya."

[Who's scared.]

Myoreusin yawned inside the carrier.
*Ding-a-ling-* As I opened the door, a clean white interior and the scent of aroma welcomed me.

"Welcome~ Do you have a reservation?"

"Ah, yes! Um... I'm here to use a winning lottery ticket."

I took out the crumpled ticket from my pocket and handed it over.
The nurse's eyes went round.

"Oh my! You're the first person to actually bring this! The Director made it for fun."

Made it for fun. I almost missed out.
After a short wait, a dog came out of the examination room, followed by him.
Director Si-woo Kang. He looked dandy in his white coat.
Wearing glasses, his intellectual charm exploded.

"Ms. Haru Seo! Please come in."

I carried the carrier into the exam room.
Dr. Si-woo looked up from the chart.

"Where does it hurt... Huh?"

He paused when he saw me.
Then, he flashed a dimpled smile.

"Oh? Convenience store?"

"Ah, yes! Hello! The... Churu..."

"Nice to meet you. Seeing you here."

He walked closer. Up close, his skin was glowing.

"What brings you here? Is he sick?"

"No! I'm here to use this."

When I showed him the ticket, he burst out laughing.

"Pukaka! You're the first person to actually bring this. You're lucky."

"Hehe... My cat found it for me."

"Your cat? Smart friend. Shall we see the protagonist then?"

I took Myoreusin out of the carrier.
The giant calico landed heavily on the examination table. *Thud.*

"Oh... he has a good build. His coat color is unique too..."

He smiled and approached Myoreusin.
But the moment he saw Myoreusin's face head-on, his smile froze.

"......?"

His eyes widened. His pupils trembled.
Like someone who had seen something unbelievable.

"Teacher...?"
*(TN: 'Seonsaeng-nim' - A respectful title meaning Teacher/Master/Doctor.)*

He uttered an unbelievable title.
What? Teacher? Me?
But his gaze was fixed on Myoreusin.

"Is it really... you, Teacher?"

His voice was wet with emotion. It even sounded desperate.
That's when Myoreusin reacted.
He stared straight at Dr. Si-woo. His amber eyes flashed.

"You... remember."

Just as Dr. Si-woo was about to reach out, overcome with emotion...

*Slap-!*

A crisp slapping sound echoed.
Myoreusin had thrown a 'Cat Punch'.

"Ah!"

Dr. Si-woo grabbed his hand and backed away.

"Hiss-!"

Myoreusin puffed up his fur and threatened him.
Shocked, I scooped Myoreusin up into my arms.

"My-Myoreusin! Are you crazy?! I'm so sorry, Doctor! Are you okay?!"

"Ah... Ugh..."

Dr. Si-woo looked more shocked than in pain.

"Are you... rejecting me?"

Rejecting? Me? *Honorifics?*
Is this man playing a character?

"Um, Doctor... do you know this cat?"

My question seemed to snap him out of it.
His eyes returned to normal in an instant.

"Ah... No. I'm sorry. I was mistaken. He looks like someone I used to serve."

Someone he used to serve? A cat?
He pretended nothing happened and started the examination.
But the hand holding the stethoscope was trembling slightly.
Myoreusin didn't take his eyes off Dr. Si-woo either. A cool gaze of warning.

The diagnosis was obesity. Diet urgent.
Dr. Si-woo walked us out after the checkup.

"Um, Butler-nim. Where did you get that cat?"

"I picked him up at the recycling area in front of my house. He seemed like a stray."

"...I see. Please take good care of him. That per... no, Myoreusin."

He quickly changed the subject and went back into the exam room.
What is this uneasy feeling?

In the elevator, I asked Myoreusin.

"Hey, do you know the doctor?"

"Nya."

[Mind your own business.]

"I'm curious! He called you Teacher."

He ignored me and turned away.
But seeing his ears twitch, he's definitely hiding something.

I kept thinking about it even after getting home.
Former owner? Or a lover from a past life?
Then my phone buzzed.

[Fortune Completed: The Door of Secrets has opened.]
[Update: Don't trust that guy too much.]

[That guy needs to taste my claws more.]

The bad blood is real.
What happened between them? And what's with the 'Teacher' title?

I looked out the window at the Animal Hospital Director's office.
Is Dr. Si-woo looking at our house from there too?

That night, I dreamt that Myoreusin was sitting on a cloud wearing a Taoist robe, and Dr. Si-woo was bowing to him from below. Must be a nonsense dream.

Next morning.
I was leaving for work when the door next to mine opened and Dr. Si-woo came out.
We ran into each other.

"Ah, hello..."

I greeted him first.
He flinched when he saw me.
Then, looking at Myoreusin who had come out to see me off behind me, he bowed his head.

"......Good morning, sir."

The greeting wasn't directed at me.
It was to the cat. Very politely.

Wow, crazy.
This man is really committed to his concept.
Or... there's really something going on.
