"I'm home~"

A strange sense of dissonance struck me as soon as I opened the front door after work.
Myoreusin's food bowl, which I had definitely filled this morning, was spotlessly empty.
Usually, he leaves some? Myoreusin has a small appetite and doesn't eat much at once?

"Myoreusin? Were you hungry today?"

"Nya."
[Give me food.]

Myoreusin tapped the empty bowl. *Tap, tap.*
Puzzled, I poured more food.
But the guy ate twice as fast as usual.
He devoured it like a cat that had starved for days.

"Eat slowly. You'll get indigestion."

I tried to pet him, but he flinched and dodged.
And he kept glancing towards the balcony.

'What is it? Is something on the balcony?'

As I tried to open the balcony door, Myoreusin blocked my path.

"Hiss-!"
[No Entry!]

"Why? Did you poop there?"

I gently pushed him aside and opened the door.
An empty balcony. Just the washing machine and recycling bins.
Nothing there.
I must have been sensitive.

* * *

That night.
I woke up thirsty.
I came out to the living room to get a glass of water when I heard a rustling sound.
The sound came from the balcony.
*Rustle.*

'A-A thief?'

My heart pounded. *Thump, thump.*
I grabbed a baseball bat (actually a backscratcher I use as a substitute) and crept towards the balcony.
I saw a silhouette in the moonlight.
It was Myoreusin.
But he wasn't alone.
Something fist-sized was wriggling in front of Myoreusin.

"......!!"

I held my breath and watched.
Myoreusin was carrying his kibble in his mouth and dropping it in front of that tiny creature.
It was... a very small and dirty 'kitten'!

"Oh my god..."

I made a sound without realizing it.
Startled, Myoreusin hid the kitten with his body.
His eyes were so determined, like a mother protecting her child... no, like a father.

"Myoreusin... you..."

I put down the backscratcher and approached slowly.
The kitten trembled at the stranger's presence and burrowed into Myoreusin's fur.
Where did it come from? My place is on the 3rd floor?
Ah, the screen!
The screen in one corner was slightly torn.
It must have come in through that gap.

*Buzz-*

My phone vibrated at this timing.

[Today's Fortune: A rolling stone is a bundle of luck.]
[Interpretation: Treat small lives as precious. Greater fortune comes when you share your bowl.]

"The rolling stone... is this kid?"

I let out a dry laugh.
I was tense thinking there was a thief, but a real 'thief cat' was living in my house.
And such a tiny, cute thief at that.

"Okay, okay. I won't kick it out."

I spoke softly.
Only then did Myoreusin drop his guard and cry, "Nyaang."
It sounded like [Thanks, Butler.]
Looking closely, the kitten wasn't in good shape.
Its eyes were crusted shut with discharge, and it was limping on one leg.

"We need to treat this..."

A hospital open at this hour... there is one.
Right next door.
The Friendly Animal Hospital, Director Si-woo Kang.
But it's 3 AM. Waking him up would be a nuisance.
Then I heard the sound of the next door opening.
I went out to the hallway and saw Si-woo carrying a convenience store bag.

"Oh? Haru-ssi? You weren't sleeping?"

"Ah, Ssaem! No, Oppa... No, Doctor!"

I was so rushed that my honorifics got tangled.

"Is something wrong? You look pale."

"Um... is emergency treatment possible right now? I have a critical patient!"

"Critical patient? Myoreusin?"

"No, Myoreusin's hidden... hidden child... something like that..."

* * *

We ran to Si-woo's hospital.
I carried the kitten wrapped in a towel, and Si-woo guided the way beside me.
The kitten on the examination table was truly tiny.
Maybe sensing Si-woo's touch, the little one settled down.

"Fortunately, no fractures. Malnutrition and conjunctivitis are a bit severe, but it should heal quickly with a few days of treatment."

"Ah... that's a relief."

My legs gave out and I slumped down.
Si-woo handed me a cup of hot cocoa.

"You were surprised, right? Drink this."

"Thank you. Sorry for the trouble at dawn."

"Not at all. Seeing sick animals is a vet's job. And..."

He looked at Myoreusin and smiled.

"Myoreusin is amazing. Sharing his own food. Usually, they are territorial animals so it's not easy."

Myoreusin was sitting in the corner of the exam table, watching over the kitten with a solemn expression.
With a pose that said, 'If you touch my kid, I won't let it slide.'

"I know. He always bullies me to give him Churu."

"It's love, love."

Si-woo muttered.
Love?

"You too, Haru-ssi. Running out barefoot to save that little one."

"Huh? Ah, my feet..."

I looked down and saw I was wearing mismatched slippers on bare feet.
My face felt hot.

"Ahaha... I was in such a rush..."

"It's cool. You've really become a Butler."

He lightly patted my head.
It was a brief, passing touch, but the lingering feeling was long.
My heart started beating erratically again.

[Fortune Flash: Good deeds are rewarded.]

A notification popped up on the phone screen.
Rewarded?
I looked at Si-woo in front of me.
Warm eyes, gentle voice, and the hand that patted my head.
Is this the reward?
If so, it's a pretty decent reward.

"Um... what should we name this little one?"

Si-woo asked.

"Hmm... Since it's a rolling stone... how about 'Gureum' (Cloud)? Rolling in like a cloud."
*(TN: 'Gureum' means Cloud in Korean.)*

"Gureum. Pretty. If we wash him up nicely, he'll be white as a cloud."

We looked at each other and smiled.
4 AM at the animal hospital.
The kitten's soft breathing and the scent of hot cocoa.
And the two of us.
It was a night where something fluffy was rising in the air.

But the romance was short-lived.
Myoreusin squeezed in between Si-woo and me.

"Nya!"
[Don't set the mood. If treatment is done, let's go home.]

"A-Alright! We're going!"

As expected, my house's master is a romance breaker.
But I decided to forgive him just for today.
Because he's a hero who saved a small life.

- End of Episode 8 -
