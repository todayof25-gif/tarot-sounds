"Myoreusin? Why aren't you eating your mam-ma?"
*(TN: Mam-ma is baby talk for food.)*

The food bowl was untouched.
Normally, he's a guy who would step on my solar plexus saying "Give me food, human" before I even opened my eyes.
Myoreusin was curled up in the corner under the bed.
He didn't answer when called, and only turned his head away when I shook his favorite Churu right in front of his nose.

"Are you sick? Hmm?"

When I touched him, his body was hot like fire. His nose was dry.
My heart sank *thud*.

"Si-woo-ssi!! Si-woo-ssi!!"

I screamed and ran out to the living room.
Si-woo, who was preparing breakfast, ran over in surprise.

"What's wrong? What happened?"

"Myoreusin... Myoreusin is acting strange! His body is like fire and he won't eat..."

Si-woo's expression hardened instantly.
He immediately went into the room and examined Myoreusin.
Checking pupil response, taking temperature, palpating the abdomen—his hands were fast and precise.
But his frown deepened more and more.

"Fever over 40 degrees. We have to go to the hospital immediately."

We ran carrying Myoreusin, still in our pajamas.
The time for the elevator to reach the 1st floor felt like an eternity.
Myoreusin in my arms hung limply without strength. That heavy weight felt so light and precarious now.

* * *

As soon as we arrived at the hospital, an emergency was declared.
Si-woo entered the treatment room without even time to put on his white coat.
I stuck to the waiting room glass and looked inside.
The process of inserting an IV, drawing blood, and taking X-rays moved urgently.

'Please... Please let it be nothing. Please say it's indigestion, or a cold.'

My hands shook uncontrollably.
Tears blurred my vision.
Then someone wrapped their arm around my shoulder. It was Vice-Director Seo-yoon Kim.

"Don't worry. Oppa... no, the Director will save him."

Her voice was calm, but she too looked clearly tense.

1 hour later.
Si-woo came out of the treatment room.
His face without the mask was pale.
I shot up and approached him.

"He's okay, right? It's just a simple cold, right?"

"......"

Si-woo couldn't answer.
That silence broke me further.

"Let's go... to the Director's office."

On the monitor in the Director's office, Myoreusin's X-rays and blood test numbers were displayed.
Numbers with red warning lights were everywhere.

"Acute renal failure... and extensive organ damage of unknown cause is visible. The numbers are too bad. If it were a normal cat, he would have gone into shock already."

"What? Organ damage? Why all of a sudden? He played well until yesterday. He ate well and broke the Gundam too... He was fine!"

As I sobbed, Si-woo bit his lip as if in pain.

"It's not a simple disease. This is..."

Si-woo clenched his fists. Veins popped on the back of his pale hands.

"Side effects from past drug experiments."

"Drug... experiments?"

"That Project M... The drugs those bastards injected into Myoreusin seem to be causing toxicity now. That crazy experiment to increase intelligence and lifespan beyond a normal cat was actually disrupting his immune system and destroying his body from the inside."

It felt like I was hit in the head. No, felt like the world was collapsing.
That bad guy. The so-called former owner.
Because of his greed, for money, he did this to an innocent cat.
Thinking of the pain Myoreusin must have endured tore my heart apart.

"Treatment... you can do it, right? You're a doctor. You said you're a famous doctor."

I asked with a trembling voice. I felt like grabbing his pant leg.
Si-woo lowered his head.

"The toxins have already spread throughout the body. We administered an antidote, but I can't guarantee if Myoreusin's stamina will hold out. Tonight... is the critical moment."

"We will do our best but... you might have to prepare yourself."

"Liar..."

My legs gave way and I slumped down.
I said we were family. We were just about to be happy.
How much I... how much I love you.

"Haru-ssi."

Si-woo knelt in front of me and held my hand.
His hand was also trembling cold.

"I won't give up. I will save him. I will save Myoreusin even if I have to bet my life."

His eyes were desperate, and grim.
Just then, a nurse ran urgently from the treatment room.

"Director! Patient vitals are dropping! Cardiac arrest is imminent!"

"Damn it!!"

Si-woo kicked off the floor and ran.
I followed, but the treatment room door closed.
Through the window, I saw Si-woo compressing Myoreusin's small chest, performing CPR.

"Wake up... Please wake up, you brat!!"

Si-woo's scream was heard.
*Beep- beep- beep-*
The irregular monitor sound suddenly became a long tone.

*Beeeeeeeeeeep-------------*

Time stopped.
The world turned black and white.
No. No, Myoreusin.
Don't go. Don't leave me alone.

I put my forehead on the floor and begged and begged.
If there is a God, please show me a miracle just once.
You can take my lifespan, so please just save our Myoreusin.

At that moment, the phone in my pocket vibrated.
The screen turned on, and a familiar app notification popped up.
But Myoreusin is lying over there?
Who sent this?

[Today's Fortune: Human... don't cry.]
[Advice: I'm not dead yet.]
