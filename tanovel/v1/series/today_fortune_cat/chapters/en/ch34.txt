"Honey, we should decide now too, right?"

Weekend afternoon.
Si-woo sat on the sofa with a solemn expression and opened an Excel file.
Dozens of words were written densely on the laptop screen.

"Decide what?"

"Fetus name, fetus name! We can't call our baby '0.5cm dot' forever."

Ah, right. Fetus name.
2 weeks passed since knowing the pregnancy, but it was chaotic so we couldn't even give a fetus name yet.
Usually people do things like 'Teunteun-i' (Healthy one), 'Chukbok-i' (Blessing).

"I thought about it too... do you have any good ones, Si-woo-ssi?"

"Huhu, I prepared for this day."

Si-woo turned the laptop screen confidently.
I looked into the screen with expectation.
And frowned in 3 seconds.

1. Sarang-i (Because lovely)
2. Gippeum-i (Because gives joy)
3. Rang-i (Short for Sarang-i)
4. Jjukjjuk-i (To grow tall)
5. Teunteun-cho (Strong foundation)

"......"

"How is it? Isn't Number 1 'Sarang-i' the best? Sticks to the mouth."

"Si-woo-ssi... What year is it now?"

"Yes? It's 202X?"

"But why is your sense stuck in the 80s? What is Sarang-i, Sarang-i! Too common!"

"Common! Classic is eternal. Where is a word as good as love."

"No. Too old. Lately people name stylishly. Don't you know 'Roa', 'Eden' like that?"

"Ey, that sounds too much like English names. Koreans should use Korean."

We confronted tightly.
The Yes-man husband who usually said "Haru-ssi is all right", was stubborn before the fetus name.
Clearly he was fixated on 'Sarang-i' he thought of.

"Then what do you want to do, Haru-ssi?"

"Hmm... How about 'Haneul-i' (Sky)? To have a clear and broad mind."

"Haneul-i? Too ordinary? A regular Maltese at our hospital is named Haneul."

"Ah, really! Then 'Gureum-i' (Cloud)?"

"That's our parents' house dog name."

"Aoh!"

I hit my chest in frustration.
We bickered for an hour saying "It's Sarang-i", "No it's tacky", "Then you name it".
Shouting went back and forth for the first time in the sweet newlywed house. (Of course it was cute level.)

"Nyaaaaang!!!"
[Ah, noisy to death!]

Just then, Myoreusin sleeping on top of the cat tower screamed.
The guy came down with a face full of annoyance, squeezed between us and sat down.
And hit the table with his front paw. *Bang!*

"Myoreusin? You up? Sorry, were we a bit loud?"

"Nya."
[Let me sleep, you things. What's the big deal naming, fighting like that.]

Myoreusin looked at us alternately as if pathetic, then snatched Si-woo's phone.
And started typing with skilled hands.

"Uh? Myoreusin is going to name it?"

Si-woo's eyes sparkled.
Right, if spiritual Daoist Cat names it, we must follow without complaint.
We held our breath and looked at the screen.

*Tap. Tap. Tap.*

[Today's Fortune: Naming Service Open.]
[Recommended Fetus Name 1: Churu No.2]

"......"

"......"

"Hey! What is Churu No.2, Churu No.2! Is my kid a snack?"

Myoreusin snorted *Chit* when I shouted.
And typed again.

[Recommended Fetus Name 2: Butler Junior]

"No... Too intuitive..."

[Recommended Fetus Name 3: Tuna Belly]

"Get away from food!!"

Eventually Myoreusin's naming sense was a dud too.
Only names projecting his own desires.
We returned to square one again.

"Hah... Difficult. Shall we just name roughly?"

"Can't do that. Have to call it for 10 months."

We slumped on the sofa exhausted.
It was then.
Clear winter wind came through the living room window crack and shook the curtains.
And between open curtains, night sky was visible.
In the clean sky without a speck of fine dust, one star was shining exceptionally bright.

*Twinkle-.*

"Uh? Look at that, Si-woo-ssi."

"Yes?"

"That star there. Very bright. Wasn't visible during day."

Si-woo looked out the window following my gaze.
At that moment, that star was contained in his eyes too.

"Ah..."

He got up as if possessed and went to the window.
I followed and stood next to him.
Small star shining bravely alone in the dark city sky.
Like the baby that came to us.
Small but strong presence.

"......Byeol-i (Star)."

Si-woo mumbled lowly.

"Yes?"

"Byeol-i. Fetus name, how about Byeol-i? Last time looking at ultrasound Haru-ssi also said 'Like a star floating in universe'."

"Byeol-i..."

Rolled the word in mouth.
Byeol-i. Byeol-i.
Easy to call, pretty, and good meaning too.
Above all, not tacky!

"Good! Byeol-i! Totally like it!"

"Right? To shine twinkle twinkle. Like a present that came to us."

Si-woo smiled brightly. Dimples poked in.
Right, need no Sarang-i or Teunteun-cho.
This is it.

"Myoreusin! How is it? Byeol-i! You like it?"

We asked Myoreusin behind us.
Myoreusin looked at the star outside the window steadily, then waved his tail.

"Nyaang."
[Not bad. Pass.]

Oh, passed the picky Daoist Cat too.
Then Myoreusin tapped the phone again.

*Ding-*

[Today's Fortune: Star has risen.]
[Interpretation: Fetus name war is over now. Give me my food.]
[P.S: Educate Byeol-i not to steal my Churu when born.]

"Puhaha! Don't worry. I'll buy Byeol-i's share separately."

We faced each other and laughed.
Not Sarang-i, not Haneul-i, not Churu No.2, but our 'Byeol-i'.
It was noisy deciding this one small name, but became that much more precious.

"Hi, Byeol-ah. It's Dad."
"Nice to meet you, Byeol-ah. It's Mom."

We placed hands on the belly and greeted side by side.
Star outside the window seemed to twinkle more.
And below that, Myoreusin was watching us with a pleased expression (actually hungry expression).

It was a perfect night.
