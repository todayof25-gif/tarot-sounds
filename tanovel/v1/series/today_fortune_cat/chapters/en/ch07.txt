They say embarrassment is my share to bear.
But the expiration date of that embarrassment was longer than I thought.

I stayed under my duvet all weekend.
I couldn't face the neighbor, Dr. Si-woo Kang.
Not only was I caught sleeping in the hallway, but I was also caught talking to a cat.

"Ughhh..."

I kicked the duvet.
Myoreusin glared at me as if I was pathetic.

"Nya."
[Stop it. It's distracting.]

"It's because of you! Because you talked!"

"Meow."
[I am a cat.]

He feigned innocence and groomed himself.
The recorded voice was undeniable, but he had kept his mouth shut ever since.
He's a master of push-and-pull.

That was when.
*Ding-dong-*

My heart sank at the sound of the doorbell.
I wasn't expecting a delivery?
I stealthily checked the intercom. My breath hitched.
A handsome face filled the screen. It was Si-woo Kang.

'What do I do! Should I pretend I'm not home?'

But Myoreusin ran to the entrance and cried loudly, "Nyaang-!"
That traitor!

"I know you're there. Can you come out for a second?"

His sweet voice came through the intercom.
There was nowhere to run.
I pulled my hoodie low and opened the door with the most pitiful expression I could muster.

"......Hello."

"Did you have a good weekend?"

He smiled refreshingly as if nothing had happened.
He looked like a pictorial in his checkered shirt and cotton pants.

"Here, please take this."

"What is this?"

"New Churu. We got samples at the hospital, and I thought of Myoreusin."

Thought of Myoreusin.
Was it just my imagination that there was a strange accent on that word?
Myoreusin poked his head out through the gap in the door.

"Nya!"
[Mine!]

Myoreusin rubbed his body against Dr. Si-woo's legs, acting cute.
He only wags his tail for me. Capitalist cat.

"Haha, little guy. You know the good stuff."

Dr. Si-woo crouched down and petted Myoreusin.
His large hand gently stroked Myoreusin's back.
Myoreusin purred, clearly enjoying it.
A handsome man and a cat. This combination is dangerous for the heart.

"Um... would you like to come in for some... coffee?"

I blurted out nonsense without realizing it.
Are you crazy, Haru Seo?! inviting a man into your house when you haven't even washed your hair?

"May I?"

He stood up as if he had been waiting for it. I thought he would refuse!

* * *

Somehow, the neighbor entered my house.
My cramped studio felt full with his presence.
I hurriedly put away the laundry rack and kicked some loose hair under the furniture.

"Please sit. The place is a bit small, right?"

"No, it's cozy and nice. Smells good too."

Smell? How did he know I sprayed Febreze?
I made some instant coffee and brought it out.
He couldn't take his eyes off Myoreusin as he fed him the Churu.

"Myoreusin is really... special."

"Huh? Ah, well... he is a bit chubby."

"No, not that... his eyes are like a human's."

I flinched. Did he feel it too?

"Right? Sometimes he seems human. Like he understands what I say..."

"Hmm... maybe he was a human in a past life."

He threw it out there like a joke.
But his eyes weren't laughing, so the atmosphere turned strange.

"Um... about what you said at the hospital last time."

I asked cautiously.

"Calling him 'Teacher'..."

He put down his coffee cup.
My face reflected in his soft brown eyes as he stared at me.

"Are you curious?"

"Yes. Very."

He smirked and leaned forward.
The distance between us closed.
A subtle scent, maybe shampoo or fabric softener, wafted towards me.

"It's a secret."

"Ah, that's cheap..."

"I have a condition instead."

"What is it?"

"From now on, instead of 'Teacher' (Ssaem), call me 'Oppa'... Just kidding."

He winked playfully.
My heart dropped with a thud. What is this man? A player?

"Let's just... be friends. Drop the honorifics. I actually needed a neighborhood friend."

"Ah... Yes! Great!"

"Then can I call you Haru-ssi?"

"Yes, of course!"

His hand moved towards me.
Is he going to cover my hand on the table? Or a handshake?
Either way, my heart felt like it would explode.
Just as his fingertips were about to touch...

"Hiss-!!"

A sharp sound broke the silence.
It was Myoreusin.
Having finished the Churu, Myoreusin squeezed between us and swung his front paw.

"Whoa!"

Dr. Si-woo pulled his hand back in a hurry.

"Nyaaaang!!"
[How dare you!]

Myoreusin puffed up his fur and glared at Dr. Si-woo.
Like a conservative father trying to protect his daughter. No, even fiercer.

"Haha... Myoreusin is very jealous."

Dr. Si-woo laughed awkwardly.
I hurriedly picked up Myoreusin.

"I-I'm sorry! He's usually not like this..."

"It's okay. I guess I intruded without noticing. On the Master."

He stood up from his seat.

"I'll get going for today. Feed him well."

"Ah, yes... Goodbye."

I walked him to the door.
Before leaving, he turned back to look at Myoreusin one more time.
And muttered quietly.

"Still the same. That temper."

"What?"

"Ah, talking to myself. Rest well, Haru-ssi."

The door closed.
I slumped against the door.
My heart was still pounding.
What was that just now? Was it a 'fling'? It was, right?
But why does Myoreusin hate him so much?

"Hey! You ruined it! I could have held his hand!"

I glared at Myoreusin.
Myoreusin jumped onto the bed and thumped his tail.

*Buzz-*
Phone vibrated.

[Today's Fortune: Beware of the Wolf.]
[Interpretation: Do not be deceived by a smiling face. That guy is hiding a dark ulterior motive.]

"A wolf?! He's so sweet!"

[Additional Warning: No skinship allowed. Unless you want your Churu confiscated.]

"Tch... Boomer cat."

I pouted.
But I couldn't help grinning as I looked out the window.
I made a handsome friend next door.
Just that alone makes today a jackpot fortune.

- End of Episode 7 -
