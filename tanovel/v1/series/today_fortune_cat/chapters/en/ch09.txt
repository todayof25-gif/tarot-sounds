5 AM.
I woke up with a start, crumpled on the sofa in the animal hospital waiting room.
My neck was stiff and my mouth tasted stale. Unfamiliar ceiling, smell of disinfectant, and faint mechanical beeping.
Ah, right. This is the animal hospital.

"Ughh..."

As I groaned and stretched, someone placed a blanket over my shoulders.

"Are you awake? You can sleep more."

It was Director Si-woo Kang.
Fatigue was etched on his face as he took off his glasses and rubbed the bridge of his nose, but his eyes were as clear as the dawn air.

"Ah, I'm sorry! I slept like the dead. How is Gureum?"

"The treatment went well and he's sleeping. I gave him plenty of fluids because the dehydration was severe."

"Ah... thank goodness."

Along with relief, realistic worries came flooding in.
Premium animal hospital, night emergency care, IV fluids.
My thin bank account balance flashed in my mind. If I pay off this month's credit card bill, I'll have nothing left.

"Um... Doctor."

I swallowed dryly and asked cautiously.

"The bill... will be quite high, right? I don't have a lump sum right now... would installment payments or paying later be possible?"

I lowered the corners of my eyes to look as pitiful as possible.
But Si-woo stopped arranging the charts and chuckled.

"Don't worry. The fee for this visit has already been billed."

"What? By whom?"

"By Myoreusin."

"Excuse me?"

I doubted my ears. My fat cat paid the hospital bill?
Si-woo pointed towards the inpatient room with his chin.
Through the glass window, the tiny kitten Gureum was on an IV drip, and Myoreusin was sitting solemnly next to him.
However, instead of a receipt, a crumpled post-it note was hanging around Myoreusin's neck.

['Open 100 of my Churu.']

"Pfft!"

I burst out laughing without realizing it. What is that.
Si-woo said with a playful expression.

"I reached a settlement with Myoreusin. No snacks for Myoreusin for the next 3 months. We decided to call it even with that cost."

"Ah... really? But that's too..."

It's a ridiculous deal. 100 Churu is only a few tens of thousands of won.

"Actually... I wanted to help."

Si-woo's voice softened.

"Haru-ssi ran out barefoot to save the child. That heart was so beautiful, I wanted to put a spoon on that table too."
*(TN: 'Put a spoon on the table' is a Korean idiom meaning to chip in/join in on something good.)*

He scratched the back of his head shyly.
This man, not only is his face handsome, but his heart is silk. How can I not fall for him?

"Thank you so much... I will definitely repay this kindness. Let me clean the hospital at least!"

When I rolled up my sleeves, he laughed and waved his hands.

"Cleaning is fine. Instead, I have another favor."

"What is it?"

"When Gureum is discharged... how about 'Co-parenting' for a while?"

"Co... parenting?"

"Yes. It will be overwhelming for you to care for him alone. You need to formula feed every 2-3 hours and induce bowel movements, it's impossible alone while working."

He explained logically like an expert.

"I'll stop by after work to help. On my days off, I can keep him. How about it?"

Is this... saying he'll legitimately come to my house every day?
Or pure goodwill?
Either way, there is no reason to refuse.

"Great! That's totally great! I'm actually sorry to burden you."

"That's a relief. I was nervous you might refuse."

He let out a sigh of relief and smiled brightly. His dimples deepened.

We stood side by side and looked through the glass window.
Gureum sleeping without a care in the world, and the dependable Myoreusin guarding him.
It was a scene that made my heart feel fluffy just looking at it.

"So beautiful..."

As I muttered, Si-woo took out his phone.

"I should capture this moment. In commemoration of our historic first co-parenting."

*Click. Click.*
He took photos from various angles. The gaze looking through the viewfinder was affectionate.

"Let me see, did it come out well?"

Curious, I leaned in close to him.
Shoulders almost touching. I smelled a faint scent of soap.
He swiped through the gallery checking the photos.
But then, his finger paused.

"Uh?"

He panicked and tried to quickly turn off the screen.
But my eyes were faster.
The photo that just flashed past.
It wasn't a cat.
It was a person.
And it was... me smiling very brightly?

'Huh? That's me? When was that taken?'

Was it when I received the Churu gift last time?
It was a split second I couldn't recall.
What was certain was that he had secretly taken a photo of me and was keeping it.

"Um... that photo just now..."

My voice trembled.
Si-woo turned beet red and cleared his throat.

"Ahem. Ah, that... it's a test shot. Testing the camera quality."

"With my face?"

"Yes. The... subject... was pleasing."

"......Yes?"

The subject was pleasing?
My heart started racing like crazy.
The atmosphere was about to get strangely sticky.

*Buzz- Buzz-*

The phone in my pocket vibrated spasmodically.
There is only one guy who lacks this much tact.

[Fortune Flash: Do not drink the kimchi soup by the bowl.]
*(TN: Idiom 'Don't drink kimchi soup first' means 'Don't count your chickens before they hatch'.)*
[Advice: Raid that guy's phone gallery. It will be a spectacle.]

It was a message from Myoreusin.
A spectacle? Are there unflattering photos?
I looked at Si-woo with suspicious eyes. He was just smiling, oblivious.

"Haru-ssi? Why are you looking at me like that?"

"Ah, nothing. You seem to have a lot of secrets, Ssaem."

"Secrets? I am a transparent person."

Is that so? Why does Myoreusin keep guarding against him?
Curiosity bit its own tail, but I decided to cover it up for now.
Because Si-woo Kang in front of me right now is a savior and a kind neighbor.
And honestly, the fact that someone is keeping a picture of me smiling... wasn't bad.

"Then, shall we go now? I need to get ready for work to earn Myoreusin's Churu money."

"Ah, I'll walk you. The carrier must be heavy."

We left the hospital.
The dawning sky was pink.
The tips of his ears were also pink.

But I didn't know.
Myoreusin was putting his front paw against the hospital window behind me, glaring at us.
And that his amber eyes were filled with deep 'worry'.

- End of Episode 9 -
