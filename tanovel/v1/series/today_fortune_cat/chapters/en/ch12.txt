They say the ground hardens after rain.
After the misunderstanding (the incident I want to believe was a misunderstanding) with Dr. Si-woo was cleared, we grew close rapidly.
Today was the day I decided to treat him to dinner to repay his kindness.

"Sounds good. A meal made by Haru-ssi, I should skip lunch."

Seeing Si-woo's expectant text, I ran to the market as soon as I got off work.
The menu was the ambitious 'Mille-Feuille Nabe'.
It looks good and tastes good—the standard for housewarming food.

"Myoreusin, I'll let you feast today too. Specially opened a salmon can."

"Nya."
[Is this a bribe?]

Myoreusin looked at the salmon can and groomed himself nonchalantly.
Since a few days ago, the guy had been strangely quiet.
He didn't hiss even when Si-woo came, and just watched intensely from afar.
Like a predator eyeing its prey... or did he just give up?

*Ding-dong-*

"He's here!"

I fixed my apron and opened the door.
Si-woo stood there holding a bottle of wine.
Forearms revealed by rolled-up white sleeves, and slightly damp hair.
His face under the entrance light was working hard today as well.

"Thank you for inviting me. I felt bad coming empty-handed so I bought a wine."

"Oh my, you have sense! Come in."

A cramped studio, a handsome man, delicious smells, and soft lighting.
Everything was perfect.
We sat facing each other at the table. The glances exchanged over the bubbling nabe were warm.

"Wow, this is really delicious? Haru-ssi, your cooking skills are no joke."

"Hehe, I just followed a recipe. Eat a lot."

I felt full just watching him eat well.
Is this the fun of newlywed lif... no, the fun of dating?
We talked about Gureum, hospital episodes, and trivial daily life.
The conversation didn't stop. Like old lovers.

"Actually, I was a bit scared that day." I confessed honestly.

"When? When I chased after you?"

"No, during that notebook incident. Your expression was so... cold."

Si-woo paused his chopsticks.
Silence flowed for a moment.

"Did I? Hmm... I have a bit of an obsession with medical records. Every patient feels like my own child."

He laughed shyly.
Seeing that clear smile, my suspicion melted like snow.
Right, he's someone who loves animals this much. Myoreusin was just being sensitive.

*Buzz-*

Then my phone on the table vibrated.
It's Myoreusin Time, unable to read the room.

[Today's Fortune: Beware of sweet things.]
[Advice: When the tongue is paralyzed, one eats poison thinking it is honey.]

'Sweet things?'
The nabe we're eating is salty though?
I ignored it and flipped the phone over.
Myoreusin was staring at me from under the table.
A look that said, 'I warned you, human.'

"What is it? A message from Myoreusin?"

"Ah, yes. Well... he says don't eat too much dessert. Says I'll gain weight."

"Haha, Myoreusin even manages Haru-ssi's health."

After the meal, I brought out the cake Si-woo bought.
We had wine and cake. A perfect dessert time.

"Haru-ssi, open wide. Aaa."

Si-woo picked a strawberry with a fork and held it out to my mouth.
Oh my god. 'Aaa'?
My face felt like it would explode, but I accepted it as naturally as possible.

"Mmm! Sweet!"

"Sweet, right? I like sweet things too."

He winked.
Sweet cake, tipsy wine, and the sweet man in front of my eyes.
Myoreusin's warning flew to Andromeda.
So what if it's poison. It's this sweet.

That was when.

"Uh..."

Si-woo looked at me and reached out.
His thumb brushed the corner of my lips.
A very soft and hot sensation.

"You have cream on you."

His touch swept across my lips.
Time seemed to stop.
His eyes were fixed on my lips.
The atmosphere flowed strangely.
This is... real. This is definitely a kiss angle.

I squeezed my eyes shut without realizing.
My heart felt like it would pop out of my mouth.
His breath got closer.

'It's coming!'

I steeled myself and waited...
Suddenly, Si-woo's movement stopped dead.

"......?"

I opened my eyes slightly.
Si-woo stopped right in front of my lips, staring over my shoulder.
His expression was strangely stiff.
Bewilderment? No, shock?

"W-Why? What's wrong?"

"Um... Haru-ssi."

He pointed behind me with a trembling voice.

"Where did... Myoreusin go?"

"Huh? Myoreusin? He was under the table earlier..."

I turned around.
He wasn't there.
Not in the living room, not on the cat tower, not on the cushion.
There was no trace of the giant calico anywhere in the small house.

"Did he go to the bathroom?"

I opened the bathroom door, thinking nothing of it.
Empty.
Closet? Balcony? Under the bed?
I searched everywhere, but Myoreusin was nowhere to be seen.

"Myoreusin! Myoreusin!"

Anxiety crept in.
I looked at the front door.
The digital lock... was locked.
Windows were closed too.
It was a sealed room.
But the cat evaporated.

"Where did he go..."

Cold sweat ran down my spine.
At that moment, my phone rang.
It wasn't Myoreusin.
Caller ID Restricted.

[Look at the balcony window right now.]

It was a machine-like voice.
I walked to the balcony as if possessed.
Outside the window, in the 1st-floor flowerbed...
Someone was standing there.
A man with a black hat pulled low.
And in that man's arms...
Was Myoreusin, limp and with eyes closed.

- End of Episode 12 -
