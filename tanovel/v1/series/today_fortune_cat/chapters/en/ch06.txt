"Assistant Manager Seo, drink up! We're all dying together today!"

At Department Head Choi's toast, I smiled and emptied my glass.
Today's company dinner was a simple 'Team Building' event. It sounded nice, but it was just a day to drink and die together.
The side dishes were predictable. Starting from the Department Head's "Back in my day," to stories about difficult clients.
I just nodded and diligently grilled the meat.

'I want to go home...'

My head was filled only with thoughts of Myoreusin.
Did my Myoreusin eat? Is he bored?
I want to go home quickly and knead his belly fat.
But social life isn't easy. I dutifully drank every glass offered to me.
Fine, if I'm going to drink, let's just get happily drunk.

* * *

1 AM.
I stumbled as I opened the front door.
The world was spinning.

"I'm... ho... me..."

"Nya."
[You're finally here.]

Amber eyes flashed on the bed.
My house's master, Myoreusin.

"Aigoo~ Myoreusin! The butler is home~"

I couldn't even take off my shoes and crawled on the floor towards Myoreusin.
The cat thumped his tail as if I was pathetic. Usually, I would have cowered, but today the sadness was greater.

"Hey, Myoreusin. You don't know how I feel."

I buried my head in the corner of the bed and mumbled.

"Do you know how hard I work to earn money? Trying to earn Churu money... working overtime every day... sucking up to rude people..."

"Nyaang."
[Smells like alcohol. Get away.]

"Should I really... just throw my resignation letter... and go farming with you? You catch mice and I'll dig up sweet potatoes..."

"Hiss!"
[No nonsense allowed!]

"Okay... You only care about Churu, right? Someone like me is just a money-making machine, right? Sob..."

Tears gushed out.
"I also... want to be loved... want to be praised..."

I held onto Myoreusin's front paw and whined.
The squishy jelly texture was a small comfort. He didn't pull his paw away and just tapped my head. *Tap.*
I was grateful he didn't use his claws.

"Thank you... I only have you..."

I buried my face in Myoreusin's warm belly and fell asleep.
My last memory was Myoreusin staring down at me while my phone screen flashed.

* * *

"Ugh..."

A splitting headache. Fortunately, it was my ceiling.
As soon as I woke up, I ran to the bathroom and puked my guts out.
My reflection in the mirror was a panda.
Memories of my drunken tantrum in front of Myoreusin came back in fragments.

"Hah... You were crazy, Haru Seo."

When I returned to the room, Myoreusin was peacefully grooming himself by the window.
He glanced at me and seemed to snort. 'Hmph.'

"Um... Myoreusin? Sorry about yesterday..."

As I apologized and looked for my phone, a notification was on the screen.

[A new voice memo has been saved.]

"Huh? Recording?"

Did I sing while drunk? The title was <Comfort.mp3>.
I didn't name it this?
My hands trembled as I pressed the play button.

*Static-* My voice could be heard through the noise.
"I also... want to be loved... Sob..."

My dark history was perfectly preserved. I was about to turn it off when, after a moment of silence, an unbelievable sound was heard.

"Sigh."

A human sigh. Very deep and husky.

"You pitiful thing."

*Click.* Recording ended.

"......?"

I stared blankly at my phone.
A man's voice? No, an old man?
It was just me and Myoreusin in the room?
I slowly looked at Myoreusin. He was still peaceful.

"Is... is it you?"

"Nya?"
[What.]

A very ordinary cat meow.
But the recording file definitely existed. Listening to it again—"Sigh, you pitiful thing."
It was definitely the nuance of Myoreusin clicking his tongue.
My cat speaks? And in an old man's tone?

Then, a phone notification popped up.

*Buzz-*
[Today's Fortune: Birds hear words spoken in the day, and mice (or rats) hear words spoken at night.]
[Update: Don't spout nonsense when drunk. The embarrassment is yours.]

The illustration of Myoreusin on the screen was smirking.
I got goosebumps. This guy is not just a cat.

*Ding-dong-*
Just then, the doorbell rang.

"Who is it?"
"Delivery."

A box labeled 'Hangover Soup Meal Kit' was in front of the door. Sender: Si-woo Kang.

[I barely managed to help you inside when you were sleeping in the hallway yesterday. Sending this to help settle your stomach. - Si-woo Kang]

A note was attached. I slept in the hallway... and Dr. Si-woo caught me.
Please let that be the end of it.

[P.S. Your conversation with the cat... was impressive.]

"Aaaack!!!!!!"

I screamed while tearing at my hair.
He heard it. He heard everything!
I wish the earth would just explode.
Watching me scream, Myoreusin yawned widely.
As if saying, 'You're really making a scene.'

- End of Episode 6 -
