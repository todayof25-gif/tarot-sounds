Wedding preparation is war, and a series of choices.
Starting from 'S-D-M' (Studio, Dress, Makeup) to dowry, gifts, and wedding invitation design.
It should be a happy process, but we bickered over trivial things.

"I said I like simple. Clean and sophisticated."

"But it's a once-in-a-lifetime wedding, isn't something a bit flashy better? Elders will see it too so if it's too plain..."

We were debating for an hour with twenty invitation samples spread on the table.
Si-woo preferred a flashy design with glittering gold foil and lace, while I preferred a clean illustration design without clutter.
Si-woo, who usually accommodated everything saying "Do as you wish, Haru-ssi", was subtly stubborn in front of wedding prep.
From studio shooting concept to wedding venue flower decoration colors, our tastes diverged subtly.

"Hah... Shall we rest a bit? My head hurts."

I raised the white flag first.
As I flopped onto the sofa, Myoreusin climbed onto my stomach.
Heavy weight. That pathetic look asking "Humans, fighting again?".
This warmth is the biggest comfort right now.

"Myoreusin, you choose. Number 1, or Number 2?
Honestly this gold foil looks too old-fashioned, right? Right?"

I shook two invitations, but Myoreusin yawned widely as if uninterested.
Then suddenly he corrected his posture and stared into the air.
Pupils dilated wide, ears stood perk up. The tip of his tail trembled.
That behavior where cats sometimes look into empty space as if chasing something.
Commonly called seeing a 'Ghost Spot', but seeing Myoreusin focus like that felt eerie.

"What's wrong? Is something there? Did a bug get in?"

Si-woo also got tense and looked around. Looking around holding a fly swatter.
But Myoreusin stared piercingly at the living room corner, the empty space with only the air conditioner, for a long time.
As if someone we can't see is there.
And he tapped my phone with his front paw. *Tap.*

*Ding-*

It was a clear notification sound different from usual.
Looking at the screen, a new window called [FUTURE_VISION] popped up instead of [Today's Fortune].

[Future Forecast: 3 years later, Clear.]
[Location: Just our living room.]

"Future forecast? Do we have this function? Did it update?"

Si-woo looked into the phone fascinated.
At that moment, a short video played on the screen.
It had noise like an old film movie, but it was definitely a color video.

The background in the video was our current living room.
But the furniture changed a bit. Sofa position was different, and curtain color was warm beige.
And in the corner of the living room, exactly where Myoreusin was staring...
A colorful baby mat was laid out, and a small baby with chubby milky cheeks was sitting on it.
The baby with apple hair shook a rattle and laughed "Kkyarreuh".
And next to him, Myoreusin lay belly up, staying still even when the baby pulled his tail.
Rather, he was playing by tapping the baby's cheek softly with a *thud* (cat punch).

"Uh...? That..."

The video cut off in 5 seconds. *Click.*
Silence flowed.
Si-woo and I stared at each other blankly. Speechless.

"Just now... was a baby, right? Our... baby?"

"Yes. And Myoreusin... was watching the baby. Staying still even when tail was stepped on."

My heart beat off-beat. *Thump thump.*
3 years later. Our house. And baby and Myoreusin.
That peaceful and warm scenery stuck in my mind and didn't leave.
It felt like the future I only imagined unfolded before my eyes.
What Myoreusin showed wasn't a simple video, but the 'perfect happiness' we will protect and create.

"Myoreusin... You really..."

Si-woo hugged Myoreusin with a moved expression.
Myoreusin kneaded Si-woo's chin with his cotton bat paws with an expression asking "What's the surprise".

"Haru-ssi, did you see? The baby's eyes... were exactly like yours. Big and sparkling."

"What are you saying. The nose was totally Si-woo-ssi? High bridge, anyone could see it was Si-woo Kang Mini-me."

We got excited chewing on the future we just saw.
A baby not even born yet, no, not even planned, but it was lovely as if already by our side.

"If such a future comes... I really want to become a good dad. A dad who doesn't leave early like my parents, but stays by the side for a long long time."

Si-woo's voice trembled minutely.
His long-time scar and lack. Family.
That empty spot will soon be filled with love.

"Of course. Si-woo-ssi will be the best dad. I know seeing how you treat Myoreusin. And... I will try hard too. I will become the best family to Si-woo-ssi and our baby."

"Haru-ssi..."

Si-woo held my hand. The warmth from his palm was hot.

"When the future we will make together is that pretty, what importance does a piece of paper design have. Gold foil or silver foil, if Haru-ssi chose it, it's all good. If Haru-ssi just smiles, that is the answer."

My heart felt lumpy at his words. My nose stung.
Right. The important thing is that we are together.
Whatever invitation design we send, whatever dress I wear, eventually at the end there will be 'every day' we spend together.
And by that every day, that reliable and mysterious guardian Myoreusin will be there.

"Thank you, Si-woo-ssi. And... I will yield too. Gold foil is actually... looks fancy and nice. Elders would like it too."

"No. Simple is best. I trust Haru-ssi's eye."

"I said no. Let's do gold foil!"

"No, Simple!"

We faced each other and burst into laughter. A happy squabble completely different from before.
The fatigue of war-like wedding prep washed away.

*Ding-*

[Today's Fortune: Parenting is about item power.]
[Advice: Earn lots of money. Formula is expensive.]

"Puhahaha!"

It was Myoreusin-like until the end.
Right, gotta earn lots of money.
To buy our Myoreusin's tuna, and future baby's diapers.
Our future is already set.
It is 'Clear'.

- End of Episode 29 -
