I honestly don't remember how I ran to the convenience store.
I was gasping for air, lungs burning, but I couldn't stop.
Only one thought occupied my mind. 'Absolutely no laundry today.'

"Welcome."

Ignoring the part-timer's greeting, I dashed straight for the pet section.

"Churu... Churu..."

Thankfully, they had Tuna Flavor Churu in stock. A pack of four was 3,000 won.
It was pricey, but I had no choice. My hands trembled as I grabbed it.
Just then, my phone buzzed.

[Warning: Time remaining - 3 minutes.]

"A-Alright! I'm buying it! I'm buying it!"

I shouted without realizing it. Embarrassment is fleeting, but laundry is eternal.
The person at the counter was taking forever. Triangle kimbap, cup noodles, a hot bar... and even earning points.
As I stood there burning with impatience, I spotted a familiar pair of shoes.
Polished brown Chelsea boots. Was it that man who asked if I was okay when I fell earlier?

The man finished paying and turned around, locking eyes with me.
Up close, he was even more handsome. Eyes that were cool yet somehow gentle.

"Ah."

He seemed to recognize me. Well, it's hard to forget a woman covered in mud screaming "Churu!" in the rain.

"Go ahead."

He stepped aside. Even his voice was voice-actor quality.

"T-Thank you!"

I slammed the Churu onto the counter and thrust out my card.
The moment the payment confirmation popped up, I bolted out the door. I only just realized I looked like a homeless person.

* * *

"Huff... Huff..."

As soon as I opened the front door, I sprinted into my room. 1 minute remaining.

"Here! Take it! Churu!"

The creature loafing on my bed opened its eyes.
Its amber irises stared at me intently before sniffing the Churu and licking it elegantly.
*Lick, lick.*

"...."

It must be delicious. It even closed its eyes to savor the flavor.
Tension left my body, and my legs gave out. I slumped onto the floor.

[Mission Success: Churu Tribute Verified.]
[Reward: Safety of the Duvet.]

"...Thanks. Really."

The cat finished the treat in seconds and smirked at me while licking its chops. A look that asked, 'One more?'

"No. Only one a day."

But I crumbled under that wide-eyed, teary attack.
I eventually tore open another one. You're a total pushover, Haru Seo.

I washed up while the cat ate. It seemed full now and was grooming itself.
I cautiously reached out to touch its chubby belly. Squishy. It felt like heaven.

"Kraa!"
"Ouch!"

The brat bit my hand.

"That's mean. I even gave you food, you could let me touch you once."

[Warning: Do not forget your duty as a Butler.]
[Advice: Use more bribes if you want to get close.]

An app notification popped up. More bribes? Seriously?

"You... what's your name? If you don't have one, should I give you one?"

The cat's ears perked up.

"Chonky? Piggy? Nabi?"
*(TN: 'Nabi' is a common generic name for cats in Korea, like 'Kitty'.)*

The cat thumped its tail and turned its head away. Guess it hates tacky names.
Then, it tapped my phone. The words 'Today's Fortune' flashed on the screen.

"Hmm... since you tell fortunes... Fortune Cat?"

The cat lifted one front paw with a solemn expression. Like a wise ascetic.

"...Myoreusin?"
*(TN: A portmanteau of 'Myo' (Cat) and 'Eoreusin' (Elder/Sir).)*

Combining 'Myo' for cat and 'Eoreusin'. It fit perfectly.

"Myoreusin, how about that?"

"Meow."

Wait, was that a nod? It sounded satisfied.

[New Name Registered: Myoreusin.]
[Fortune Accuracy Rate has slightly increased.]

So, the name is Myoreusin.

Next morning.
I woke up feeling a heavy boulder on my chest. It was Myoreusin. I almost died of suffocation.
While getting ready for work, I looked for the remaining Churu but couldn't find them. There were definitely two left yesterday.
Two empty wrappers were rolling around in the corner of the room. He ate them all during the night.

"Hey! What are we going to do if you eat them all?!"

"Mya~"

He shamelessly tapped his empty food bowl. Like a king demanding breakfast.
I ended up having to stop by the convenience store on my way to work. This time, I'd better buy actual cat food.

The convenience store pet food corner only had expensive organic kibble. 20,000 won... that's my lunch money for a week.
As I sighed and reached for it, someone approached me from the side.

"Do you raise a cat?"

It was the handsome man from yesterday. Today, he was in a crisp shirt.

"Ah, yes. Since yesterday."

"So you're a novice butler."

My heart reacted when he flashed a dimpled smile.

"That food, the palatability isn't great. You'd be better off with this one. It's cheaper and the ingredients are decent."

He recommended a canned food from the next shelf. It was half the price.

"Wow, thank you! You must know a lot about cats?"

"Yes, well. It's my job."

Just then, my phone notification went off. Probably a demand from Myoreusin.
As the screen lit up, my wallpaper—a photo of Myoreusin—became visible.
The man's gaze lingered on my phone screen. His eyes suddenly widened.

"Huh?"
"Yes?"

The man pointed at my phone.

"That cat..."

Does he know this cat? Is he the previous owner?

"By any chance... do you know this cat?"

The man made a strange expression. A mix of surprise, delight, and suspicion.

"No, just... it looks like a cat I've seen a lot."

Liar. He definitely recognized him.
But he quickly said, "Have a nice day," and hurriedly left the store. As if he was running away.

My phone buzzed again as I walked out after paying.

[Today's Fortune: If you go North, you will meet a Noble Person (Gui-in).]
[Update: That guy is not the Noble Person. Be on your guard.]

A warning from Myoreusin. 'That guy'? The handsome man just now?
My head was already hurting this morning. Let's just go to work first. If I'm late, the Department Head will crush me.

- End of Episode 2 -
