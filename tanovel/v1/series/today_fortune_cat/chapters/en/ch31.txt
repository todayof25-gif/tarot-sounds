"Congratulations. You are 5 weeks pregnant."

The air in the OB/GYN office seemed to stop for an instant.
The doctor's calm voice hovered in my ears, but it took time for the meaning to reach my brain.

I looked at the man sitting next to me with a blank expression.
My husband, and veteran veterinarian Si-woo Cha (Cha Si-woo), who always maintained composure.
But now, he was frozen like a broken robot.

"...Doctor, what did you just... say?"

Si-woo's voice trembled minutely. The doctor smiled and pointed at the monitor.

"You see the gestational sac here? It's very small but definitely settled in. About 0.5cm?"

Inside the black and white monitor screen, a small and faint dot was visible amidst white noise.
Merely 0.5cm. But that small dot twinkled like a star floating alone in the universe. Its presence was as massive as the cosmos.
A child resembling us is in there?

Suddenly my nose stung and my vision blurred.
I brought my trembling hand to my lower belly.
It was a flat stomach with no sensation yet, but a miracle was happening inside.

"Ah..."

Si-woo let out a short gasp.
His large hands lost their way on his lap, clenching and unclenching repeatedly.
He had treated countless lives, but in front of his own child, he was just a clumsy novice dad.

"Really... Am I becoming a dad?"
"Yes, you are becoming a dad."

Hearing the confirmation, Si-woo's eyes reddened, and thick teardrops fell *thud*. The great Si-woo Cha crying in the doctor's office. That sight was pitiful yet lovely, so I laughed with wet eyes.

* * *

Even after leaving the office, Si-woo was silent for a while.
He didn't let go of my hand tightly until we got in the car. His tension was transmitted from his sweat-soaked hand.
Sitting in the driver's seat, he buried his face in the steering wheel without starting the engine.

"Hah..."
"Si-woo-ssi, are you okay?"
"...Haru-ssi. I'm not dreaming right now, am I?"

He lifted his head. Red bloodshot eyes and flushed cheeks. He was making the most overwhelmed expression in the world.

"It's just... I'm so happy it doesn't feel real. My heart has been beating like it's broken since earleir..."

He pulled my hand and placed it on his left chest. *Thump, thump, thump.* It was a fast and strong beat like someone who just finished a sprint. That heartbeat proved his joy more certainly than a hundred words.

"Can you hear it? I feel like my heart is going to explode right now."
"Yes, it's beating incredibly loud."
"Thank you. Really thank you. For coming to me, and for coming to us."

He planted a hot kiss on the back of my hand. His sincerity wrapped around me warmer than the sunlight spreading inside the car. We shared overwhelmed emotions for a long time with our foreheads touching in the narrow car.

* * *

As soon as we returned home, a familiar welcome awaited us in front of the entrance.

"Nyaa-"

Our other family member, Myoreusin. The guy who usually looked down arrogantly from the top of the cat tower came out to the front door today and raised his tail affectionately.

"Myoreusin, we're home."

As I tried to take off my shoes, Myoreusin rubbed his body against my legs and started singing a 'purr' song.
It wasn't the usual cheerful sound, but a deep, low-bass purr like a cello performance.

"Huh? Myoreusin is very well-behaved today?"

Si-woo reached out in fascination, but Myoreusin dodged coldly and thrust his head towards my stomach.
And started sniffing seriously. *Sniff sniff.*

"What's wrong? Do I smell like disinfectant?"
"They say cats know hormonal changes amazingly well. Maybe Myoreusin knows too? That the family is increasing."

At Si-woo's words, I looked down at Myoreusin.
Myoreusin stopped sniffing and looked up straight into my eyes.
Those deep and mysterious golden eyes seemed to say, 'Don't worry-nyang, I know everything-nyang'.

[Ding!]

Just then, the smartphone in my pocket rang. It was the 'Today's Fortune Cat' app notification.

[Today's Fortune: One more distinct lump has increased.]
[Advice: Earn lots of money. Formula is expensive, stupid butler.]

For a moment, a smirk escaped.
It felt like this guy saw through my heart that was half worried and half excited looking at the ultrasound photo earlier.
A lump. Not a wrong word.

"Si-woo-ssi... Look at this."

When I showed the screen with trembling hands, Si-woo's eyes widen.

Si-woo muttered lowly.

"What should we name the fetus? Any good ideas?"

"Well... Let's think about it slowly. For now... shall we just call it 'Kkomul-i' (Wriggler)?"

"Kkomul-i... Haha, cute. Let's call it that for now."

As if understanding our conversation, Myoreusin cried "Nyaang-" shortly and boldly.
It seemed he didn't like the name Kkomul-i.
That night, Myoreusin settled on my waist instead of near my feet where he usually slept.
At a perfect distance of exactly 3cm from my stomach, where body heat is transferred but weight is not, he sang a low purr song all night.
Like a lullaby for the baby in the belly.

In the dark room, Si-woo's comfortable breathing sound and Myoreusin's warm heat. And the small life inside me.
Our happy second act was beginning quietly like that.

- End of Side Story 01 -
