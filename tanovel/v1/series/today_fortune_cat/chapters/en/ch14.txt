"Hold on tight!"

Director Si-woo's white SUV glided across the rain-slicked road.
Where did the usual 'Safe Driver, Law-Abiding Citizen' Si-woo go? Sitting next to me now was a racer ready to be cast in the 'Fast & Furious' franchise.
He spun the steering wheel with one hand, weaving through narrow alleys, his cornering nothing short of artistic. The sound of tires sliding on wet pavement almost sounded like BGM.

"Kyaaa! Ssaem! Save me! No, slow down! W-Wait, faster!"

I clutched the passenger grab handle like a lifeline and screamed.
I wanted to close my eyes because I was terrified, but he was so cool I couldn't look away.
Why does his side profile look like a photoshoot even in this emergency?
Sharp eyes frowning in concentration on the road, veins popping on his forearms, rough handling of the wheel...
Ah, look at me.
My cat is kidnapped and I don't even know if he's alive, yet I'm swooning over the guy next to me. I'm trash.

"The scent... is getting stronger. Close. Directy ahead."

"Scent? In this rain? Are you really a bloodhound? No, that's a compliment! A superpower!"

"That car. The black car waiting at the signal ahead!"

I looked where he pointed and saw the black Starex from the CCTV.
The suspicious vehicle with its license plate covered in mud.
The brazen jerk was stopped at a red light at the intersection.

"Gotcha."

Si-woo was about to step on the gas, but cruelly, the light turned green.
The Starex shot off like an arrow.
Our car tried to follow, but an oblivious orange taxi cut in.

"Ah, Mister! We're in a hurry! Move!"

I rolled down the window and screamed into the rain, but there was no way he could hear me over the downpour.
The taxi was casually driving at a snail's pace. I felt like I would die of frustration.
But Si-woo was calm. He was so calm it was scary.
He yanked the wheel into a side road on the left.

"Taking a shortcut."

"What? This is... the marketplace?"

We entered an extremely narrow traditional market alley.
I thought it would be closed at this hour, but maybe a night market was open because it was bustling.
Grandmas with fruit stalls, delivery motorcycles, high schoolers sheltering from the rain, and even stray cats.
It was an obstacle course.
A route absolutely impassable for a normal person.

But Si-woo dodged them miraculously.
Without touching a single box, brushing past telephone poles by a hair's breadth—it was a parade of stunts.

"Sorry! Urgent patient! Coming through!"

He bowed his head repeatedly out the window but didn't reduce speed.
The world's kindest biker gang member.
Amidst the chaos, profanities flew from a tempura shop owner splashed by puddle water, but we were already gone.

"Haru-ssi, over there."

As soon as we exited the alley, we saw the tail of the black Starex across the main road.
It was heading towards the Han River Bridge.
If we lose it here, it's over. If he gets on the expressway, we can't catch him.

"We can't lose him! Step on it, Ssaem!"

"I won't lose him. He's in the palm of my hand. And..."

He shifted gears and smirked with one corner of his mouth.
That smile was so lethal my heart dropped.

"My car has a turbo."

*Vrooom-!!*
The engine roared like a beast. The car sprang forward like a compressed spring.
G-force buried my body into the seat.
We cut through the rain and fiercely chased the Starex.
The distance closed. 100 meters, 50 meters, 10 meters!

"Stop right there! You thief! Give me back my cat!"

I rolled down the window fully and shouted.
Rainwater flooded my mouth, but I couldn't tell if it was salty or just rain.
The Starex, hearing my voice, drove in a zigzag to threaten us.
But it couldn't match Si-woo's driving skills. He seemed to read the opponent's moves in advance.

*Skrrt-! Bang!*

Eventually, unable to withstand Si-woo's pressure, the Starex swerved and was cornered into a dead-end alley in front of an abandoned building in a redevelopment zone.
There was nowhere left to run. Retreat blocked.

The Starex screeched to a halt, and our car blocked perfectly behind it.
Si-woo unbuckled his seatbelt as soon as he stopped the car.
The smile had completely vanished from his face.

"Haru-ssi, stay in the car. It's dangerous, so lock the doors."

"No! I'm saving Myoreusin! He's my family!"

"Hah... stubborn."

He sighed as if resigning himself and got out first.
In his hand, he was holding a large black umbrella that had been in the back seat.
Holding the folded umbrella like a sword, he looked almost like a Kingsman agent.

I scrambled out too.
Heavy rain poured down, obscuring vision.
The driver's door of the Starex opened, and the man stepped out.
Black raincoat, hat pulled low, and face covered with a mask.
He was tall. He looked bulky too.

"......Persistent. Really."

The man muttered in a low voice.
He looked at us, or precisely at Si-woo, and smirked.

"Long time no see. Si-woo Kang. You still drive rough?"

"......!!"

I looked at Si-woo in surprise.
Si-woo's expression was frozen cold.
They know each other? They really knew each other?
Then Myoreusin's warning about 'Snake Eyes'... is it related to this man?

"Where is my cat."

Si-woo growled low.
It wasn't his usual soft and gentle voice. It sounded like the growl of a real beast facing prey. I felt murderous intent.
The man leisurely opened the back door.
And took out a carrier. I saw Myoreusin trapped inside the wire cage.
Fortunately, the drug must have worn off because he was awake.

"Nyaaaang!!"
[Butler!! Save me from this crazy bastard!!]

Myoreusin scratched the bars and wailed desperately.
He's safe. Thank god. He's alive.

"Hand him over. While I'm asking nicely."

Si-woo adjusted his grip on the umbrella and took a step forward.
Rainwater dripped from the tip of the umbrella.
The man lifted the carrier lightly with one hand.
Using Myoreusin as a shield, like a villain in a hostage situation.

"Take him. If you can."

The man's provocation.
Taut tension.
A silence where even the sound of rain seemed to stop.
I swallowed hard.
Wait... wasn't this a healing rom-com genre? Why did it suddenly turn into a noir action movie?
Si-woo Ssaem, he'll be okay, right? He's a doctor, not a fighter?

But that was my mistake.
The tip of the umbrella Si-woo held was trembling minutely. It wasn't fear.
Rage.
Uncontrollable rage was dominating him.
And his eyes, shining clearly even in the rain...
Were too cold and sharp to belong to a human.
