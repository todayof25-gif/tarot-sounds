[Today's Fortune: Human... don't cry.]
[Advice: I'm not dead yet.]

I went blank seeing the message on the phone screen.
Myoreusin is definitely unconscious. Si-woo is doing CPR right in front of my eyes, and the ECG graph on the monitor is shaking precariously.
But a message.
Is this... sent by Myoreusin's soul? Or the power of this strange app?

"Si-woo-ssi! Myoreusin... Myoreusin says he's alive!"

When I shouted, Si-woo lifted his head. Face covered in sweat, bloodshot eyes.

"What?"

"A message came! Says he's not dead yet! Says don't give up!"

It was unscientific words, but at that moment, we needed even a straw to clutch at.
Si-woo's eyes burned again.

"Right... He wouldn't go that easily."

He started heart massage again.
One, two, three, four.
Desperation was loaded in his fingertips.
I also held Myoreusin's cold paws and rubbed them endlessly.

"Come back. I'll give you lots of delicious things. I won't scold you even if you break Gundams. Please..."

How much time passed?
*Beep- beep- beep-*
The mechanical sound started to change rhythmically.
Weakly, but Myoreusin's chest began to rise and fall on its own.

"He's back. Vitals returned!"

At the nurse's shout, Si-woo finally collapsed to the floor.
My legs also gave out and I fell.
He lived. Our Myoreusin lived.

* * *

We passed the critical moment, but couldn't rest easy.
Tonight was crucial until the toxins were flushed out.
Myoreusin was moved to the VIP room again, and we guarded his side.

2 AM.
Only the sound of IV drops was heard in the silent hospital room.
Si-woo was sitting on the sofa checking Myoreusin's IV drip rate.

"Haru-ssi, get some shut-eye. I'll watch him."

"No. I can't sleep."

I placed a chair next to Myoreusin's bed and sat.
I felt I could only be at ease hearing his wheezing breath.
Si-woo came and sat next to me.
He handed me a cup of hot cocoa.

"Drink. To relax."

"Thank you..."

Warmth traveled through my fingertips.
Looking at Si-woo's profile, he looked extremely exhausted.

"Si-woo-ssi... you were very scared too, right?"

"Honestly... I was terrified."

He gave a bitter smile.

"I've seen countless deaths after becoming a vet, but letting family go... I never get used to it."

"Family..."

"Yes. Myoreusin is my savior and family. When I was left alone with my brother after my parents passed away, there was a time I wanted to give up everything because it was so hard. Then I coincidentally met a cat on the street, and it walked up to me and cried 'Meow'. It sounded like it was saying 'Live'. I decided then. I'll become a vet and protect these kids."

His eyes became moist.
The man who only looked strong had scars like this.
Knowing his pain, I felt closer to him.

"And..."

He looked at me deeply. I was reflected in his deep eyes.

"Because Myoreusin is Haru-ssi's family too. I hated seeing you sad more than anything. If you hurt, I hurt too. So please... don't crumble."

My nose stung and then ached at his words.
This man thought of me first even in an extreme situation.
He must be scared and having a hard time too, but he was holding on for me.
Just as Myoreusin is precious to me, I have become a precious existence to him.

"Thank you, Si-woo-ssi. Really. Without you... I probably couldn't have endured it. If I were alone, really..."

My voice trembled at the end.
I mustered courage and held his hand.
His large hand wrapped around mine warmly and very tightly.
Rough but gentle doctor's hand. Hand that saves lives.
We felt each other's body heat without speaking.
Darkness was thick outside the window and rain started hitting the glass again, but inside this small hospital room, it was warm as if a candle was lit.
It wasn't simple excitement. This was comradeship, trust, and deep love.
Being there when it hurts the most, when it's most scary.
Staying up all night together looking at the same place.
That was enough. We were already miracles to each other.

* * *

*Cock-a-doodle-doo-* wait no, *chirp chirp*.
Morning sunlight pierced through the window.
I must have fallen asleep leaning on Si-woo's shoulder.
Opening my eyes, Si-woo was also dozing off leaning on my head.
It was an uncomfortable posture, but he looked the most comfortable in the world.

"Mmm..."

Then a rustling sound was heard from the bed.
Don't tell me?
I jumped up and looked at the bed.
Myoreusin was opening his eyes.
Those bright amber eyes blinked at me.

"Myoreusin?!"

"He's up? Is he okay?"

Si-woo also woke up and approached hurriedly.
Myoreusin looked at both of us alternately, then opened his mouth wide and yawned.
And with a very annoyed expression, he tapped the phone with his front paw. *Tap.*

*Ding-*
[Today's Fortune: Noisy.]
[Advice: Don't chatter while the patient is sleeping. And give me food.]

"Puhahaha!"

Si-woo and I burst into laughter at the same time.
We laughed until tears pricked our eyes.
That arrogance. That chicness.
Our Myoreusin is back. Perfectly.

"Okay, I'll give you food! I'll give you premium tuna!"

I hugged Myoreusin tight.
The guy had an expression saying "Ah, let go human. It's hot", but he didn't avoid it.
Whatever, the window was bright.
It was a miraculous morning.
