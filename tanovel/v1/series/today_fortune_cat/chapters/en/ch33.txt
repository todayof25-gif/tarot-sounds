2 AM.
Silent time when everyone is asleep.
I opened my eyes wide.

Cold sweat ran down.
I desperately want to eat something.
That thing popping up in my head was very specific and vivid.

Fluffy pink fuzz.
Sweet juice bursting when taking a bite.
And soft texture.

"......Peach."

Going crazy. It's January now. Peaches in midwinter.
There's no way to get them.

"Mmm... Haru-ssi? Why are you up?"

At my tossing and turning, Si-woo who was sleeping next to me woke up groggily.
Only his eyes looking at me were full of worry.

"Are you sick? Does your stomach pull?"

"No, not that..."

Sorry to say.
12th week of pregnancy. Morning sickness subsided, but a new boss called 'Eating Sickness' (Cravings) came.
But asking to find peaches in midwinter, isn't that something an abused daughter-in-law in a folktale does.

"What is it. Tell me. What do you want to eat?"

Si-woo held my hand tight and urged.
I spoke in a crawling voice.

"That is... Peaches..."

"Peaches?"

"Sorry. I know it makes no sense. I'll just drink water and sl..."

"Wait."

Si-woo jumped up.
He started putting on clothes with a solemn expression.

"W-Wait a minute! It's 2 AM right now! Where are you going to buy it?"

"There is no impossible in today's world. There's a premium fruit shop open 24 hours in Gangnam. If not, I'll wake up a greenhouse farm owner and buy it."

"No, Si-woo-ssi! Don't do that!"

"Byeol-i wants to eat it, I must serve it unconditionally. I'll be back soon."

He left a kiss on my forehead and disappeared like the wind.
The front door closed, and silence flowed in the house again.

I sat blankly on the bed.
Why does this damn appetite want peaches at this hour, in this season, making people suffer.

"Nyaa-"

Just then, Myoreusin walked in from the living room strolling.
The guy jumped onto the bed lightly and took a spot on my lap.

"Myoreusin... Dad went out. To suffer because of me."

"Nya."
[Pathetic.]

The guy tapped my arm with his tail. *Tap tap.*
Then he poked the tablet PC Si-woo left behind with his front paw.
Screen turned on.

"Uh? That's Dad's. Shouldn't touch."

I tried to put away the tablet.
But Myoreusin's cotton bat stopped my hand.
The guy started touching the screen with a serious expression.

*Tap. Tap. Tatap.*

'Delivery Nation of Nyang' app turned on.
Don't tell me... again?

"Hey, what are you doing! No! Late night snack forbidden!"

"Woong!"
[Noisy!]

Myoreusin pressed the 'Search' tab skillfully, then put his jelly paws on the virtual keyboard.
And typed on the keyboard with amazing speed.

T... u... n... a...

Tuna?
Is this guy searching for tuna right now?

"No! Last time too living expenses had a hole because you ordered!"

I tried to take the tablet.
But Myoreusin turned his body nimbly blocking me, and slammed the 'Order' button. *Bang.*
And it was 'Top Class Bluefin Tuna Otoro Set' (120,000 won).

[Order is complete.]

"Aaaack!! 120,000 won!!"

I clutched my head.
Husband is wandering outside to find peaches in midwinter, but the cat orders a 120,000 won late-night snack?
Is this a household!

"You're really in for it! I'll tell Si-woo-ssi everything when he comes!"

"Nyaong."
[Byeol-i said she wanted to eat.]

The guy pointed at my stomach shamelessly.

"Liar! Byeol-i doesn't even know tuna taste yet!"

"Nya."
[Blood doesn't lie. Isn't your husband a tuna ghost too.]

Dumbfounded.
If only he couldn't talk I wouldn't hate him.
That shamelessness demonstrating self-rationalization somehow.

30 minutes later.
*Ding dong-*

"Delivery here~"

It came.
I opened the front door with a feeling like a sinner.
A heavy packaging bag was handed over.

"Enjoy your meal."

The delivery driver smiled brightly and left.
Unpacking the bag on the living room table, tuna sashimi of brilliant crimson color revealed itself.
Top-class Otoro flowing with grease.

"......"

Honestly... looked delicious.
Saliva gathered in my mouth.
No, this is Myoreusin's scheme.

"Nyaaang~"
[Butler, bring chopsticks.]

Myoreusin was already seated at the head of the table licking his chops.
Just then, digital lock sound was heard.

*Beep beep beep beep- Clunk.*

"Haru-ssi! Bought it!"

Si-woo came in panting.
In his hand was a box wrapped in gold cloth.
Face red from cold, but expression confident like a triumphant general.

"Begged the department store food court staff and got what was in the warehouse. Says it's top class."

He put down the peach box on the table.
And discovered the tuna sashimi spread next to it.

"Uh? What is this? Tuna?"

"Ah... that is..."

I glared at Myoreusin.
Now, make an excuse you swindler cat.

Si-woo looked at tuna, me, and Myoreusin alternately.
And seemed to grasp the situation, letting out a dry laugh.

"Haha... Myoreusin, isn't this a bit excessive this time?"

"Nya."
[Prenatal care. The kid in the belly says she needs DHA.]

Myoreusin tapped my belly with his front paw. *Tap tap.*

"Byeol-i did? Ha, my goodness."

Si-woo laughed as if dumbfounded, but didn't get angry.
Rather he sat next to Myoreusin and picked up chopsticks.

"Right. Well, if Byeol-i wants to eat what can we do. Let's eat, eat."

"Si-woo-ssi, really okay? This is expensive..."

"It's okay. Haru-ssi eats peaches, and I eat tuna with Myoreusin. This is a party."

Si-woo peeled a peach prettily and offered it.
Sweet juice spread in my mouth.
A taste having the whole world.

"Delicious..."

"Delicious? That's a relief."

He smiled contentedly just watching me eat.
And picked up a piece of tuna and put it in Myoreusin's mouth.

"Here, Myoreusin eat a lot too. Worked hard making excuses for our Byeol-i."

"Nyam nyam."

Myoreusin accepted it without declining.
Seeing the guy making an ecstatic expression chewing fatty tuna, couldn't hate him.
Right, you are our family too. Have to eat together.

Midwinter dawn.
Peaches and Tuna Sashimi.
It was the most mismatched combination in the world, but our dining table was warmer and more bountiful than ever.

Just then, phone placed on the table rang.

*Ding-*

[Today's Fortune: Fetus Logic.]
[Advice: Giving when the kid asks is the national rule. Even if that is my snack.]

"Puhaha! Right, national rule."

Si-woo burst into laughter.
I mumbled on the peach and stroked Myoreusin's back lightly.

"Right, eat a lot. Our Guardian Deity."

Myoreusin waved his tail instead of answering.
His bulging belly looked as full as my belly.
It was a happy dawn.

- End of Side Story 03 -
